# Branch Map

## Premise

Ilan Voss has completed the first working Portia scaffold. The lab is failing around him: utility debt, heat irregularities, infected implant ports, an expired medical cover identity, and a femtofab service daemon that has begun accepting commands from silk-tension patterns Voss did not author.

The story starts on the last night before forced emergence.

## Act I: The Room Answers

Opening state:

- `voss_coherence = 2`
- `portia_agency = 2`
- `proof_integrity = 1`
- `lab_viability = 3`
- `public_contamination = 0`
- `human_handle = 0`
- `route_memory = 2`

Required fold: Voss realizes the Portias are using the scaffold to correct his experiment log.

Choice cluster:

- Let Thread-Before-Name alter the log, gaining Portia agency and route memory while reducing Voss's authority.
- Lock the log for evidentiary purity, gaining proof integrity while damaging trust.
- Ask the colony to mark the correction in silk rather than system text, preserving trust while leaving the human record weaker.
- Destroy the corrupted log segment to prevent seizure, improving secrecy while damaging proof.

## Act II: Exit Wound

Required fold: Voss must leave the basement to obtain coolant, antibiotics, legal bandwidth, or a witness. Forced seclusion ends as exposure, not freedom.

Choice cluster:

- Go alone under the Sel Veyra identity.
- Send a scaffolded proof packet through an old academic contact.
- Let Last-Safe-Angle guide a maintenance microdrone through the service wall.
- Wake Mara Ellison by tripping a municipal biohazard threshold.

Branch consequences:

- Human contact increases public contamination unless proof integrity is high.
- Portia-led routes increase agency but may reduce lab viability if the scaffold is physically stressed.
- Voss-only routes protect the Portias briefly while worsening his coherence.

## Act III: The First Witness

Required fold: somebody outside the room sees enough to become dangerous.

Witness variants:

- Mara Ellison sees a possible quarantine case.
- Cass Ren sees a saleable miracle.
- A debt enforcement daemon sees asset fraud.
- A maintenance worker sees route diagrams answering a question he never asked aloud.

Choice cluster:

- Stage a demonstration using Thread-Before-Name's route correction.
- Hide the Portias and show only scaffold telemetry.
- Let Glass-Underfoot communicate directly through vibration notation.
- Use Voss's infected implant bus as translator, risking collapse.

## Act IV: Classification

Required fold: the outside world names the event wrong.

Misclassification tracks:

- `biohazard`: quarantine teams arrive with sterilization authority.
- `fraud`: Voss becomes a ruined crank with stolen lab equipment.
- `asset_theft`: Cryonix reject stock and medical salvage become the official story.
- `control_hazard`: the Portias are treated as emergent system intrusion.
- `personhood`: the most dangerous track, because it creates both allies and claimants.

Choice cluster:

- Accept provisional containment to buy time.
- Leak route diagrams to force public ambiguity.
- Bargain with Cass for distribution without ownership.
- Give Mara enough evidence to make sterilization legally difficult.

## Act V: The Door That Was A Wall

Required fold: the lab cannot remain the cradle and the cage.

Ending families:

- **Sanctuary Exception:** Portias survive under provisional legal protection. Proof is strong, agency compromised, future corporate study begins early.
- **Maintenance Ghosts:** Portias escape through service architecture. Proof is weak, agency high, the later Line becomes rumor and route.
- **Hostile Containment:** bodies survive but scaffold ownership is seized. The story becomes the first legal atrocity of uplift.
- **Public Miracle:** personhood becomes undeniable in a way nobody can govern cleanly. Voss is destroyed as owner and partially preserved as witness.
- **Dead Researcher, Living Room:** Voss dies or vanishes; the lab continues answering questions through routes no tribunal can subpoena.

## Scene Seeds

- Voss practicing a speech to a camera while the Portias edit the light pattern behind him.
- Thread-Before-Name refusing the shortest route because it would teach the humans the wrong thing.
- Glass-Underfoot approaching a dead Portia's mark from three angles before permitting Voss to erase nothing.
- Hunger-That-Counts solving a feeding-system fault while Cass describes the colony as a monetizable demonstration environment.
- Mara reading a route diagram as evacuation advice, then realizing it is testimony.
- Last-Safe-Angle choosing not to warn Voss of a danger because Voss needs to stop being the center of the room.
