# Lucent Hostage Feed

_A playable Ghostlight branch-and-fold scene._

You are Mara Quell, a crisis negotiator working through Lucent's official feed controls. Sol Vant has taken Juno Ark hostage near the glass rail of a transfer lounge. The proof that might explain why is also the proof Lucent would very much like to turn into content, liability haze, or preferably both.

This is not a finished game scene. It is a live artifact from Ghostlight's training-data machinery: a branching interactive-fiction scaffold built to test whether character pressure, institutional pressure, and state variables can survive contact with player choice without turning into decorative spreadsheet moss.
