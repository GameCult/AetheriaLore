# Game Design

*"The setting is a machine. The playable problem is deciding which gears the player gets to touch first."*

Aetheria at full scale wants to simulate a galaxy where freelance operators, corporate logistics, military doctrine, supply chains, and market failures all feed one another. The first releases do not need to ship the whole monster at once, but they do need to prove the mood, pressure, and social logic that make the wider project worth building.
