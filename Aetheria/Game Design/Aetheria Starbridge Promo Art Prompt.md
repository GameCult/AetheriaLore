## Generated Panorama Analysis

- the base core occupies the bottom foreground;
- the nebula reads as a luminous gravitational terrain surface rather than empty
  space;
- missiles, point defense, shields, and enemy pressure create a good broad
  battle rhythm;

The main weakness is allegiance and vector clarity. Several large ships on the
right read as attackers, while the defending player ships do not form an
unambiguous protective ring. The image needs more deliberate screen-space
composition: station core in fixed bottom coordinates, four defenders locked to

vectors aimed inward.
