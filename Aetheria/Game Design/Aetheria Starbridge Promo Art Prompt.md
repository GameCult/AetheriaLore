# Aetheria: Starbridge Promo Art Prompt

Date: 2026-06-24

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: Starbridge promo splash art / website hero image

Input image: use the provided Aetheria screenshot as structural reference, not
merely mood. The nebula forms gravitational terrain: a luminous blue-white
volumetric surface like an ocean of space-clouds. Resources, debris, ships,
stations, missiles, and combat activity cluster along or just above this nebular
surface. Larger celestial bodies float above the surface. The battlefield should
feel like it takes place at the playable boundary of the nebula, not in empty
black deep space.

Primary request: Create splashy matte-painting promo art for Aetheria:
Starbridge. An Aeronautics Unlimited frontier station is under siege by pirate
forces on the surface of a blue-white nebular gravity terrain. The station is
under fire, shields are catching laser impacts, incoming missile swarms are
being shredded by point-defense cannons, and multiple pirate combat roles are
actively attacking the station and its defenders.

Scene/backdrop: a luminous blue-white nebula forms a rolling gravitational
terrain surface across the lower two-thirds of the image, with darker ridges and
bright cloud valleys. Asteroid resources and debris float at the surface. A
small icy moon or pale celestial body floats above the terrain in the upper
middle distance, echoing the reference image.

Main station: a large Aeronautics Unlimited frontier station sits slightly right
of center, hovering just above the nebular surface. It has clean industrial
modular architecture: docking arms, radiator wings, shield emitter pylons,
point-defense turret clusters, a central fabrication core, and repair/service
bays. It is visibly under siege but still functioning.

Four defending player ships, explicitly placed and differentiated:

1. DPS ship A: sleek red-and-white interceptor in the lower left foreground,
   knife-like triangular profile, bright engine flare, banking upward across the
   nebula surface while firing twin blue-white laser streams into a pirate
   fighter pack.
2. DPS ship B: heavier gunship in the upper right near the station shield, broad
   forward cannons and missile pods, launching a salvo diagonally outward into
   the incoming horde while its nose points away from the station.
3. Tank ship: bulky armored shield ship directly in front of the station on the
   left side of the shield perimeter, slab armor, large glowing frontal shield
   projector, intentionally absorbing a concentrated red-orange laser barrage.
   Its projected shield bubble overlaps the station shield and visibly deflects
   or refracts several beams.
4. Support ship: compact utility/service craft below the station near a damaged
   radiator wing, with articulated repair arms and glowing cyan coolant beam. It
   is tethered to the station module with one repair beam and a second cooling
   beam, venting white-blue heat mist away from the damaged section.

Enemy combatants, explicitly placed and acting:

1. Pirate missile boats: three wedge-shaped pirate craft on the far right edge
   of the nebula terrain, launching dense curved missile swarms toward the
   station. Their launch racks glow orange, and fresh missile trails arc across
   the image toward point-defense fire.
2. Pirate laser raiders: a tight pack of small fast ships on the far left border
   skimming the nebula surface, firing red-orange beam volleys at the tank ship
   and station shields. Several beams visibly splash, bend, or reflect off the
   overlapping cyan shields.
3. Boarding pod carriers: two ugly armored carrier craft in the lower right
   midground releasing small boarding pods or breach drones toward the station's
   docking arms. The pods form a dotted line of hot engine sparks approaching
   the shield boundary.
4. Torpedo bomber: one larger pirate bomber above the nebular surface near the
   upper left, lining up a heavy torpedo run on the station fabrication core
   while DPS ship B's missile salvo streaks toward it.
5. Jammer corvette: a spindly pirate support vessel at the far upper right,
   projecting faint red interference rings toward the station sensor mast, while
   station point-defense tracers try to punch through the interference.
6. Salvage skiffs: two small pirate skiffs near the lower edge trying to tow
   wreckage or resource fragments away from the battlefield, giving DPS ship A a
   clear target cluster.

Combat action: laser volleys splash across semi-transparent cyan station
shields, with some beams partially absorbed and reflected as bright refracted
streaks. Dense swarms of incoming missiles arc toward the station from the
pirate missile boats. Station point-defense cannons fire rapid white tracer
streams, detonating missiles into small bright explosions before impact.
Boarding pods attempt to cross the shield boundary near docking arms. The jammer
corvette creates visible interference around the sensor mast. Debris and
resources drift along the nebula surface.

Style/medium: high-end cinematic sci-fi matte painting, key art, painterly
realism, dramatic scale, dense but readable battlefield composition, no UI, no
text.

Composition/framing: wide landscape hero image. Nebula terrain sweeps from
bottom left to upper right like a playable surface. Station is the focal point
slightly right of center. DPS A in lower left foreground attacking skiffs and
raiders, tank between station and left-side laser fire, support below station at
damaged radiator, DPS B upper right pushing outward against bomber and missile
boats. Pirate missile boats occupy far right, laser raiders far left, boarding
pod carriers lower right, torpedo bomber upper left, jammer corvette upper
right, salvage skiffs lower edge. Missile trails, shield arcs, boarding pod
paths, and point-defense tracers lead the eye back to the station. Leave
atmospheric negative space near the upper left for possible website copy, but
include no text.

Lighting/mood: intense blue-white nebula backlight, cold cyan shields and
coolant beams, hot red-orange pirate impacts, white point-defense flashes,
desperate but heroic co-op defense energy.

Color palette: cold luminous blues, cyan shields, white nebula haze, steel-gray
AU station, red-orange impact accents and pirate engine glows, small red/white
accents on DPS A.

Materials/textures: industrial station plating, glassy shield refraction,
heat-scarred turret barrels, missile plasma trails, repair/cooling beams,
asteroid debris, volumetric nebula clouds, ragged pirate armor plating, scorched
missile racks, flickering jammer rings.

Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no readable cockpit UI, no single
huge human figure, no Star Wars/Star Trek styling, no fantasy magic. The four
player ships must be visibly distinct by silhouette, location, and action
exactly as described. Enemy ships must not be a generic horde: show their
specific attacks and battlefield jobs.
