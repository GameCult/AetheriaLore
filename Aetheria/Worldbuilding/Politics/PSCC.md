The Pan Solar Consortium Credit (PSCC) is the common currency of the off-world colonies, standardized by the [[Pan-Solar Consortium]]. Colonies depend so heavily on one another that they cannot function without a shared currency. That dependence makes the PSCC universally used and gives the Consortium economic leverage.

By [[Identity Abyss]], corporate scrip—money issued by companies—had replaced traditional currencies in the colonies. The PSC standardized that scrip into the PSCC, backed by the corporations’ collective assets, including [[SolEx]]’s minerals and [[NiteLife Energy]]’s energy. Its universal adoption followed from the colonies’ need to trade with one another, rather than policy alone.

The PSC uses [[Restrictions on Warfare|sanctions]] to enforce the currency’s stability, punishing noncompliance with isolation. For colonies that rely on access to the wider economy, exclusion is a powerful threat. This dependence keeps the PSCC dominant even as the Consortium itself remains a hollow institution.
