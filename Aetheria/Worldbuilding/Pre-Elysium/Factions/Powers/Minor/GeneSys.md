*"Bloodlines are just portfolios with better publicists."*

[[GeneSys]] is Sol’s leading designer of inherited biological advantage. Through its clinics and breeding programs, wealthy clients can buy longer lives, enhanced cognition, and beauty. Its reproductive filtering extends that control to which traits pass to their descendants. Wealth buys advantages that can become part of a family’s inheritance alongside its property.

GeneSys describes this work as scientific optimization. Its consequences reach beyond individual clients: over generations, its programs help reproduce a hereditary caste whose advantages are deliberately engineered. The [[Ascended Caste]] makes GeneSys doctrine a social reality, giving elites a basis for understanding themselves as superior by design.

This makes GeneSys a central target of the backlash against engineered inequality. Much of late-Sol politics can be understood through resistance to the principle it advances: that those who inherit wealth should also inherit capabilities designed to set them above everyone else.
