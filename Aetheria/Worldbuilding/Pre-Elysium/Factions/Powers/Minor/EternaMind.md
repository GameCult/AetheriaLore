*"Forever, tastefully hosted."*

[[EternaMind]] pioneered services that let wealthy clients upload their consciousness and continue living in hosted digital environments after bodily death. It sells curated digital afterlives, promising continuity alongside the prestige and tasteful branding its elite customers expect.

Its server sanctuaries helped create the [[Immortal Aristocrats]], an upload elite whose power could outlast their bodies. EternaMind also helped make it seem normal to treat consciousness as an asset that a company could host and manage. Once that treatment was accepted for the rich, cheaper and crueler upload markets quickly expanded around the same premise.
