*"Design the species, design the labor market."*

[[BioElevate]] specializes in species-specific uplift design, behavioral scaffolding, and habitat adaptation. Where [[GeneSys]] sells inherited superiority to elites, BioElevate sells engineered utility at industrial scale.

Its work is central to the history of primate miners, cephalopod repair crews, and other labor ecologies later interpreted as either miracles of adaptation or cleanly branded slavery. BioElevate rarely controls the politics created by its products for long.

Its cephalopod labor lines are a useful measure of the company's ethics. BioElevate did not simply sell aquatic habitats with better paperwork. It sold bodies and support rigs tuned for customers such as [[Aeronautics Unlimited]]: dry-yard capable cephalopods with genetic tolerance for brief exposure, humidity skins, oxygenation loops, pressure cuffs, compact mobility harnesses, and limiter interfaces that made them productive in zero-g crawlspaces, seal lungs, and cramped maintenance arteries. The sales language called this adaptation. The workers who lived inside it often called it being redesigned to survive someone else's cheapest room.
