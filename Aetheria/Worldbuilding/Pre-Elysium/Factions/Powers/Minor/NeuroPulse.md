*"Pleasure, properly dosed, is cheaper than dissent."*

[[NeuroPulse]] sells neural systems that alter feelings and reward responses to make people compliant. Buyers may seek bliss, relief, an emotional reset, or obedience. The products blur those purposes: the comfort someone experiences can also be the means by which another party controls them.

The company grew out of the wellness-interface business as a spinout from [[NeuroSyn]]. It turned emotional regulation into tools for tactical pacification and deliberately engineered addiction, later developing [[Wireheading]] weapons that directly capture a mind's reward mechanisms. Its ability to make domination feel temporarily comforting made it indispensable to the most control-oriented factions.

Its most durable commercial systems keep customers active enough to remain useful and subscribed. They preserve self-care, identity authentication, attention to sponsored content, responses that reveal preferences, narrowly defined work, and continued displays of consent. They do not need to leave every customer inert to control them.

NeuroPulse can therefore show buyers clean facilities and responsive clients as evidence that its systems work. Neither establishes that clients act independently: the interface conditions the very responses presented as engagement and agreement, making persuasion and trained obedience increasingly difficult to distinguish.
