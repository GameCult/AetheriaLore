*"Count the tons, count the shifts, count the dead later."*

[[SolEx]] is the bureaucracy that organizes much of late Sol’s mining and resource extraction. It was formed by merging [[Horizon Ventures]]’ transport infrastructure with [[TerraCore Industries]]’ mining operations and life-support administration, bringing the movement of supplies, the extraction of minerals, and the systems that keep workers alive under one organization.

SolEx governs by measuring how much work and material moves through those systems, auditing their operation, and carrying obligations forward through inherited contracts. It treats workers as a supply of labor to be allocated alongside worlds, habitats, and transport corridors. These are connected inventories: SolEx seeks to make them more productive and defend them as parts of the same extraction system.

Long before [[Sol Dominion]] made hierarchy an explicit political doctrine, SolEx had helped much of the solar economy accept this way of organizing life. Its foundational influence lay in making people and settlements answer to the demands of extraction through routine accounting and administration.
