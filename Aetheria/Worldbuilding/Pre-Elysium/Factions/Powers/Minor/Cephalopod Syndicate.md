*Always one tentacle ahead*

The [[Cephalopod Syndicate]] is a loose network of uplifted cephalopods, allied technicians, smugglers, and sabotage specialists. Its members use their expertise in zero-gravity maintenance to extract people through mutual-aid operations, take pirate contracts, and disrupt firms that still treat uplifted workers as configurable equipment. Knowing how to keep a habitat running also gives them political leverage: they know its service passages better than most formal security forces ever will.

Many founders came from AU and BioElevate work systems, where corporations treated the needs of cephalopod bodies as opportunities to make money rather than requirements for a decent life. In corporate cavity yards, workers were fitted with support rigs that let them operate outside water. These combined pliable humidity skins, oxygenation loops, pressure cuffs, neural limiter interfaces, and compact mobile harnesses. Workers could move through human workspaces and squeeze through zero-g service passages and seal cavities.

The rigs deliberately provided the bare minimum. A habitat designed for cephalopods might accommodate water, comfortable pressure, and movement together. AU yards instead prioritized how far a worker could reach, how little they cost, how reliably they complied, and how small, mobile, and self-managing they could remain while surviving long enough to finish the job.

The Syndicate grew out of that indispensable labor. Workers fitted to move through service passages and seal cavities learned to use their access and knowledge to exert power over the systems they maintained.
