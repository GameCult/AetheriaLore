*Always one tentacle ahead*

The [[Cephalopod Syndicate]] is a loose network of uplifted cephalopods, allied technicians, smugglers, and sabotage specialists who turned zero-g maintenance expertise into political leverage. They know the service arteries of habitats better than most formal security forces ever will.

Many of the Syndicate's founders came out of AU and BioElevate work systems that treated cephalopod physiology as a problem to monetize rather than a life to accommodate. In corporate cavity yards they were fitted with dry-operation support rigs: pliable humidity skins, oxygenation loops, pressure cuffs, neural limiter interfaces, and compact mobile harnesses that let them move through human workspaces and squeeze through zero-g service arteries and seal cavities. These rigs were deliberately bare-minimum. A cephalopod-native habitat might build around water, pressure comfort, and communal movement; an AU yard built around reach, cost, compliance, and how small, mobile, and self-managing a maintenance worker could be while surviving long enough to finish the job.

Their operations range from mutual-aid extraction and pirate contracting to industrial disruption against firms that still treat uplifted workers as configurable equipment. The Syndicate is what happens when indispensable maintenance labor learns how much power it has been quietly carrying.
