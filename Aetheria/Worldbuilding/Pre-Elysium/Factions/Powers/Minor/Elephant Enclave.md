*Wisdom through Peace*

The [[Elephant Enclave]] is a self-governing society of uplifted elephants. It began when elephants working in logistics and their human allies broke away from managed labor systems to build communities of their own. They organized those communities around preserving memory, caring for one another, and sustaining life over the long term, rather than continually optimizing work.

In late Sol, where much of political power depends on speed and spectacle, the Enclave deliberately refuses the pressure to keep accelerating. Its sanctuary work and alliance with [[Aya Collective]] networks give that refusal practical force: they help sustain alternatives to corporate ways of organizing life.

Outsiders often idealize the Enclave's commitment to peace. Its pacifism, however, includes self-defense and draws on historical experience. The Enclave treats peace as a strategy requiring judgment about how to resist and protect its communities, rather than assuming that peaceful intentions will keep them safe.
