*"If flesh can be specified, labor can be grown."*

[[VitaForge]] is an industrial bioengineering company that made organisms engineered for labor commonplace in the late-Sol system. Larger infrastructure operators widely subcontract its BioDrone Standard line for basic maintenance, service, and general labor. These workers meet a demand for adaptable living bodies in a system that refuses to grant them equal standing.

In a yard staffed by several suppliers, a VitaForge biodrone may work alongside BioElevate uplift lines, baseline crews, and corporate supervisors. Each supplier tries to limit its responsibility to what its own invoice covers. That makes a worker's origin politically important. If a BioDrone Standard worker refuses to work, the dispute first concerns a VitaForge biodrone; it cannot simply be treated as a question about the scaffolding used in BioElevate's uplift lines.

VitaForge also produces combat variants and mass-market gene lines. Together with its biodrones, these are central to every major argument about exploitation in the setting. The company mass-produces beings for work, leaving everyone who uses, supervises, or lives alongside them to confront the question its business depends on: which workers receive the standing of persons, and which remain products?
