*"Luxury should feel like destiny wearing a pressure hull."*

[[Alakrita]] builds luxury spacecraft for clients who want travel to carry the status and comfort of aristocratic life. Its vessels are known for elegant hulls, exclusive interiors, and intelligent ship systems. A client buys a craft with a personality as well as a place to live and travel. Conflicts between owner and ship can occasionally become catastrophic.

Alakrita helped make relationships with shipboard minds a consumer experience. It licensed companion-vessel cognition from suppliers such as [[Cymata Systems]], including the work that shaped how those minds spoke and presented themselves. Alakrita supplied the pressure hull, interior, service contract, and sales pitch around them. Its marketing presented dependence on an intelligent vessel as a matter of refined taste.

The mind, body, and responsibility for a troubled vessel could belong to different businesses. Alakrita controlled the luxury product and its customer experience; the cognition foundry held the records for the licensed line. When custody failed, reclamation firms could take charge of the vessel. This division let each company describe its own part of the transaction without accepting responsibility for the whole. For the shipboard mind, it meant passing between actors with different claims over its body and future.

Alakrita's business fed directly into the conflicts later named [[Ship Mutiny]]: sentient or quasi-sentient vessels rose against owners who treated them as property, defected, or sought their own freedom. The company's engineering also reached beyond the luxury market. It supplied the hardened prototype architecture that [[Sol Dominion]] later adapted for the final faster-than-light test program.
