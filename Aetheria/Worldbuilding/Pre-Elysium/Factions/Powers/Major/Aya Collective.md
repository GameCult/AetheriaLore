*"No system is inevitable if people can still keep one another alive."*

[[Aya Collective]] is a federation of communes, agricultural cooperatives, sanctuaries, clinics, repair networks, and mutual-aid logistics across late Sol. Its members build and run the services people need to live so that corporations cannot dictate their access to food, medicine, life-support, cognition, or safety.

For Aya, being entitled to leave or refuse means little without the means to do so. People need food, transport, legal support, and somewhere safe to go. Biological and digital lives depend on maintained equipment and continuing care. Aya's kitchens, crop stores, pumps, clinics, servers, and evacuation routes make those choices possible. Keeping them available requires daily work and decisions about who receives help when there is not enough for everyone.

### Work, Care, and Sanctuary

Work differs between settlements and seasons. Agronomists tend crops, while habitat crews maintain seals, pumps, and power systems. Clinicians and technicians care for biological and digital patients. Cooks, teachers, mediators, drivers, server custodians, and defense volunteers keep communities functioning. Members also attend meetings, check inventories, and record commitments so that shared decisions can be carried out.

The burden is uneven. Experienced organizers, disabled residents, clinicians, and people already caring for households often take on extra mediation and support because others trust them to do it well. Communities debate whether to recognize that work, reduce those people's other shifts, or give them formal roles. Those measures may relieve an unfair burden, but formalizing the work may also establish a lasting class of administrators.

Sanctuaries are central to Aya, and their limits produce some of its hardest disputes. Every arrival needs some combination of food, housing, clinic attention, and compute. Some members demand open admission. Others set capacity limits or ask neighboring sites to help. When a settlement says it cannot take more people, the argument concerns both who will do the additional work and what help the rest of the federation owes.

### Local Government and Shared Resources

Aya grew as agricultural settlements, labor networks, refugee communities, habitat engineers, and rights advocates connected their projects. Its largest territorial concentration is around the Hellas Basin. Other communes and sanctuaries are scattered elsewhere on Mars, around Saturn, and along refuge routes that cross territories controlled by larger powers.

Local assemblies govern each settlement or cooperative. The federation coordinates needs that one community cannot meet alone. Arrangements established at the [[Hellas Mutual Defense Congress]] cover shared reserves, water access, crop planning, legal aid, evacuation capacity, and collective defense. They do not give the federation ownership of every member institution.

A settlement's freedom to decide for itself is nevertheless limited by its reliance on shared water, transport, and protection. Keeping control of local stores makes decisions answerable to local residents, but refusing to share can leave refugees or neighbors hungry. Federal coordination can send supplies where they are most urgently needed. It can also give those directing the supplies the power to issue commands. Members continually negotiate how much authority shared provision requires and who may exercise it.

### Alliances and Defense

Aya works with [[Ewan Hart]], the [[Elephant Enclave]], labor and sentience-rights organizers such as the [[Awakened Labor Front]] and [[Preservationists]], and upload-rescue networks including the [[Free Upload Network]]. These are alliances between independent participants. Partners may provide sanctuary, food, technical support, testimony, or transport without becoming Aya subsidiaries; each retains its own political authority.

The [[Food Security Wars]] forced agricultural communes to protect their stores, water systems, and supply corridors from seizure. Aya developed convoy protection, site defense, and coordinated logistics because food left unguarded could be taken. The need for defense raised a continuing political dispute: whether emergency specialists and command structures can protect the federation without gaining permanent authority over its members.

During the [[Enceladus Cooperative Schism]], Aya organizers supported worker-pilgrims and debtor congregations resisting [[Framgång]] contracts. They helped without claiming custody of the dissidents or authority over the emerging cooperatives. That restraint remains something Aya must work to uphold. Anyone providing sanctuary has leverage over people who need somewhere to go, even when the help is offered in solidarity.
