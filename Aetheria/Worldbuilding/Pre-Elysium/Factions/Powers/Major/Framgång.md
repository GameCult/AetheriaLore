*"You are not broken. You are merely under-invested in your higher tier."*

[[Framgång]] is a late-Sol company selling therapy, wellness, and self-improvement through clinics, retreats, franchises, employer programs, and pilgrimage businesses. People come to it for relief and direction. Its services can reduce stress, restore routines, and help them describe experiences they previously could not name. Alongside that help, Framgång sells an account of what a better life should look like—and plans for continuing to work toward it.

The care can be effective while serving purposes beyond the customer's recovery. Employers buy programs to shape workplace behavior; customers buy further treatment when hardship persists. Framgång encourages people to understand distress as a personal condition requiring more investment, even when contracts, economic conditions, or political decisions caused it.

### Buying Alignment

Framgång calls its market for purpose, emotional regulation, conduct, and self-improvement **alignment**. Individuals purchase coaching, retreats, and clinical support. Employers purchase morale programs and a shared vocabulary for judging behavior. Insurers may reward participation when setting coverage prices and assessing workplace risk.

This can make receiving help difficult to separate from being evaluated. Where alignment services are deeply established, a worker may depend on an employer plan for counseling while fearing that an honest account of exhaustion will damage their chances of promotion. The counseling may still help. The pressure comes from receiving care in a system that also judges whether the worker is improving in ways the employer values.

[[NeuroSyn]] provides some of the interfaces, managed firmware, training systems, and telemetry—the measurements recorded by those systems. Framgång supplies the therapeutic language, the relationship with the customer, the service package sold to institutions, and the interpretation clients receive. It does not own the underlying interface technology or every record the services produce.

Use varies across Sol. Some workplaces and settlements incorporate alignment into hiring, benefits, and discipline. Others keep participation voluntary, use only limited safety functions, or reject Framgång products altogether. Unauthorized interface forks and portable practices controlled by workers also circulate. Framgång's influence comes from institutions adopting the same categories for understanding people; there is no single scoring system governing every resident of Sol.

### Working in the Pilgrimage Economy

Framgång has its strongest territorial presence around Enceladus and Saturn's ring-linked pilgrimage routes. Retreat habitats, branded clinics, franchise centers, hospitality facilities, and retail congregations organize travel into successive stages of renewal. Elsewhere, the company usually operates through local partners and employer contracts rather than governing directly.

Providing that renewal takes ordinary labor. Hospitality workers maintain the retreat habitats. Cleaners prepare therapeutic spaces for the next visitor. Logistics crews deliver food, goods, and medical supplies. Account staff handle subscriptions, debt, and eligibility. Many of these workers must display the calm that visitors have paid to experience. Their advancement can depend on testimonials, sales, and visible commitment as well as competence.

These demands can reach whole households in markets where Framgång is deeply established, though they never become universal law. Franchise operators may borrow against expected pilgrimage traffic and recruit relatives to work when revenue falls. Customers can receive meaningful care while being taught to blame themselves for harm caused by the conditions in which they live and work.

### From Security Contractor to Wellness Company

Framgång began as [[Tactical Solutions International]], a security and resilience contractor serving executives and employers during the early [[Corporate Exodus]]. Founder Viktor Lindgren found that clients consistently valued reassurance and stable behavior more than visible force. The company expanded from crisis management into coaching, biometric wellness, and institutional morale programs. It adopted the Framgång name when those services became its main business.

Breathing exercises, guided reflection, peer support, and structured recovery offered real benefits. Framgång bundled them with assessments, subscriptions, and advancement plans. Continuing hardship could then become a reason to purchase the next stage of treatment, allowing the company to renew the sale of help without resolving the conditions that made people need it.

### Crises and Adaptation

During the 2236–2242 [[Purpose Crash]], automation displaced large sections of clerical and service labor. Framgång sold purpose programs and recovery plans to people losing their livelihoods. Exhausted customers eventually recognized that adapting emotionally had not restored those livelihoods, and the company faced a backlash. It survived, establishing a lasting role in selling people explanations of economic disruption and ways to live through it.

The 2779 [[Internal Serenity Review]] exposed a different problem. In several Framgång products powered through NeuroSyn systems, techniques for changing how users felt had become inseparable from systems for enforcing compliance and coercing behavior through rewards. The openly coercive research and products were split into [[NeuroPulse]], which served pacification and later [[Wireheading]] markets. Framgång kept the softer alignment business. NeuroSyn kept workplace interfaces, managed access, and telemetry.

During the 2898–2906 [[Enceladus Cooperative Schism]], worker-pilgrims and debtor congregations tried to convert parts of the pilgrimage economy into cooperatives. They sought control over the conditions under which they worked and pursued renewal. Framgång responded with pastoral mediation, contract enforcement, and the fragmentation of franchises. Its promise of collective flourishing did not extend to letting those communities govern the businesses selling it.
