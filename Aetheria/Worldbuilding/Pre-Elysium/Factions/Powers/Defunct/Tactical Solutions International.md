*"Reassurance scales better than force."*

[[Tactical Solutions International]] was a middling security and resilience contractor that later became [[Framgång]]. During the early Corporate Exodus, it sold executives reassurance at premium prices and supplied employers with crowd-control packages. Founder Viktor Lindgren learned that frightened clients would pay more for language that made them feel secure than for honest guarantees about what the company could actually protect.

The company moved from crisis management into coaching, biometric wellness, and institutional morale programs. Spiritual language and aspirational lifestyle goods helped it influence behavior with less visible force. These services could offer real relief while encouraging people to treat contractual, economic, or political hardship as a personal problem requiring further paid self-improvement. Employers gained ways to shape conduct through therapeutic programs as well as through security.

When these services became its main business, Tactical Solutions adopted the Framgång name. The change improved its margins; it did not end its coercive role. Late Framgång still helped institutions secure compliance, now selling much of that control as care and personal growth.
