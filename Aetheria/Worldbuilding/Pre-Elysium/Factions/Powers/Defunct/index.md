---
title: Defunct Powers
description: "Dead firms, collapsed ventures, and institutional wreckage that still cast a long enough shadow to matter."
---

The powers in this branch are gone in the formal sense only. Some were absorbed, some collapsed, some were litigated into mulch, and some simply ran out of runway. They still matter because late-Sol history is full of institutions whose afterlives are more politically durable than their balance sheets ever were.
