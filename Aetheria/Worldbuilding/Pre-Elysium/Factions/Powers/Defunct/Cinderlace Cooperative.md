*"We taught heat to leave by another door, and the market answered by buying the building."*

[[Cinderlace Cooperative]] was a Belt materials venture that developed membranes for releasing waste heat. Made from knotted carbon lace, these membranes were the first extraordinary emitters that could be manufactured and make a practical difference under real operating conditions. The cooperative grew out of thermal-weave research led by Sadaf Nouri and Tomas Vale at Ceres Polytechnic.

That breakthrough gave a small, specialized cooperative patents that threatened much larger firms. Industries needing high-performance cooling might use a standard governed from the Belt, rather than depend on an inner-system monopoly. Control of Cinderlace's technology would help decide who could supply that cooling and on whose terms.

The [[Cinderlace Licensing War]] ended the prospect of a Belt-governed standard. [[Cryonix]] absorbed the cooperative's manufacturing arm, while PSC patent panels upheld enough of Cryonix's claims to give its victory commercial backing.

Cinderlace's surviving engineers scattered into Orbital Forge yards and black laboratories. Their knowledge continued to leak out, helping others build alternative combinations of thermal-management equipment. Cryonix gained the manufacturing business and recognized claims, but those engineers helped keep it from ever controlling the field without challenge.
