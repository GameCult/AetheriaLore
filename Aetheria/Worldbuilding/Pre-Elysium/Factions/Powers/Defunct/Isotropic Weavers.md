*"Give us dust and strain, and we will give you a world."*

[[Isotropic Weavers]] was a small materials cooperative whose composites helped make early [[Bloom]] habitats safe enough to survive construction. It formed around Laleh Pinter's research into ferrofluid-carbon containment meshes at the Ceres Polytechnic Array.

Bloom builders broke apart and recombined asteroid material to make rotating habitats. During construction, that fractured material needed support before permanent structures could carry the load. The cooperative's Dust-Shear composites provided temporary paths for those forces, making early enclosures survivable. This narrow but essential contribution briefly gave the Weavers patents with enough leverage to challenge much larger firms.

The cooperative did not survive its success. After a long licensing war, [[SolEx]] acquired its manufacturing arm. Engineers and habitat planners carried its methods and design ethos into [[Orbital Forge]], where construction knowledge circulated beyond proprietary control.

They are remembered as a small supplier whose work changed what larger powers could build and control.
