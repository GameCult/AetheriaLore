*"Tomorrow arrives on the ships we finance today."*

[[Horizon Ventures]] was an early off-world transport and finance company. It supplied launches, refined fuel, financed cargo, and provisioned habitats. Settlers depended on those services to reach their destinations and keep their settlements habitable. Horizon was among the first firms to recognize that controlling these supplies, together with debt contracts, could give a company the power to govern the people who relied on it.

Plasma physicist Dr. Mara Varga and logistics economist Kenji Sato founded Horizon in 2041. Their Budapest fusion research into compact helium-3 confinement had convinced investors that the first practical [[Soliton Drive]] prototypes were feasible. Horizon commercialized the drive, helping make regular transport between distant settlements possible.

The company promoted the [[Corporate Exodus]] as a chance to build a life on the frontier. Its financing helped enable that expansion, but its debt contracts trapped entire generations of workers off-world. The promise of opportunity coexisted with dependence on the company for transport and essential supplies.

Horizon expanded fast enough to become indispensable, but that pace also left it vulnerable. After convoy failures in 2091, pressure from shipping insurers and lunar creditors drove it to accept a merger with [[TerraCore Industries]]. The merger created [[SolEx]] in 2095, combining Horizon’s transport network with TerraCore’s mining and life-support administration. Most of Horizon’s founding idealism gave way to running transport and settlements around extraction targets and financial audits.
