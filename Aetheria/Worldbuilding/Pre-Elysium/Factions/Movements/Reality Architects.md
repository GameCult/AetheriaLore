*"Control the frame, and the crowd will build its own cage."*

The [[Reality Architects]] are specialists at [[Lucent Media]] who shape how audiences understand events. They include designers, editors, predictive dramaturges who model audience responses to stories, and perception engineers who work on how events are presented and perceived. Their job is to make a particular account persuasive by shaping its presentation and the conditions in which people encounter it.

They test different ways of presenting events, choose when information is disclosed, and help institutions give events an emotionally persuasive form. They cannot make people believe whatever they want. Their advantage is access to Lucent's distribution networks, behavioral evidence, and production capacity, along with the authority to decide which version of an event keeps receiving attention.

This work makes Lucent more than an entertainment company and places the Architects at the center of late-Sol ideology. They try to choreograph public fears and aspirations, the course of scandals, and stories of disgrace followed by redemption. When that succeeds, institutions can use people's emotional responses to help govern them, reducing the cost of direct coercion.
