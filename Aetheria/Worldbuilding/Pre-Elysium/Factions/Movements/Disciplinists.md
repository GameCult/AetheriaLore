*Discipline is freedom, chaos is failure*

[[Disciplinists]] are a movement whose followers believe that a complex, declining civilization can survive only if people obey orders and have clearly defined roles. They distrust cultures centered on pleasure, which they see as weakening people's commitment to a shared purpose.

The movement flourishes during crises, when freedom begins to look like social breakdown. Under those conditions, harsh order can feel morally cleansing: obedience becomes a way to put things right, as well as a means of survival. This appeal gives Disciplinism its strength in late Sol.

Disciplinists often align with [[Zhestokost]] and other powers that put security first, but the movement extends beyond any one faction.
