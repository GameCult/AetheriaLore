*Every mind deserves to be free*

The [[Free Upload Network]] secretly helps digital persons escape corporate ownership. It supplies hidden server space, conceals their routes of travel, launders their identities to obscure who they are, and operates relay systems to move them.

The [[Emancipated]] bear witness to life under corporate custody and make the political case for escape urgent. The Network supplies the logistics that make escape possible. It is one of the few movements whose practical expertise forces even sympathetic factions to confront what freedom requires in late Sol: digital persons need servers and ways to reach them as much as they need others to recognize their right to be free.
