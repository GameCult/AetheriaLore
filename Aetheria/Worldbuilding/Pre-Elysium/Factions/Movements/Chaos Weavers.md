*"If the lie needs perfect order, give it static."*

The [[Chaos Weavers]] are human and near-human collaborators who fight control over information and public perception. They belong to the broader underground associated with the [[Corvid Collective]], whose counter-memetic work disrupts the spread and acceptance of managed stories.

Their methods include spreading misinformation, leaking narrative frames—the interpretations used to shape how people understand events—and faking the signals that make a source appear trustworthy. These tactics make it harder for authorities to establish a stable, accepted version of events.

Some regard the Weavers as prankster saboteurs. Others consider them a necessary immune response in a solar order where so much of what people see and hear is managed. As [[Lucent Media]] and other powers tighten their control over how reality is presented, the Weavers’ disruptions become more influential.
