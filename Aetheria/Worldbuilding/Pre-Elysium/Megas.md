The [[Megas]] are powers that govern territory and organize whole societies. People living under them depend on more than their products: each mega has its own laws, supply systems, arrangements for work, political beliefs, visual culture, and account of the past.

They took shape over a thousand years as organizations consolidated, managed successive collapses, and chose which parts of earlier orders to preserve. What survived was larger and more durable than a single company. The powers below differ in what they control and how that control shapes people's lives.

### [[Zhestokost]]

Zhestokost holds Mars through military command and rationing. Its war-state builds arsenals, demands discipline, and administers the sacrifices expected of its population. Its rulers trust fear to secure obedience more reliably than consent.

Sub-faction emphasis: [[NeuroPulse]]  
Characteristic movements: [[Disciplinists]], [[Pragmatists]], selected [[Bio-Purists]]

### [[Aeronautics Unlimited]]

Aeronautics Unlimited dominates industry on the Belt frontier. It builds worlds faster than rivals can regulate them, then makes money from the social changes and problems that follow. Construction gives it power; managing the consequences extends that power into the societies it helps create.

Sub-faction emphasis: [[Orbital Forge]], [[BioElevate]]  
Characteristic movements: [[Free Minds]], [[Species Controllers]], Belt-aligned labor and salvage cultures

### [[Finch Cybernetics]]

Finch Cybernetics sells premium augmentation: graceful bodies and neural technology that preserves dignity for those who can afford it. For everyone else, augmentation comes with lifelong dependence on its services. The empire's reach rests on the continuing relationship between an augmented person and the company servicing their body.

Sub-faction emphasis: [[VitaForge]]  
Characteristic movements: [[Enhancement Liberals]], professional augmentation strata

### [[Cryonix]]

Cryonix controls enclaves along the far-side lunar arc of materials facilities and clinics, together with a narrow fabrication corridor in the Belt. Its power comes from premium materials, control over heat, and the ability to manage detectable signatures so that people and facilities can survive.

These capabilities determine who can operate more densely, at lower temperatures, and with less detectable activity than their rivals. Cryonix turns control over those operating conditions into territorial power—its form of thermal sovereignty.

Sub-faction emphasis: former [[Cinderlace Cooperative]] networks  
Characteristic movements: [[Enhancement Liberals]], elite technical guilds, quiet-running security markets

### [[Lucent Media]]

Lucent Media governs through control of what people see and how they feel about it. Spectacle and status are its instruments: it shapes what people desire, whom they envy, and what they remember. By managing visibility and collective moods, it makes perception a basis of rule.

Sub-faction emphasis: [[EternaMind]], [[Alakrita]], [[Corriedales]]  
Characteristic movements: [[Reality Architects]], [[Immortal Aristocrats]], [[Hedonists]]

### [[Sol Dominion]]

Sol Dominion concentrates Earth-Luna power in a late imperial administration. Artificial general intelligence governs alongside a hereditary hierarchy, while centralized planning directs industry. The Dominion brings these forms of authority together in pursuit of imperial power and faster-than-light travel.

Sub-faction emphasis: [[CogniSys]], [[GeneSys]], [[Quantum Dynamics]], [[SolEx]]  
Characteristic movements: [[Ascended Caste]], [[Pragmatists]], technocratic authoritarians

### [[Cetacean Navigators]]

The Cetacean Navigators govern travel corridors more than land. Their authority depends on trust, rescue, and stewardship of the routes people use. Those responsibilities make them indispensable to movement through the outer system.

Sub-faction emphasis: [[Lightsail Express]]  
Characteristic movements: [[Preservationists]], [[Free Upload Network]], [[Gene Commons]]

### [[Aya Collective]]

The Aya Collective is a federation of anti-corporate commons. It earns support by providing food, sanctuary, and repair, and by building the infrastructure people need to help one another. Mutual aid is the practical basis of its legitimacy.

Sub-faction emphasis: [[Elephant Enclave]], [[Ewan Hart]]  
Characteristic movements: [[Awakened Labor Front]], [[Preservationists]], [[Gene Commons]]

### [[Framgång]]

Framgång sells people ways to find meaning, explain themselves, and seek transcendence as civilization breaks down. It combines therapeutic promises with capitalism, making personal understanding and transformation things people can buy. Its ideas spread as a market-centered cult.

Sub-faction emphasis: [[NeuroSyn]]  
Characteristic movements: anxious consumer blocs, soft [[Bio-Purists|Bio-Purist]] currents, aspirational [[Mortal Majority]] constituencies

### Cross-Territory Powers

Other powers operate across mega borders. Some are widely distributed, some work through activities whose sponsors can deny involvement, and some control infrastructure that rival territories all need.

- [[Rossum & Douglas]] certifies the operating limits within which systems can run quietly, the safety of thermal circulation, and other routine necessities that no serious power can ignore.
- [[NiteLife Energy]] controls the main power networks on which rival territories depend.
- [[AstroDyne]] supplies the repair economy of the frontier, gray markets, and businesses close to pirates.
- [[Cognitum]] conducts cognition research where secret funding and atrocity meet.
- [[Pirate Coalition]] exercises power where the rules governing travel permits diverge from how people and goods actually move.
- [[Activist Network]] connects rights struggles across jurisdictions hostile to them.
- [[Baseline League]] organizes mortal resentment and opposition to posthumans.
