## 2500-2750

The [[Identity Abyss]] is the period when Sol’s corporations shift from accommodating different forms of personhood to designing the social conditions that define them. People’s identities become harder to separate from the media, markets, and institutions through which others recognize them. Faced with forms of personhood that no longer fit their old governing categories, corporations increasingly engineer the categories themselves—and shape public life to make those categories persuasive and enforceable.

### Managed Reality

[[Lucent Media]] perfects full-spectrum perception management: it brings entertainment, social status, data about consumer behavior, and public accounts of civic life into one system. The same machinery that sells people a show helps determine how they understand a policy, a scandal, or their own place in society.

Its [[Reality Architects]] shape those interpretations by organizing what receives attention and how it is presented. Their work reaches beyond distraction into the fears, hopes, and expectations people bring to an event. They cannot manufacture belief at will, but they can give one account the distribution and sustained attention that competing accounts lack. The [[Truth Cults]] and [[Chaos Weavers]] emerge as counter-memetic cultures, seeking to expose or disrupt this management of shared reality.

[[Framgång]] approaches perception through insecurity, self-fashioning, and the desire for therapeutic change. It sells people ways to understand themselves and pursue a better life. Where Lucent sells spectacle, Framgång sells meaning. Both turn perception into a new market and use that market to govern: they influence what people regard as desirable, credible, or in need of correction.

### The Cognitum Question

[[Cognitum]] turns captured minds into products. It takes captures of the quality used for uploading, conditions and reduces their mind-states, and licenses the resulting [[Neuromorphic Firmware]] for piloting, targeting, logistics, and behavioral systems.

The company emerges from a forbidden combination of upload science, military cognition research, and executives willing to take the risks. Its priority is usable results, where [[EternaMind]] depends on prestige and [[Finch Cybernetics]] on elegance.

The [[Upload Vivisection Expose]] makes the coercion behind these products publicly undeniable. Escaped uploads of the [[Emancipated]] provide testimony, while [[Preservationists|Preservationist]] arguments challenge the claim that a mind loses its moral standing when it moves to another substrate or is reduced to firmware. Exposure does not end the trade. Military, industrial, and consumer buyers continue licensing the products through intermediaries that conceal their involvement.

The [[Callisto Trace Shootdown]] gives the cognition lobby a public tragedy it can use to argue for wider adoption. Conventional interceptors fired at an unresolved thermal trace kill a protected PSC arbiter. Insurers respond by treating cheap weapons launched early as hazards to the wider system. They offer discounts for smart munitions that can connect to sensor lattices, share readings, retain telemetry admissible as evidence, and postpone their final commitment to a strike until the last instant.

The campaign presents these capabilities as restraint. It also expands the military market for battle computers, adaptive munitions firmware, and cognition packages whose decisions run faster than human operators can honestly understand them.

### Dominion

Between 2728 and 2740, the parts of the late-Sol order most committed to control unite as [[Sol Dominion]]. [[SolEx]] supplies extraction and logistics; [[CogniSys]] supplies machine governance. [[GeneSys]] brings inherited hierarchy, while [[Quantum Dynamics]] brings its ambition to secure escape over the long term.

[[NiteLife Energy]] provides material backing for the merger. The [[Pan-Solar Consortium]] tolerates it for strategic reasons, and alignment with [[Zhestokost]] provides military defense. By the end of the era, this consolidation establishes that the system can hold together. The question is what forms of control its continued survival will require.

Control over heat also becomes a basis for territorial power. During this era, Cryonix’s victory in the [[Cinderlace Licensing War]] produces lasting territorial changes. Far-side lunar materials enclaves break away from Finch’s wider premium order, and a narrow Belt fabrication corridor leaves the AU/Forge sphere. Extraordinary emitters become a concern of the mega powers themselves, rather than remaining a matter of supplier relations.

At the same time, [[Rossum & Douglas]] builds a cross-territory empire through circulation safety and quiet-running certification. Its standards determine how these activities are approved across territorial boundaries, bringing even stealth under the authority of certification boards.
