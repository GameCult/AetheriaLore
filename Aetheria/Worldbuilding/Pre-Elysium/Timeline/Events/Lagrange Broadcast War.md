### Lagrange Broadcast War

**Date:** 2812-2818  
**Location:** Earth-Moon L4 and L5  
**Primary actors:** [[Lucent Media]], [[Sol Dominion]], pirate relays, PSC sanctions boards

The [[Lagrange Broadcast War]] was a struggle for control of the transmission networks at Earth-Moon L4 and L5. These networks carried enough media to shape the moods and shared concerns of people across the inner system. Whoever controlled them could influence how distant populations understood events, including the war itself.

[[Sol Dominion]] wanted the networks under its authority because it regarded that influence as a strategic tool of government. For [[Lucent Media]], keeping control meant preserving both its independence as a power and the distribution system at the heart of its business. The same infrastructure served both purposes, which made control of it consequential to both sides.

Direct exchanges of fire remained limited. Much of the fighting instead involved hijacked signals, seized docks, stolen copies, raids on escorts, and escalating sanctions. Together, these struggles over access, distribution, and physical infrastructure made the war one of the defining conflicts of the late 29th century.

Lucent retained decisive cultural control despite losing some physical infrastructure. It redirected distribution around those losses faster than the Dominion could establish a settled public interpretation of its gains. For many observers, the outcome ended the assumption that control of media was secondary to control of territory.
