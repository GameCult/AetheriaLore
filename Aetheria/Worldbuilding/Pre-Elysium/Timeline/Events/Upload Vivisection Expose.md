### Upload Vivisection Expose

**Date:** 2662  
**Location:** distributed leaks across Lucent, Finch, and black-market cognition channels  
**Primary actors:** [[Emancipated]], [[Free Upload Network]], [[Cognitum]], [[Lucent Media]], investigative dissidents

The [[Upload Vivisection Expose]] was a series of leaks that revealed how uploaded people were being tortured for industrial use. Their minds were mutilated, rewritten to make them comply, and copied for experimentation. The work took place where entertainment, research and development, and military consulting overlapped.

Smuggled testimonies, time-accelerated memory fragments, and technical logs documented the extent of the abuse. As the evidence spread, it became the first cascade of leaks to make industrial digital torture publicly undeniable across Sol.

The exposure did not stop the torture: too many elites and institutions depended on its results. It did, however, change how the era could argue about the treatment of digital persons. The [[Preservationists]], [[Emancipated]], and their allies now had an archive of evidence that corporate denial campaigns could never fully erase.
