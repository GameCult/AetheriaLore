### Atmospheric Processing Dispute

**Date:** 2979-2990  
**Location:** Venus cloud cities and contested surface-processing sites  
**Primary actors:** [[Lucent Media]], [[Aya Collective]], [[Elephant Enclave]], climate engineers, Venusian labor crews

The [[Atmospheric Processing Dispute]] was a conflict over how Venus should be engineered and who its territory should serve. For Venusian labor crews and communities seeking to grow food communally, the stakes included control over their work and food supply. Decisions about atmospheric processing would help determine whether those uses had a meaningful place alongside Lucent's cultural developments.

The dispute began with disagreements over ecological engineering priorities, then widened into a territorial struggle. [[Lucent Media]] promoted its expansion as a way to make Venus humanity's most beautiful cultural capital. Its opponents sought a planet with room for several uses, including communal agriculture and work under greater control of the people doing it, rather than territory arranged around a media aristocracy's productions and residences.

Organizers linked to the [[Aya Collective]] and the [[Elephant Enclave]] argued that Lucent's promise of beauty did not answer their demands for food autonomy and labor rights. In their view, development that denied those things enclosed territory for Lucent's benefit, however attractive it looked.

Lucent largely won control of the contested territory. But memories of dispossession, philanthropy staged for publicity, and ecological work used to promote its brand permanently damaged the glamour it sold around Venus.
