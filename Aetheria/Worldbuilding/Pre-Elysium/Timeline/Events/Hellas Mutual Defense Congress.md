### Hellas Mutual Defense Congress

**Date:** 2384  
**Location:** Hellas Basin, Mars  
**Primary actors:** early [[Aya Collective]] communes, agronomy cooperatives, labor radicals, sentience-rights advocates, refugee organizers

The [[Hellas Mutual Defense Congress]] brought scattered communities together to organize the supplies and protection they needed to remain autonomous as corporate powers encroached. Representatives of agrarian communes, debt-refusal networks, labor cells, and sanctuary organizers met to coordinate water policy, crop distribution, legal aid, and collective defense. It became the closest thing the [[Aya Collective]] has to a founding convention.

The delegates faced a practical problem: an isolated community could be picked apart even if it was determined to govern itself. They established grain reserves, mutual-aid pledges, and evacuation procedures so communities could support one another. These commitments formed the basis of a federation rather than a state, allowing members to meet shared needs while retaining local authority.

The Congress also developed language for expressing institutional responsibilities that later helped Aya coordinate across species and across biological and digital forms of life. Its influence persists in later conflicts: Aya's commitment to autonomy depends on its ability to supply and defend communities, and it continues to resist being treated as a movement that offers moral protest alone.
