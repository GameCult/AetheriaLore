### Enceladus Cooperative Schism

**Date:** 2898-2906  
**Location:** Enceladus and Saturn ring pilgrimage economy  
**Primary actors:** [[Framgång]], [[Aya Collective]], [[Baseline League]], debtor congregations, retail pilgrims

The [[Enceladus Cooperative Schism]] was a struggle over who would control the businesses selling spiritual renewal around Enceladus and Saturn’s rings. Worker-pilgrims and local congregations carrying debt tried to turn parts of [[Framgång]]’s pilgrimage economy into cooperatives governed by their communities. They took the company’s promise of collective flourishing seriously: if people were meant to build better lives together, they argued, they should have a say in the work, services, and contracts that shaped those lives.

Framgång combined pastoral mediation with contract enforcement and the deliberate fragmentation of franchises. It addressed the dissidents as people needing guidance while resisting their attempt to take control. The conflict exposed a limit in its theology: the company welcomed customers seeking renewal, but opposed their demand to govern the conditions under which it was sold.

[[Aya Collective]] organizers supported the dissidents while trying to leave authority with the emerging cooperatives. They sought to help without turning dependence on outside assistance into outside rule. The [[Baseline League]] joined for a different reason: it saw Saturn as one of the last places where it could resist what it regarded as elite posthuman decadence.

The schism never fully resolved. The dispute over community control remained unsettled beyond the events of 2898–2906.
