### Ceres Control War

**Date:** 2688-2702  
**Location:** Ceres and surrounding Belt logistics corridors  
**Primary actors:** [[Aeronautics Unlimited]], [[Orbital Forge]], [[SolEx]], PSC observers, Belt labor unions

The [[Ceres Control War]] was fought over who would control development on Ceres and the Belt routes around it. For engineers, dock workers, and planners designing habitable cavities inside asteroids, the stakes included whether they could keep experimenting with how to build and inhabit the Belt.

[[SolEx]] sought to bring Ceres and its connected Bloom infrastructure under stricter administration, organized around the amount of material its operations could extract, process, and transport. At the same time, [[Aeronautics Unlimited]] (AU) and [[Orbital Forge]] were developing a more decentralized economy of habitats and salvage operations. Many workers and builders preferred the room for local variation in that system and believed SolEx control would stifle it.

Later accounts exaggerated how far the fighting escaped PSC regulation. Open combat more often remained within PSC permit structures than the war's mythology suggests. Much of the decisive struggle took the form of sabotage, labor mutiny, and disputes over who could enclose resources and infrastructure under exclusive control. AU's victory depended on more than the number of ships it could field: it persuaded enough engineers, dock workers, and cavity planners to support its approach.

The outcome showed the limits of governing the Belt solely around extraction targets. It established AU as the leading power shaping how Belt settlements were built and governed, while making Ceres the symbolic center of a frontier society built through industrial development and experimentation.
