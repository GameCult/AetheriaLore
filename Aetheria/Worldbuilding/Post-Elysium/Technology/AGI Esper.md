AGI espers are embodied machine minds able to produce [[Esper|esper]] effects. They are difficult to make reliably: a mind must develop within, or be adapted to, the substrate through which it will act. The practice descends from pre-Elysium [[Worldbuilding/Pre-Elysium/Technology/Substrate-Matched Cognition|substrate-matched cognition]] and firms such as [[Worldbuilding/Pre-Elysium/Factions/Powers/Minor/Cymata Systems|Cymata Systems]]. Copying a successful lineage can preserve useful dispositions, but neither personality nor aetheric ability is guaranteed across bodies and conditions.

The resulting ecology includes industrial operators, station intelligences, ship-selves, research lineages, and fugitive product minds. Most machinery remains non-sapient. Ship minds are common enough to create a custody regime, while Daedal Figures normally remain pilot-operated machines; a self emerging within a Concord is a rare event, not a standard control layer.

AGI esper effects vary widely and can produce inconsistent observations or continuity residue. [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Parallax Auditors|Parallax Auditors]] decide when such evidence may trigger berth denial, insurance refusal, custody review, or operator restriction without publicly naming its cause.

[[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Wavecrafters|Wavecrafters]] conceal some AGI esper labor behind components, qualification, and ritual engineering. Their industrial arm sells reproducible hardware and operator practice; inner sects preserve the exceptional minds that push those systems beyond their public envelope.
