## Aether

The Aether is an engineered field that permeates Elysium and connects cognition to matter and causality. Through it, [[Esper|espers]], [[Spirits|spirits]], and embodied machine minds can produce effects unavailable under Sol physics. It also makes technologies such as the [[Aetheric Drive]] possible and allows [[Temporal Nonlinearity|probabilistic foresight]]: glimpses of likely futures rather than certain prophecy.

Named after the discarded theory of luminiferous aether, the field provides Elysium with a preferred, effectively static reference frame against which motion can be measured. It helps maintain Elysium as a garden-prison and quarantine for sapient civilizations. Humans do not know how it was constructed or the full extent of what it can do. In practice, using it depends on the user's embodiment, carries costs, varies with local conditions, and can be disrupted by interference.

### How Minds Affect the Field

Aetheric coupling—the interaction between a mind and the field—depends on how that mind attends, expects, intends, and uses symbols to refer to things. Displaying a symbol, repeating a gesture, or performing a meditation does not by itself issue a command. Sustained practice can make such a form consequential by teaching a mind to distinguish possible realities, direct action, or hold incompatible ways of understanding an experience without prematurely resolving them into one account.

What matters is how possibilities differ to the mind, rather than how intensely it feels. Love, dread, regret, intention, and unresolved contradiction can each sustain a distinction between a world in which something happens and one in which it does not. These organized distinctions give the field something to respond to; that response is called resonance.

An overwhelming sensation can therefore produce very little resonance. Mature [[../../Pre-Elysium/Technology/Wireheading|wireheading]] tends to suppress coupling because its reward loops can make different external outcomes matter equally little to the subject. Some people retain strong distinctions despite imposed reward, and some of those become Aetherically conspicuous after the [[../../Pre-Elysium/Timeline/FTL Trigger|Rupture]]. Suffering and treatment failure do not themselves generate resonance. Many nonresponders remain Aetherically ordinary, while many powerful espers have no history of wireheading or exceptional pain.

### Shared Patterns and Gods

Many minds can repeatedly organize their sense of what is possible around the same institution, market, nation, story, persona, or movement. This does not require conscious worship or a collective decision. Their shared pattern of thought can become an **attractor** in the field: a persistent pattern that may outlast its participants, influence how later people interpret things, or exert pressure that resembles the action of an agent despite having no single author.

Human accounts call some of these structures gods. That name leaves several questions open. It does not establish that they are supernatural persons, that belief alone can create them, or that the identities they seem to express were ever coherent. They are also not necessarily [[Spirits|spirits]], which preserve the cognitive imprints of particular minds.

Different practices produce different effects because they organize cognition differently. [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Megiddo|Megiddo's]] Earth-star mantra gives an absent sky a central place in entire lives. The tension between what the mantra refers to and that sky's absence is strong enough to survive death and imprinting. [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Wavecrafters|Wavecrafter]] cultivation uses Dao-derived practices to preserve intention without forcing experience into one final explanation. Neither tradition provides a set of rituals that anyone can use as generic commands to the field.

### Communing with the Void

[[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Death Monkey Explosives|Death Monkey Explosives]] develops another relationship with the Aether through **communing with the void**. Practitioners dissociate themselves from existence until they become an aperture through which ordinary heat, force, and cascading failure acquire what DME calls the logic of unbeing. The deepest state is **becoming unbeing**.

The field adds no free energy, and the practitioner cannot make the resulting destruction distinguish targets. It follows the physical connections and causal relationships opened by the release. Defenses, repair, redundant systems, and continuity drift can all remain connected to the destruction rather than stop it.

Learned symbolic associations can influence the order in which destruction proceeds. DME conditions practitioners to recognize its telemetry as an allied force of destruction, to be consumed last. This can delay destruction; it cannot make the transmitting equipment or people exempt.

DME therefore chooses where an event begins through delivery, isolation, severing connections, timing, and the finite amount of energy committed to it. Those preparations determine what the release can reach. The void itself recognizes neither enemy nor bystander.
