## Aether

Named for the discarded luminiferous aether, the Aether is Elysium’s pervasive engineered substrate and preferred, effectively static reference frame. It couples matter and causality to cognition, enabling [[Esper|espers]], [[Spirits|spirits]], and embodied machine minds to produce effects unavailable under Sol physics, including [[Temporal Nonlinearity|probabilistic foresight]] and the [[Aetheric Drive]].

The field helps maintain Elysium as a garden-prison and quarantine for sapient civilizations. Its construction and full capabilities remain beyond human knowledge; practical use is embodied, costly, locally variable, and vulnerable to interference.

Aetheric coupling responds to cognition as organized through attention, expectation, intention, and symbolic reference. A symbol, gesture, or meditation is not a command that affects the field merely by being displayed or repeated. It becomes consequential when sustained practice makes it the stable form through which a mind distinguishes realities, directs action, or holds incompatible frames without collapsing them prematurely.

[[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Megiddo|Megiddo's]] Earth-star mantra is one population-scale example: the absent sky organizes entire lives strongly enough that its referential tension survives death and imprinting. [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Wavecrafters|Wavecrafter]] cultivation produces another relation through Dao-derived practices that preserve intention without forcing experience into one final account. The traditions produce different effects because they organize cognition differently; neither supplies a generic ritual vocabulary for commanding the field.
