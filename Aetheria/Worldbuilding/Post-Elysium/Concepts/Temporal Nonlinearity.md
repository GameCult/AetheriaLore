### Temporal Nonlinearity

One of the peculiarities of the Timeless’ purpose in constructing Elysium is that they, for their own inscrutable reasons, did not wish to actually exterminate any of the civilizations they quarantined to this isolated shard of bespoke spacetime. To ensure that the prisoners never manage to wipe themselves out, temporal contingencies were put in place. Each prime species has its own reset lineage within a shared inhabited Elysium. The lineage names the population whose continuity the contingency tracks, not an exclusive world occupied by that species alone; mixed settlements, diplomacy, migration, and interspecies institutions can all occur inside it.

A reset rewrites the lived history of that lineage when its prime species can no longer continue. Other species encountered within the rewritten history do not thereby have their own prime lineages reset. The same contact, settlement, or person may therefore have incompatible histories across lineages without one account becoming the universal outside view. Around each lived history lies a continuum of unrealized possibilities rather than a set of simultaneously inhabited alternate civilizations. The Aetheric Field, with its ability to directly interact with sapient minds, monitors those possibilities and the continuity it has been built to preserve.

Human institutions repeatedly reward reckless acceleration, competitive deployment, and the exploitation of technologies and resources whose costs can be displaced. Humanity therefore tends to speedrun its own destruction. Other species may sustain longer branches because their histories produced different institutions for risk, redundancy, obligation, dissent, and survival—not because cooperation is a species essence. [[../Species/Ratfolk|Ratfolk]] societies contain examples of hard-won precaution and its paternalistic failure modes.

The Aetheric Field also incorporates some predictive capacity, a side effect of its pervasiveness and inherent ability to process information. Visions of the future accessed via the [[Aether]] are not prophetic, merely likely extrapolations of the timeline.

Possibility does not vanish merely because one branch is lived. Strong cognitive structures can recur, interfere with the present, or acquire inertia across resets without granting ordinary memory to the people in the next iteration. Repeated symbols, relationships, institutions, and apparent spirits may therefore be evidence of Aetheric pressure rather than proof that two characters share one identity or that a previous history has been restored.
