Espers are sapient minds that can sense or alter reality through the [[Aether]]. In practical work, an esper may be unusually good at tuning a drive, interpreting an interface, or keeping a difficult system stable. The disputed point is whether the operator’s intention itself helps produce the result.

An esper’s ability depends on predisposition, the body or substrate they inhabit, training, practice, and local conditions. Trauma, neurodivergence, meditation, and exposure to deep pseudospace can each affect access to that ability. None explains every esper or proves that someone is one. Even the most capable mortal espers have little control compared with the Timeless.

Uploading can preserve an esper’s ability, but modifications, coercive conditioning, or a poor match between the mind and its new substrate may destroy it. Because the law denies uploads personhood, an uploaded esper can be treated as research property. Such minds are attractive to researchers, yet the difficulty of preserving their abilities makes them unreliable mass-market products. [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Technology/AGI Esper|AGI espers]] bring machine minds into the same dispute over sapient intention.

Operator certification systems recognize that some people perform measurably better than others. They attribute the difference to training, temperament, or proprietary technique while suppressing evidence that sapient intention can directly produce an effect. Research programs exploit that capacity. Entertainment, clinical labels, secrecy law, and controlled publication help keep it publicly dismissible even where it is put to use.

Esper activity can leave inconsistent observations or continuity residue: records that remain credible individually but disagree about what happened. [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Parallax Auditors|Parallax auditors]] may classify this evidence for a named receiving office and a specified use. The office then decides whether to accept the finding. Any restriction it applies must fall within its own authority; the auditor’s classification does not itself impose one.

[[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Wavecrafters|Wavecrafters]] conceal their deepest esper practice within the manufacture of components, operator qualifications, and ritual engineering. Customers can buy the product without being told about the mind whose work made its best performance possible.
