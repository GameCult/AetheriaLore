# Death Monkey Explosives Products

The DME catalogue spans weapons, propulsion, power, sensing, cooling, and protective equipment. Product names and marketing copy circulate across the [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Death Monkey Explosives|Death Monkey Explosives]] brand commons; a name may describe several workshop forks rather than one centrally controlled design.

Weapon codes follow the shared scheme of range prefix, caliber where applicable, weapon or launcher type, and functionality suffix. Two prototype codes have been normalized here: `XPC-I` to `XPS-I`, and `EMPKP` to `LEMPS`. Blank source fields remain unspecified.

### cold like my heart

- **Type:** Heat pump
- **Tech level:** High

> nothing can touch me/i'm an agent of entropy/heat death in a bottle like a molotov cocktail

An expensive high-performance heat pump built for DME thermal chains expected to endure violent load changes and ugly emergency states. It moves heat; it does not destroy it.

### scorched void policy

- **Type:** `SRDMM-I`
- **Expanded type:** Short-range dumbfire self-propelled incendiary micromissiles

> let's set the stars on fire/ there's nothing left on earth to burn/ we're just killing time while we're filling space/ and catacombs in turn

The code combines `SR` (short range), `D` (dumbfire), `MM` (self-propelled micromissile), and `-I` (incendiary).

### banana split

- **Type:** `MIRV-I`
- **Expanded type:** Incendiary multiple independent reentry vehicle

> it's the scatterplot non-stop cherry-on-top of the sundae/so go bananas/start a fight/ it's a banana split kind of sunday, yeah

`MIRV` takes no guidance prefix in the inherited category scheme; `-I` marks the incendiary variant.

### fuck your feelings

- **Type:** Sensor

> we're long distance lights of stars colliding/come on, do your worst/ let's see who blinks first/i don't care, i don't care

The surviving catalogue does not specify active, passive, or combined operation.

### pretty pretty bang bang

- **Type:** `XPS-I`
- **Expanded type:** Incendiary explosively propelled slug weapon
- **Tech level:** Low tech
- **Legacy source code:** `XPC-I`

> make your last stand/it's a pretty pretty mess you've got us into/it's a pretty pretty pretty day to die/ locked and loaded we're going out with a bang

The live code uses `XPS` (explosively propelled slug) and `-I` (incendiary). The prototype `XPC` class does not occur in the shared weapon-category scheme.

### make bombs not war

- **Type:** Reactor

> took so long to get a reaction/at last we're gaining some traction/listen to the blast as we rev the engines

The surviving catalogue specifies no reactor scale, construction, or technology level.

### deep space burnout

- **Type:** Thrusters
- **Size:** Large
- **Construction:** Sturdy
- **Tech level:** Low

> go go go go go/ get the drift/you'll burn before you stop / you're taking this comet trail right to the top

A deliberately repairable propulsion line whose low technology level is part of its appeal in workshops expected to replace damaged parts without clean facilities.

### rail against the machine

- **Type:** `LEMPS`
- **Expanded type:** Large electromagnetically propelled slug weapon
- **Construction:** Sturdy
- **Tech level:** Medium
- **Legacy source code:** `EMPKP`

> electromagnetic super-kinetic/let's get frenetic/throw up your hands and rail against the machinery

The live code combines `L` (large caliber) and `EMPS` (electromagnetically propelled slug). The prototype `EMPKP` class does not occur in the shared weapon-category scheme.

### watch me burn

- **Type:** Flamethrower

> i've got armageddon in my sights and you're the smoke in my lungs/ i'm a fire eater/watch me burn/ watch me burn

The catalogue preserves the plain-language class and supplies no additional scale, construction, or technology level.

### not if i see you first

- **Type:** Sensor

> Blind your eyes, staring into the void/ there's nothing looking back/ there's nothing for you here

The surviving catalogue does not specify active, passive, or combined operation.

### gamma raybans (make you incandescent)

- **Type:** Protective accessory
- **Function:** Reduces ionizing-radiation exposure to the wearer or protected system

The prototype catalogue represented this protection as `-25% ionizing damage`. The live description preserves the function without treating a game statistic as an in-world measurement.
