# Port-Insurer Compact

The Port-Insurer Compact is not a sovereign court or a ship-mind ministry. It is the shared restriction layer that lets berth authorities, insurers, custody desks, and claimant offices act on the same dirty record without pretending they all own the same question.

Its public purpose is dull: preserve traffic safety, coverage integrity, and custody continuity when a vessel arrives with contaminated route history, disputed command authority, or an embodied mind whose status is still unresolved. Its practical purpose is sharper. The compact names which office gets the first lawful cut against a ship-self before anyone has to decide whether that ship-self is survivor, asset, debtor, witness, weapon, or person.

### Jurisdiction

The compact applies at controlled movement gates: docking permission, departure clearance, tow acceptance, emergency repair berth, insurance coverage, salvage title hold, custody review, and bonded transfer. It matters most when a vessel cannot be handled as an ordinary ship because its mind, body, and record have become the same disputed object.

Parallax auditors do not command the compact. A [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Parallax Auditors|Parallax]] desk may issue or validate a [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Reference/Continuity Admissibility Finding|continuity admissibility finding]], but the compact only bites when a named receiving office accepts that finding under its own rules. A port can cut berth. An insurer can cut coverage. A custody desk can cut testimonial control. A claimant office can cut transfer. The same contaminated record does not become every punishment at once unless each office signs its own route into the restriction.

### Powers

The common compact tools are small enough to sound administrative:

- conditional berth with isolation requirements
- reconciliation bond before physical docking
- coverage refusal or temporary policy suspension
- custody liaison before repair, tow, or transfer
- command-authority freeze inside a named operating surface
- claimant hold on title, cargo access, or component removal
- operator credential suspension for the person or mind presenting control
- compact emergency panel for CAF-4 quarantine cases

This is why the restriction is so useful. It does not need to seize the whole vessel to make movement expensive. A ship can be allowed to keep speaking while being denied a dock, allowed to dock while losing coverage, allowed to repair while losing command authority over one subsystem, or allowed to depart only after a bond turns suspicion into payable risk.

### Limits

The compact does not decide personhood, ownership, continuity, guilt, or metaphysical truth. It decides which first right may be altered at the next gate. A CAF-3 packet may deny berth as of right; it does not sell the hull. A custody liaison may prevent a ship-self's testimony from controlling one hearing; it does not prove the mind is false. A coverage refusal may price inherited contamination; it does not make contamination a crime.

Every valid compact action needs four things: an admissibility class, a receiving office, a named consequence already recognized by that office, and a recorded first right being altered. Without those four elements, the compact is supposed to collapse back into notation, surcharge, or ordinary negotiation. In practice, poor crews discover that "supposed to" is not a docking pass.

### Abuses And Conflict Hooks

Ports use compact holds to discipline unwanted traffic without issuing a ban. Insurers use them to discount expensive claims, force reconciliation bonds, or make contaminated crews sell future work at worse terms. Salvage houses try to provoke compact action before a ship-self can establish rescue, personhood, or bonded crew testimony. Manufacturers hide warranty failures inside custody language. Military registrars borrow compact categories when they need a command chain disowned without a public scandal.

Gray-market clearance brokers survive in the shadow of the compact. They sell dirty windows, shell-owner transfers, spoofed route histories, poisoned audit packets, and temporary sponsor bonds. These services can keep a fugitive ship mind alive. They can also make the next gate worse by teaching every respectable office that the vessel's history has already been negotiated by liars.

For [[Lore/Nibu|Nibu]], the compact is the name of the door that keeps closing differently. It can require proof that an attached shuttle is inert, quote an isolation surcharge because everyone knows inertness is the disputed point, deny arrival while leaving recovery contractors free to call themselves authorized claim escorts, or accept a narrow helm-continuity finding while refusing to unlock cargo title, owner vaults, or full custody recognition. The cruelty is not inconsistency. The cruelty is that the inconsistency has a receipt.

### Provenance

The compact descends from the same route-integrity panels that produced modern Parallax practice after the Kestrel Palisade claim. Ports needed a way to keep strange vessels outside the pressure wall. Insurers needed a way to suspend coverage without admitting their tables could not describe the event. Custody desks needed a way to hold embodied minds long enough for hearings to become affordable to the powerful. The compact is the settlement between those needs.

Its current form is visible in PAF-CAF records: old custody marker, new admissibility class, owning Parallax desk, named receiving office, mandatory consequence, first altered right. That record shape is the compact's real body. The office is wherever the next gate has enough leverage to make the finding hurt.
