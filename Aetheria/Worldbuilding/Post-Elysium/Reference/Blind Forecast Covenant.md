# Blind Forecast Covenant

A **Blind Forecast Covenant** is a financing instrument that makes ignorance part of the collateral. It is used when a lender, insurer, supplier pool, or infrastructure patron prices a long obligation through [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Technology/Oracular Computing|oracular computing]], but expects the forecast to become materially less reliable if the people inside it learn which outcomes were predicted.

The covenant does not sell a future and an oracle does not guarantee one. [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Concepts/Temporal Nonlinearity|Temporal nonlinearity]] supplies weighted possibilities from observable conditions, with reliability falling when a forecast changes the decisions it describes. The contract turns that technical limit into an allocation of power: the capital side may inspect the adverse branches, while named **reflexive subjects** must continue working, traveling, treating, buying, or governing without knowing which of their injuries, refusals, defections, deaths, or acts of rescue made the project bankable.

## The Dark Schedule

Every covenant attaches a **dark schedule** to an otherwise ordinary loan, lease, insurance policy, supply agreement, or service bond. The schedule identifies:

- the oracle, observation packet, run time, horizon, and confidence limits;
- the obligation priced against the forecast;
- the reflexive subjects whose informed choices could materially change it;
- the protected branches that those subjects may not receive;
- the offices allowed to inspect each branch;
- the observable operating conditions the borrower promises to preserve;
- the events that count as forecast contamination;
- the dependent contracts entitled to notice; and
- the cure window, reforecasting right, bond, or termination path after contamination.

Subjects do not always sign. A habitat authority can covenant to preserve the ignorance of its workforce, a House can do so for pilots and service families, and a ship operator can do so for a crew. A person may therefore be bound as a forecast input without being recognized as a party. They receive operational instructions stripped of the reasons behind them: remain on the third shift, defer a clinic transfer, do not change a route supplier, retain one unpopular supervisor, carry a component past its normal service interval. The disclosed instruction may be accurate. Its concealed purpose may be that the oracle priced a death, strike, desertion, or last-minute intervention whose probability depends on nobody involved seeing it coming.

The schedule also creates compulsory surveillance. Creditors need evidence that the observation packet remains comparable, managers need to know who learned what, and every information system becomes a possible contamination path. Medical records, maintenance complaints, private messages, union meetings, route inquiries, and even unusual purchases can be reviewed for signs that a subject has inferred a protected branch. A covenant can preserve a forecast without proving the forecast true; it need only preserve the conditions under which capital agreed to trust it.

High-grade covenants are commonly run through unique [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Technology/Necrotech|Eidolon]] oracles whose behavior cannot be reproduced on demand. [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/House Valence|House Valence]] benefits twice when one of its registered instruments supplies the forecast: the client depends on the oracle for the first price and on Valence-recognized continuity for any later reforecast. Damage, disputed repair, or distress in the Eidolon can therefore impair an entire credit stack without invalidating a single physical asset.

## Knowledge Default

A **knowledge default** occurs when a protected branch reaches a named reflexive subject or when an intervention taken from that branch changes the observed conditions beyond the schedule’s tolerance. The borrower may be current on every payment. The project may be safer than it was. Neither fact preserves the old bargain. What defaulted was the priced claim that the relevant people would decide without this knowledge.

Once declared, notice moves through the dependency web. The oracle provider withdraws the old confidence statement. A lender freezes undrawn credit or demands a new bond. An insurer reprices coverage or applies a hold available under its own policy. Suppliers shorten terms because the operating facility has lost insured continuity. Licensed service firms decline work they cannot place under the former risk packet. Route and berth operators that require recognized coverage demand security the borrower no longer has. Payroll and maintenance reserves are spent keeping one gate open long enough to reach the next.

No central office commands this cascade. Each party protects one narrow exposure, and each decision makes the following refusal locally reasonable. The [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Reference/Port-Insurer Compact|Port-Insurer Compact]] does not merge those authorities or let a forecast covenant manufacture powers for them. A port still controls only its gate, an insurer only its policy, and a registrar only its records. A borrower can nevertheless be stranded by several bounded decisions that all rely on the same vanished price.

Warnings become sabotage under this arrangement. A worker who tells a pilot that the schedule predicts their death may save the pilot and destroy the credit supporting five thousand other lives. Employers call this **forecast spoilage** and seek damages equal to reforecasting, delay, and downstream default rather than the value of the leaked document. Unions and dissidents call it charging the warned person for becoming difficult to kill on schedule.

## Class And Slack

Wealth purchases the right to know. Large sovereigns and established Houses commission several independent runs, pay for short reforecast intervals, post contamination bonds, and keep redundant suppliers outside any one schedule. Their principals can read an adverse branch and absorb the changed price. Poor habitats, small crews, clinics, and marginal workshops buy one sealed run because it is the only instrument cheap enough to unlock long credit. Their people must remain predictable for years.

The same divide shapes appeal. A wealthy borrower can contest whether knowledge actually reached a subject. A poor borrower cannot keep wages, air, and service contracts current while the question is reviewed. Even a false allegation of contamination can force the very defaults that make a new forecast worse.

Small work groups have developed one useful countermeasure without pretending they can refinance a station. They keep a little **unforecast slack**: meal chits, repair hours, spare bunks, clinic transport, independent instruments, and passage promises allocated by immediate need rather than by a dark schedule’s risk categories. Nobody must disclose why they need the reserve, so using it does not prove that a protected branch leaked. It cannot preserve the corporate stack. It can keep one warned person alive, let one witness leave, or carry a household through the cure window while respectable institutions explain that knowledge was the expensive part.
