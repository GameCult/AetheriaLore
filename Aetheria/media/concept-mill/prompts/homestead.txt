## Prompt

Design the Homestead, a small two-person Ewan Hart tug spacecraft. Its whole body follows a vintage farm tractor: a long, rounded hood at the front, a cab behind it, and two huge rear fenders swollen into broad, closed, rounded drums. The two fender drums are the drive pods, each ending in a round drive nozzle at the rear. The cab is the cockpit, with a rounded roof and a large gauge panel visible through the front window. The hood carries a grille at its nose and one small defensive mount on top. A heavy towing hitch projects from the rear between the drums. One implement mount sits under the front grille. Faded harvest-gold hood and drums, agricultural-green cab, cream maker plates, black hoses along the hood, chipped enamel at the corners. One complete ship, centered with clear margin around it, three-quarter front view from slightly above showing the dorsal surface and one side; moderate lens; plain seamless light gray background; soft key light with gentle form shadows; matte finishes throughout.
