# Bloom Habitat Article Audit

Status: brainstorming. This audits the current [[Worldbuilding/Pre-Elysium/Technology/Bloom Habitat Anatomy]] article before rewriting it. The point is not to preserve the article. The point is to identify which parts are salvageable, which parts need a causal basis, and which parts should be cut.

## Keep As Orientation

### Open interior correction

The article's correction that a mature Bloom is a broad pressurized commons should remain. This is foundational and changes the whole spatial model.

Needs improvement:

- explain how a large common atmosphere is zoned without becoming a compartment stack
- explain smoke, heat, humidity, and pathogen control
- separate normal open atmosphere from emergency isolation

### Radial vocabulary

The radial terms are useful:

- inward
- outward
- spinward
- counterspinward
- axial

Needs improvement:

- the article should use these terms consistently instead of falling back into poles/equator language without explanation
- every Pallas visual prompt should know which direction is outward into shell and which direction is inward to open air

### Ilya route correction

The fixed answer that Ilya comes from the glassed Kappa operations gallery and badge-only route should remain.

Needs improvement:

- show how that gallery relates to spoke-base control deck
- show why the route exists in standard AU yard planning
- show how authority moves faster than labor because of architecture

## Rewrite From First Principles

### Primary layers

Current problem:

The article lists axial hub, spire, spokes, inner surface, shell, exterior skin. That is a useful skeleton but not a mechanism.

Rewrite method:

- start with rotating pressure-shell model
- explain which parts rotate and which parts are despun or partially despun
- explain transfer collars before calling spokes "elevators"
- derive inner surface, utility mat, structural shell, and shielding from loads and flows

Needed decision:

- choose a working spire/hub rotation model or explicitly present variants

### Pressure boundaries

Current problem:

The article lists envelope types but does not explain why each boundary exists.

Rewrite method:

- global envelope contains open atmosphere
- normal airflow zones manage daily life
- emergency isolation zones close under breach/fire/toxin/quarantine/security
- service cells have different atmosphere because maintenance, heat, contamination, or pressure transients require it
- seal lungs exist to buffer specific pressure events, not as generic pressure stuff

Needed decision:

- decide whether Kappa's seal lungs are primarily air-pressure equalization, industrial process damping, or emergency buffer for a yard segment

### Life support

Current problem:

The air/water/heat/waste sections contain plausible details but behave like separate essays.

Rewrite method:

- trace one breath, one liter of water, one unit of waste, and one unit of heat through the habitat
- show where those flows cross
- show where crossovers need manifolds, valves, sensors, and maintenance access
- show how AU monetizes or defers those maintenance points

Needed decision:

- decide whether farms are primarily visible ecology, food production, air contribution, waste processing, or all of the above by Bloom class

### Maintenance hierarchy

Current problem:

The article names public hatches, galleries, service rings, crawl throats, service arteries, and blind pockets. The names are good. The derivation is weak.

Rewrite method:

- introduce maintenance access as cost optimization
- size each access level by replacement part size, failure frequency, rescue requirement, and operator ethics
- distinguish spaces for routine work from spaces only entered when the cheaper robot failed

Needed decision:

- formalize the access hierarchy in terms of what can pass through it: resident hand, technician body, equipment cart, suited baseline worker, modified octopoid worker, inspection drone, emergency stretcher

## Cut Or Demote

### Clever one-liners that substitute for design

Lines like "the pipes will know" can survive only after the engineering is earned. In the rewrite, remove style until the mechanism works. Add flavor after the load paths and flows behave.

### Lists of technical nouns

Any sentence of the form "contains manifolds, valves, sensor racks, coolant trees, condensate traps..." should be rewritten unless it directly follows a flow explanation.

Replacement pattern:

> The coolant trunk crosses the return-air gallery here because heat exchange has to happen before contaminated air re-enters the main processors. That crossing creates a manifold room with replaceable valve cartridges, condensate drains, contaminant sensors, and an access route sized for a baseline cart.

### "Districts" as default sealed cells

Do not let district language imply compartment stacks. Districts are civic/social/economic zones inside a common atmosphere unless a hazard or service boundary is specified.

### "Polar" geography without a cylinder explanation

The older Bloom article still talks about polar/far-pole agriculture. This may be salvageable as endcap or low-g specialty agriculture, but not in the current vague form.

## Missing Systems

### Fire ecology

Need answer:

- how smoke moves
- where smoke returns live
- what shuts during fire
- what happens to farms and open plazas
- how industrial yards isolate without depressurizing the world

### Acoustic and vibration ecology

Large open habitats carry sound differently from compartment stacks. Industrial zones, spokes, pumps, traffic, and axial machinery all create noise/vibration politics.

Need answer:

- where expensive quiet exists
- where workers live with pump noise
- whether TCS dampens vibration or transmits it

### Pest, pathogen, and biofilm ecology

Open air plus farms plus wet loops means life gets into places. Maintenance is biological, not just mechanical.

Need answer:

- who cleans air returns
- how biofilm affects seal lungs and condensate traps
- whether BioElevate organisms or VitaForge TCS organisms complicate this

### Structural inspection

A rotating pressure shell needs crack monitoring, strain sensors, shear-key inspection, and repair paths.

Need answer:

- where sensors live
- how often humans/uplifts enter inspection cavities
- what a dangerous false-clear route looks like

### Law and incident classification

For Ghostlight scenes, authority flows through data as much as corridors.

Need answer:

- what events must be logged automatically
- what supervisors can reclassify
- which logs go to AU, BioElevate, labor, safety, or external regulators
- how workers use logs as leverage

## Rewrite Acceptance Criteria

A revised article is acceptable only if a reader can answer:

1. Why is this space here?
2. What flows through it?
3. What load or boundary does it respect?
4. Who normally uses it?
5. Who enters when it fails?
6. What would AU cheapen?
7. What would Orbital Forge standardize?
8. What would BioElevate exploit?
9. How does this shape a scene?
10. How does this shape an item, commodity, or maintenance contract in the game economy?
