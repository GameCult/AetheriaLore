# Corporate Roster and Item Wishlist

An at-a-glance index of the corporations and the item flavor written for them, pulled together from two source documents that never reached the vault: the *Aetheria Worldbuilding To-Do* (faction table, taglines, equipment field list) and the *Items [Aetheria]* spreadsheet (per-corporation item ideas). Quoted lines are verbatim from those documents.

This is brainstorming, not settled canon. It exists so the flavor stops living in scattered files, and so branding decisions have one place to start from. The right-hand columns say what the game's catalog actually ships today, checked 2026-09-19, because the gap between the wishlist and the catalog is the useful part.

### Taglines and self-image

- **NiteLife Energy** — "We keep the lights on, so you can keep on". A 'hip young company', not at all like those fat cat overchargers back on Earth. They went with the Colonists on the bet they could become the only fat cat overcharger in the business.
- **Alakrita** — "Speed. Elegance. *Alakrita*." Ultra high-end. Spare parts are hard to come by, the perfect alignment of the whole system makes them failure prone, but when they go, they go. Marketing protects the exclusivity of the brand.
- **Lightsail Express** — "We'll lose our lives before we lose your cargo". Markets itself on speed and reliability, is in actual fact neither, and holds every major contract in a titanium grip because Elysium has no postal service.
- **Adrasteia** — "Shhh." Where are they based? No one knows. Their stealth tech makes other corps very, very nervous.
- **Death Monkey Explosives** — "Turn it up, burn it down". Brand promotes chaos and anarchy; actually led by a very sharp gal named Yara So, who may have her own destructive agenda.
- **Corriedales** — a toy company that pivoted when a small part of a poorly selling toy solved a targeting problem. Their weapons could be named after in-universe cartoon characters, for the superfans who outfit their ships that way.
- **Odla Framgång** — atoms of gold among the heaping space dumps of garbage. Spifes (spoon knives) for zero gravity snacking, and the ultimate nullgen shield.

## Equipment flavor wishlist

Every item wants: a name, a tweet-length description, a manufacturer, a size category (small, medium, large, capital), a tech level (low, medium, high), and a construction (light and fragile, or heavy and durable). The catalog's own fields now carry most of this, so the wishlist is mostly an authoring checklist.

Item kinds the list asks for: cargo bay, cloaking system, thruster, reactor, capacitor, shield, cooler (radiator plus heat pump), sensor (passive plus active), ballistics (railgun, slug, shotgun, burst, machinegun), energy (laser, continuous, pulse, lightning gun, ion cannon, ion slug, ion shotgun, charged variants), and launchers (guided torpedo, MIRV, guided swarm, dumbfire swarm, incendiary variants — "pretty pretty bang bang").

The spreadsheet's abbreviation scheme is the same one the reference note documents: [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Reference/Weapon Category Codes|Weapon Category Codes]].

Condensed; the flavor is what matters, and the best lines are quoted as written.

## Where the wishlist and the catalog disagree

- **Four corporations in the table have no catalog presence at all**: Aya Collective, Corriedales, Odla Framgång, Megiddo, plus the pirates.
- **Two ship nothing despite having designs**: Finch Cybernetics and Ewan Hart Inc. have influence but zero products, so nothing of theirs can spawn.
- **Fourteen designs no product sells**, so they cannot appear in play: Autocannon, Refinery, SRMM72, Industrial Thermostatic Heater, LRMM72, Surface Ore Extractor, Core Power, Deep Ore Extractor, Large Drive, plight, Tractor Beam, pswarm, Assembly Line, Shipyard.
- **Whole item kinds from the wishlist are missing from the catalog**: no shields at all, no cloaking systems, no capacitor products, no consumables.
- **Several spreadsheet items did make it in** and can be read as the branding working: Arctica, cold like my heart, deep space burnout, OK Disperser, ClearPath, Earp, Traction, Vulcan, Manhattan, ChargeBlast.

## What this is for

Two live pieces of work read from here. Product names and flavor text are being split so that a design carries dry functional text and a product carries the manufacturer's voice ([[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Reference/Transponder Credentials|credentials]] aside, the brand a player sees is derived from who made the lot). And each product authors quality per role, so a market segment is a second product with one part raised — which needs exactly the identity this roster describes: who is actually good at what, and who merely says so.
