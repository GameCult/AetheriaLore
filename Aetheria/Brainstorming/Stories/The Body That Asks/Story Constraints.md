# Story Constraints

## Social Body

The story must preserve the agency and material presence of ordinary cadets and families; current and former Kinship teams; Vesper's pilots; teachers, clinicians, cooks, fabricators, riggers, security, and medical workers; staff with different knowledge; populations threatened by invasion; communities affected by the Unseating; and the Houses, sponsors, insurers, and custody authorities claiming the Figures and AFTER.

Recurring school-side decision owners include Rhea Mott, Jun Vale, Pava Neris, Enna Quill, Marae Dho, and Aru Sen. They must disagree for material reasons and alter what the protagonists can do; none exists only to certify that escape is correct.

No aggregate becomes innocent merely by opposing Meridian, and no worker becomes identical with the institution because their labor keeps it running.

## Narrative Invariants

- Trauma may correlate with esper access, but damage is not the source of love, compatibility, or worth.
- Kinship cannot manufacture free attachment by making dependency more sophisticated.
- Standardized roles shape behavior without becoming psychological destiny.
- Historical and present characters remain distinct; parallels do not prove reincarnation or recurrence.
- Concordance never collapses the five into one mind.
- AFTER is a participant, not an appliance, guide, parent simulation, or authorization token.
- The Necro Concord genuinely functions; its centralized cycle creates its tactical power and strategic ceiling.
- Training, experimentation, transformation, combat, rescue, and separation obey the same local-ownership rules.
- Refusal materially changes the climax and improves coherence.
- Calyx produced liberation and domination pressure; neither cancels the other.
- The invasion model is credible. Meridian's abuse does not make the threat imaginary.
- The Continuance Directorate's expansion and the Lantern Compact's forecast remain credible without turning predicted casualties into consent.
- Vey is complicit in Kinship and deceived about Necro. His opposition cannot switch off distributed authority.
- Meridian genuinely educates and serves students. Ordinary cadets have divided responses and are not scenery.
- Escape does not require staff stupidity, universal cruelty, or universal sympathy.
- Found family emerges through work, repair, boundaries, humor, and chosen obligation.
- At least one honored refusal makes the immediate plan worse. Consent cannot matter only when it improves performance.
- Component expertise remains local while crown and coordination offices are practiced, transferable work rather than permanent identities.

## Prohibited Resolutions

- A permanent benevolent crown pilot.
- Clones, reincarnations, bloodline selection, or metaphysical identity with the Fivefold.
- A compatibility score, confession, or group embrace unlocking power without prior practice.
- Deliberately intensified trauma as performance enhancement.
- AFTER secretly controlling the escape.
- Destruction of Meridian with cadets and workers as acceptable collateral.
- Exposure producing immediate universal recognition or support.
- The five becoming enlightened rulers.
- A clean verdict that the Fivefold were pure liberators or failed tyrants.
- Prior candidates existing only to prove the protagonists special.
- Defeating, persuading, or replacing Vey switching off the institution.
- A shouted combination slogan substituting spectacle for individual assent.

## Reader Contract

Without setting homework, readers must understand that each teenager pilots a humanoid ship as an extended body; five Figures can transform into one larger body; and the combined body works only when coordination preserves each pilot's refusal.

By ten percent, Meridian should feel desirable and the Finalists' continued composition should matter. By twenty-five percent, AFTER should feel like a vulnerable possible person. By fifty percent, escape should be desirable, materially frightening, socially costly, and politically complicated.

The opening fails if Meridian reads immediately as a torture chamber, if the five announce assigned archetypes, or if giant-robot embodiment is withheld so sternly that the premise apologizes for itself.

Comedy comes from academy rivalry, attachment metrics, clinical vocabulary used against clinicians, engineers confronting embarrassing transformation aesthetics, and a military project whose organization chart misunderstands friendship. Tenderness comes from maintenance: repairing a joint another person feels as pain, learning when not to ask, carrying work without converting it into debt, and checking whether someone still wants the plan after it becomes possible.
