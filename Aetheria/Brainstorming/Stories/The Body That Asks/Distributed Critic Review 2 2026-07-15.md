## Critic One: Reader Experience And Braid Legibility

### New Blockers

#### The literal opening remains undecided

[[Braid#Reader Reveal Ladder]] says the reader begins with Harrowglass. [[Braid#The Manufactured Family]] calls the eighty-two-percent Meridian rehearsal the opening rehearsal and only afterward says the Calyx strand opens. The architecture must name Chapter One.

If Harrowglass opens, it needs an immediate contested task and a human orientation job beyond announcing the historical thesis. If Meridian opens, Harrowglass remains the first Calyx chapter but not the reader's first scene. Either can work; the current room claims both.

#### The opening and midpoint refusals blur together

Movement I says one component refuses to become an arm. At midpoint, Iri refuses a commanded movement and discovers constitutional transfer. Without a sharp distinction, the midpoint repeats the opening's apparent action.

Specify whether the eighty-two-percent failure is mechanical, imposed by Kinship, deliberately performed, or a refusal nobody honors. Also resolve “scored covert-assembly trial”: identify who observes the score, what remains hidden, and why the event does not disclose the household's secret.

#### Calyx has coordinates, not yet chapter engines

The C1/L6 table makes reverse state legible. It does not yet make the reverse line pleasurable fiction. Every Calyx chapter needs a present desire, obstacle, irreversible choice, POV, and adjacent Meridian scene whose emotional meaning changes because of the pairing. Otherwise the strand risks opening with a grand conclusion and continuing as explanatory subtraction.

#### The climax carries too much visible grammar

The occupied spindle, five local bodies, balance crown, propulsion, perception coordination, transmission, rescue triage, separation, legal challenge, security combat, Vey's override record, Vesper recognition, Necro disabling, worker rescue, escape, and broadcast all compete for reader attention.

Keep the constitutional richness in sideband state, but choose two or three visibly repeated handoffs the reader can learn. Pay off other cross-training before or after the central maneuver. Authority moving with work is the image; a complete office inventory is not.

### Major Findings

- Movement II still risks becoming a revelation conveyor. Ordinary academy rivalry, maintenance, bad meals, jokes, desire, and recurring secondary relationships must survive between wounds and disclosures.
- Six ordinary-school decision owners may exceed novella attachment bandwidth alongside five teens, five historical pilots, AFTER, Vey, and Vesper. Select two or three recurring emotional anchors; retain the others as lower-resolution social body.
- AFTER's final question is structurally repaired but dramatically pre-decided. At least one answer should contain hesitation or a condition rooted in AFTER's earlier theft of informed choice. Asking must cost AFTER something in the moment.
- The dormant handshakes remain potent but can read as resurrection-shaped sequel bait. Prepare them as surviving contemporaries and political aftermath, not recovered pieces of a mythic set.

### Protected Strengths

- Dramatic irony and separate reader/Meridian ladders.
- AFTER's progression from reflex to interlocutor, claimant, and survival interest.
- Iri's somatic no remaining distinct from Omi's ethical no.
- Vesper's famous movement becoming recognizable captivity.
- Institutional desirability, Vey's bounded evidence action, and authority moving with work.
- The comedy and maintenance-based tenderness contract.

## Critic Two: Character And Relationship Persistence

### New Blockers

#### Coordination-office ontology is split

[[Concordance#Concordance Authority]] defines crown as the temporary office owning whole-body balance and intended motion. [[Meridian/Characters]] and [[Braid]] additionally name propulsion crown, perception coordination, transmission, rescue, and separation offices, sometimes concurrently.

Before scene deltas, define whether:

- one crown moves between governing tasks;
- crown delegates bounded coordination offices while retaining balance;
- or each office can independently coordinate without becoming crown.

For each office establish owner, inputs, output, local-pilot boundary, transfer trigger, and return condition. The prose need not explain the whole contract, but the story room must stop using “crown” and “coordination office” interchangeably.

#### The Fivefold still lack private lives

The new directional injuries are excellent. Mara, Sol, Eda, Tern, and Ysra still lack non-project futures, appetites, voices, mundane rituals, and attachments outside Calyx. Harrowglass costs them offices and political power; it does not yet cost five recognizable people particular lives.

Give each one a private future or practice, one lived bond not reducible to project function, and one thing disassembly prevents or preserves.

#### Office exchange is assigned rather than learned

Every Meridian pilot receives one tidy unassigned competence and successfully uses it. This risks replacing Kinship's fixed aptitude chart with the author's rotated chart.

Require at least two rehearsal cycles:

- an early failed, refused, or withdrawn handoff that leaves relational and practical residue;
- a later partial success whose ceiling remains visible.

Pilots coordinate unfamiliar work without inheriting another person's body knowledge. The wrong cross-trained pilot should sometimes remain wrong.

### Major Findings

- AFTER's continuity needs a lived chain across Calyx, wipe, dormancy, reflex, contact, naming, omission, and repair. Clarify what “remembers Mara” means after vocabulary and episodic access are removed: somatic expectation, affective recognition, recovered record, or surviving episode.
- Give AFTER directional bonds, misreadings, and a choice at Harrowglass beyond agreeing to disassembly.
- Convert Senn/Tem, Senn/Iri, Omi/Tem, and Lio/Iri from thematic summaries into event chains: ordinary bond, misread act, injury, changed available action, incomplete repair.
- At least two characters should initially misdiagnose AFTER's omission and act harmfully. The current five appraisals are elegant and substantially correct, producing a symposium where a breach should produce error.
- Only Senn has a concrete post-escape life. Give Omi, Lio, Tem, and Iri specific careers, places, relationships, citizenship choices, crafts, or bodily practices they may gain or lose.
- Name the person harmed by Lio's report. Specify Tem's prior Seat, duration, and one relationship carried from the previous household.
- Lio and Tem should each make a different choice that changes what Vey can do; his response should preserve their asymmetrical histories.

### Protected Strengths

- Fivefold injuries now differ causally, especially crown return, liaison access, and parallel accounts.
- AFTER's omission has material stakes and repair through surrendered authority.
- Iri's costly refusal precedes any tactical reward.
- Vey's evidence action carries responsibility without absolution or system mastery.
- Senn's civilian repair-and-teaching future has the right specificity.

## Critic Three: Aetheria Themes And Institutional Materialism

### New Blockers

#### The external conflict has no lived people

The Continuance Directorate is materially plausible as an accession machine, and the Lantern forecast gives Kinship rational pressure. Both remain abstract. The Directorate appears only as the thing coming; the Compact appears mainly as the sponsor frightened by it.

Ground the conflict through at least:

- one person or community already living under Directorate accession;
- one Compact constituency that rejects Kinship without denying the threat;
- one viable but politically costly defense alternative Kinship displaced;
- one actor who benefits from the forecast's most alarming assumptions.

Otherwise the book may criticize custody inside Meridian while treating millions outside it as casualty inputs.

#### AFTER and Vesper need an explicit threshold contrast

AFTER is disputed under ship-mind custody because it can claim, ask, conceal, and refuse. Vesper's souls are classified as non-agentic residue because agency cannot reach the certified control plane. This is one of the story's sharpest Aetheria arguments, but the room does not yet state which legal, clinical, or technical test produces opposite recognition.

Define the admissibility threshold and show why it measures controllable expression rather than consciousness. AFTER can become conditionally legible by speaking; Vesper is made administratively dead by preventing speech. The classification should expose the institution's need, not resolve metaphysics.

### Major Findings

- The Directorate and Meridian deliberately rhyme as systems that provide continuity while removing refusal. Preserve the rhyme without flattening them into moral equivalence; their histories, violence, and available exits should differ.
- Acceleration is seeded in academy speech but not yet embodied through repeated deadlines, procurement decisions, or lost alternatives. Make urgency change ordinary choices before Vey presents the forecast.
- The authority-by-verb map is strong sideband machinery. The manuscript should follow a few consequential handoffs rather than tour the organization chart.
- Exposure still needs a material evidence path: which records can be authenticated together, who has standing to publish them, what remains classified, and why the broadcast cannot be dismissed or silently erased.
- Consent now matters when it costs performance as well as when it improves coherence. Protect that repair fiercely; it prevents Kinship from absorbing the story's ethics as a better engineering method.

### Protected Strengths

- No mastermind, no Vey kill switch, and no office possessing the whole harm.
- Executive exclusion as technically accurate bureaucratic language.
- Genuine education, medicine, careers, and defense pressure inside an exploitative institution.
- Workers and ordinary cadets disagreeing for material reasons.
- Exposure producing dispute rather than recognition.
