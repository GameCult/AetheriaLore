# Persona Baseline Review

## Initial Blockers

- AFTER's first-Concordance baseline leaked mature identity, legal fears, Meridian-era concepts, and future actions backward through lived chronology.
- Iri and Tem had no concrete directional relationship.
- Cadet baselines lacked present pressure, active memories, explicit boundaries, and credible institutional exits.
- Several Personas encoded future irreversible decisions as currently available actions.
- Vey's access and embodiment were broader or more specific than the evidence allowed.
- Aggregate care authority collapsed clinics, insurers, scholarships, and academy certification into one institution.

## Repairs

- AFTER r0 now contains only pre-verbal protective anticipation, local strain, preference, and aversion. Continuous-person rules are labeled coordinator invariants.
- Iri and Tem now have a concrete provenance conflict, repair injury, and bounded reconciliation path.
- Each cadet baseline carries current pressure, active memories, boundary, and real exits that preserve something desirable.
- Future outcomes were removed from baseline candidate actions.
- Vey's access is limited to ordinary academy and welfare surfaces within clearance; embodiment remains unspecified.
- Medical, insurance, and scholarship authorities are separated from Meridian Academy.

## Verification

Independent character, continuity, and cast-load rechecks returned clean after repair.
