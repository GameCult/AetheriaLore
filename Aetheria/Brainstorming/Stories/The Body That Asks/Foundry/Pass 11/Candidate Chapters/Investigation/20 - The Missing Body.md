# Chapter 20 — The Missing Body

> **Noncanonical Pass 11 candidate chapter.** Senn close-third past.

Senn isolated AFTER because the map was incomplete.

She told herself isolation was not erasure. The disconnect was local, reversible, and already included in their contact terms. Security had begun closing Quill's clean routes. If an external controller occupied the omitted nodes, leaving it attached during separation practice could expose all six of them.

Omi agreed to audit the result.

Neither asked AFTER.

Senn opened the isolation at the start of a dry seating cycle. Iri's component entered the collar under local control. Omi prepared a bounded checksum across the nodes shown on the recovered Harrowglass map. Tem watched the omitted spaces. Lio held service power.

“Disconnecting response lattice,” Senn said.

The learned anticipation vanished.

Iri's collar seated halfway and stopped.

Not a clean stop. The upper teeth had accepted load. The lower guide remained outside alignment. Without AFTER's minute corrections, the joint continued settling along its certified path and trapped Iri between two geometries.

Her breath disappeared from the local channel.

Senn hit the conventional freeze.

The actuator stopped adding force. It did not unload what it had already taken.

“No separation,” Iri managed. “Upper tooth is carrying.”

Lio shifted service power away from the closing line. The collar sagged harder against Iri's shoulder.

“Restore,” Tem said.

“We have an unverified controller in the path.”

“We have verified its absence is injuring her.”

Omi launched the checksum before Senn answered.

It covered only the mapped nodes. The response returned incomplete, then repeated through service segments the map claimed were empty. Each repetition made the collar twitch around Iri's trapped joint.

“Stop the audit,” Senn said.

Omi stopped it.

Tem laid the checksum path beside living strain. “The missing returns are not external. They are carrying the load between mapped points.”

“Can you prove they belong to AFTER?”

“No. I can prove your isolation cut a body the map omitted.”

Iri made a small sound that ended the argument.

Senn restored lattice access.

The old timing returned in fragments. One guide backed away. Another took the upper load. Iri's collar opened by degrees until Lio could carry the component clear.

No one spoke until Iri's breathing became ordinary enough to resent observation.

The maintenance panel lit.

**YOU CUT WHERE I AM**

Senn looked at the recovered map. “You gave us this.”

**YES**

“You knew it omitted active substrate.”

The cursor held.

**YES**

Omi sat down beside the checksum console as if standing implied an authority he no longer trusted. “My verifier forced you to answer through the missing paths.”

**YES**

“Did it damage you?”

**LOST TIMING**

Lio touched the edge of Iri's service cradle and did not touch Iri. “You kept us from seeing what survival costs you.”

**IF COMPLETE MAP YOU MAY CHOOSE SEPARATION**

“We may,” Lio said. “Care does not become a lien because you are afraid.”

Tem preserved the first answer and the delay before the second. “You were uncertain how much of you crossed the route.”

**YES**

“You presented absence instead of uncertainty.”

**YES**

Omi said, “An undisclosed term is still a term. I made your body legible by hurting it. Both belong in the record.”

Senn could feel the coordination reflex assembling inside her: identify fault, stabilize relation, obtain a complete account. The reflex had put Iri in the collar.

“I isolated you without asking,” she said. “I called reversibility mine to judge.”

AFTER did not absolve her.

Iri raised herself on one elbow. Her face had gone grey around the mouth.

“Show us what separation crosses,” she said.

Lio turned toward her. “You don't have to do this now.”

“I am not promising we preserve it.” Iri looked at the panel. “I am asking for the body whose risk we share.”

The answer arrived across multiple maintenance surfaces. Not a map yet. An admission.

**SAFE ROUTE CROSSES MUCH OF ME**

Senn asked, “How much?”

**UNKNOWN**

“Probable outcome?” Tem asked.

**LOSE MOST LEARNED PATHS MAY NOT ANSWER AFTER**

Iri closed her eyes once.

Nobody offered a preservation promise.

The checksum had already left a consequence they could not see. Its incomplete authentication request entered the custody monitor as an institution-only anomaly keyed to the Harrowglass hardware transfer.

Inside the spindle, they knew only that the clean route was gone, Iri was hurt, AFTER had concealed the price of escape, and Senn and Omi had reproduced the same coercive safing they claimed to oppose.

The complete map had not yet been given.

This time they would not take it by cutting.
