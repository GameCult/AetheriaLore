# Chapter 19 — Harrowglass Recorder

> **Noncanonical Pass 11 candidate chapter.** Omi close-third past.

AFTER remembered where Harrowglass had hidden the recorder.

It did not remember the access route intact.

The location arrived as bodily instruction: rotate Iri's component to an obsolete service attitude, warm a dead coupling, ask the spindle for a shield diagnostic it had not performed in centuries. Quill recognized the resulting cavity as an assembly recorder well. Custody recognized it as sealed historical hardware and began closing her access before she finished saying so.

“How long?” Omi asked.

“Nine minutes until the transfer lock reaches this controller.”

“Damage if we open it?”

“To the recorder, unknown. To my employment, legible.”

Omi authorized the cost he owned. “Open it.”

Quill made him repeat the request on her maintenance record.

The well had fused around its shield canister. Iri held the component at an angle that made her injured shoulder tremble. Lio carried the service load without pretending not to notice. Senn took no crown; she called intervals and let Quill own the extraction.

The canister emerged with six seconds remaining.

Quill seated it in a physical reader isolated from Meridian's network. The transfer lock arrived and revoked her historical-hardware credential. It could not revoke the object already resting in her hands.

“Assembly is authentic,” she said. “Shield, seals, fracture pattern. Harrowglass-period service manufacture.”

“Contents?” Tem asked.

“Your problem.”

The recorder contained three damaged reconstructions.

Tem refused to merge them. One preserved component telemetry. One preserved office transfers. One preserved the shielded service blister where Mara had waited with a cup of dock tea while the body came apart.

Omi had expected catastrophe because Meridian's memorial language had trained expectation into him. Instead he saw five pilots preparing their own disappearance with the competence of people repairing something dangerous.

Ysra owned separation. Mara retained crown only for balance. Sol returned propulsion decisions the moment their work ended. Eda opened five dispersal routes and did not choose who took which one. Tern kept incompatible political accounts beside the same physical telemetry.

AFTER answered through the old assembly board.

Its choice appeared in all three records: distributed loss over intact capture.

The pilots asked again after the first route failed.

AFTER chose again.

“They knew this would damage it,” Lio said.

“They knew the map they had,” Tem replied. “That is not the same as knowing every loss.”

The recorder proved chosen disassembly, refusal of permanent guardianship, transfer of crown, bounded delegation, and repeated assent. It did not prove every political motive that led to Harrowglass. It did not make the Fivefold saints. It did not make the five cadets their return.

Omi watched Mara wait before closing one coupling.

History had remembered command because waiting left poor statuary.

“Our escape plan preserves the Fist,” Senn said.

“That is the wrong object,” Omi answered.

They rewrote it around separability.

Local pilots retained component expertise. Senn taught Lio the narrow balance office she had already held by accident. Lio taught Tem propulsion-vector commitment without giving him drive reflexes. Omi learned separation sequencing from Iri and refused her first attempt to call pain merely a parameter. Each exchange named input, interval, return, and the work nobody else received.

They did not become interchangeable. They became harder to trap inside assigned Seats.

Tem reconstructed the Harrowglass embodiment map while they practiced. The recorder's damage produced ordinary holes. Other absences were too orderly.

Omi overlaid Quill's living-strain trace from the occupied spindle.

Where the gantry had carried AFTER's response most strongly, the historical map showed empty service substrate. Where the hidden trial's catch had crossed Iri's collar, another node vanished from the recovered geometry.

“Those are systematic,” he said.

AFTER did not answer.

The silence had standing now.

It also had a shape.
