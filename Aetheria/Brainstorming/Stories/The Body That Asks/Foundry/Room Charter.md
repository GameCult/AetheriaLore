# Room Charter

## Story Promise

Meridian tries to manufacture the power of a family whose members can refuse one another and remain together. It can assign the house, Seats, machines, medical care, prestige, and future. It cannot manufacture the freedom to remain.

Five sophomores are shoved into one prestigious Finalist household with the distributed machine person already living in their inherited hardware. Their intimacy develops through school pressure, technical work, betrayal, attraction, care, and practiced exchange of authority. The historical Calyx strand shows readers the voluntary disassembly Meridian remembers as catastrophic instability. At Concordance the strands meet; the young household then moves beyond the institution's inherited account.

## Frame

- Calyx lived chronology runs from first Concordance toward Harrowglass, approximately 3250–3260.
- Six Calyx interludes are presented backward from Harrowglass toward first Concordance.
- Meridian runs forward from sophomore selection, approximately 3350–3360.
- Both date ranges remain provisional against wider chronology.
- The Unseating is bounded to the deep-Elysium **Harrowglass Route Sphere**, a network large enough to contain eleven governments, successor states, and refugees without becoming a civilization-wide regime change.
- Close third, past tense governs both timelines. Historical coordinates are explicit; tense tricks do not simulate reversal.
- The inherited twenty-two-chapter spine is provisional architecture. Length follows the story.

## Invariants

- Concordance coordinates distinct persons; it does not merge them into one agency.
- A local body may refuse. The combined body may route around refusal but may not overwrite it.
- Crown is a temporary whole-body coordination office, not a permanent identity or sovereign.
- Coordination offices transfer through practiced competence; component expertise remains local.
- AFTER is one continuous emergent embodied ship-self, not an upload, spirit, reincarnation, replaceable backup, or Calyx pilot.
- Vesper's bound soul-patterns may retain cognition or agency while executive exclusion prevents them from controlling Necro. The story does not claim no agency remains.
- Workers at Palisade retain political agency, local stop authority, evidence custody, and ownership of the escape route.
- Care, competence, and sincere moral reasoning do not acquit the institutions that organize them into exploitation.

## Ending Contract

The six escape in their five damaged component Figures, with AFTER still distributed through surviving hardware. Worker tenders screen the anti-sunward route but do not become permanent custody or a replacement home. The immediate problem of shelter remains materially open.

The ending dramatizes one live reply: an emergent machine-person answering AFTER's personhood claim. A living pilot collective and a political project produce two additional authenticated signals that remain latent rather than becoming three closing introductions. AFTER chooses how to answer the first relationship after obeying Morrow Quay's refusal.

Kinship survives. Palisade closes. Vey pays without becoming innocent. The Plastics and Lifers remain competent and implicated. Exposure creates interlocutors and claims, not settlement.

## Process Policy

Planning earns survival only by changing a drafting decision or reducing a named uncertainty. Character-local state advances through accepted deltas from explicit parent revisions. The coordinator alone integrates canonical state and prose. Independent manuscript readers receive no planning packet. Story-local inventions remain candidates for later promotion rather than silently entering setting canon.
