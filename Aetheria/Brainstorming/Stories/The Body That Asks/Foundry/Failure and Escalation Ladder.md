# Failure and Escalation Ladder

This ladder tracks four separate authorities:

- **reader knowledge** begins with chosen Harrowglass disassembly and accumulates Calyx's political context in reverse presentation order;
- **Meridian knowledge** begins with the academy account and advances only through experience, testimony, physical evidence, and authenticated joins;
- **Calyx lived knowledge** advances from first Concordance to Harrowglass even while presentation runs backward;
- **institutional knowledge** remains partitioned. Kinship sees downstream anomalies from the hidden trial, not Iri's refusal or the transfer event itself.

The governing invariant is simple: a rung must change at least two of trust, bodily access, time, evidence custody, professional temptation, available exit, or institutional countermeasure. A disclosure that changes only knowledge belongs inside the action that makes it costly.

## Reveal Joins And Boundaries

### Personal, systemic, and cohort evidence

Lio's receipt, Tem's selection history, and the recurring Seats are three turns:

1. Lio's concealed receipt makes personal betrayal and unintended consequence legible.
2. Tem's obsolete code joins clinic, formation, and candidate handling into one selection act.
3. Public placement gaps and the candidate index reveal the cohort-scale split-and-recombine pattern.

Each turn must alter behavior before the next arrives.

### Vesper and Necro

Students obtain only custody safety certification and interface-review fragments: executive exclusion, involuntary pattern activation, pain-like conflict, and fixed separation tolerance. Vey cannot see these through academy welfare authority; Quill's physical hold and component-custody review create the bounded access path. Combat later proves the machine works and reveals its tactical architecture.

### Harrowglass technical and political proof

The shielded recorder proves the act and constitutional capacities. Public petitions, assembly minutes, Sol's agreements, traffic records, and Eda's deletion manifest prove the pressure toward permanent guardianship. Eda's destroyed private archive remains destroyed.

### AFTER continuity

AFTER may identify itself before the recorder is recovered. At that stage continuity is a credible claim supported by repeated timing, distributed challenge history, aversion, and preference. The recorder strengthens the historical claim. Neither evidence source creates personhood, and neither grants the Fivefold control over the present six.

## Calyx State Invariant

The presented order is C1/L6, C2/L5, C3/L4, C4/L3, C5/L2, C6/L1. Character state advances only L1 to L6.

- L1 contains local experiment, failure history, relationships, and pre-verbal protective timing.
- L2 adds repeated anticipation, not named or speaking AFTER.
- L3 adds working crown/refusal and role hardening, not mature doctrine.
- L4 adds AFTER's answers and cross-assembly memory, not Harrowglass standing.
- L5 adds constitutional dependence, injured trust, and consideration of an ending, not its settled execution.
- L6 contains recognized AFTER, full lived history, and chosen disassembly.

No interlude may inherit knowledge merely because the reader encountered a later lived state earlier.

## Exposition Hazards

- Vesper records must cost component access and alter escape constraints.
- Harrowglass recovery must cost maintenance access and immediately change training.
- Vey's forecast must arrive as five actionable futures.
- Halcyon's acquisition must change field orders, custody, and worker choice in real time.
- Aftermath evidence must be distributed among worker claims, privacy, medical need, pursuit, and shelter refusal.

A nontechnical reader should be able to summarize the causal spine: Meridian chooses five teenagers for a body designed to obey; they discover the body works by preserving distinct people; the institution tries to turn that capacity into governable force; they use it to save people without owning them, then survive the weapon made from agency's exclusion by refusing the answer it predicts.
