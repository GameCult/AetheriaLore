# Premise Auditions

The locked plot outcomes remain fixed. These auditions test which attractive practice organizes the academy movement before Bright Ledger.

## 1. Household Charter

**Thesis:** custody becomes legitimate when the people inside it co-author local rules.

The five negotiate privacy, refusal, reporting, repair, debrief ownership, and voluntary practice. The charter materially improves safety and trust. Its success misleads because the household owns local procedure but not selection, hardware, medicine, derived data, recomposition, or exit. Kinship treats every revision as higher-resolution measurement.

**Relational consequence:** Senn and Omi build the instrument they later disagree about; Senn violates a rule by treating care as assent; Omi uses a legitimate safety clause to injure Tem; AFTER forces the question of who counts as a charter member.

**Uncertainty:** better local law may repeatedly save them even though it cannot legitimate the owner outside the room.

## 2. Professional Futures Court Them

**Thesis:** necessary burdens become ethical when competent adults name the cost and let students choose which profession will carry it.

Plastics teach access, decisive threat geometry, classified professionalism, and pain performance. Lifers teach continuity, triage, rescue discipline, and carrying bad settlements without lying. Both make the sophomores better and offer real adult futures. Individual competence rises while each path fixes a different permanent office and splits the household.

**Relational consequence:** Vara's effective care complicates Lio and Iri; Anik's respect tempts Iri; Rhea remains outside remedial privilege while outperforming them; Omi and Lio fight over whether honored specialization is another creditor; later containment hurts because the mentors remain competent adults.

**Uncertainty:** the professions may be valid lives made coercive by custody rather than false doctrines.

## 3. Fair Competition

**Thesis:** public measures can constrain secret privilege when ordinary cadets outperform Finalists.

Rhea's intact Fist is genuinely better at ordinary formation. Competition forces practice, produces cafeteria pleasure and status injury, and gives Senn a peer standard no adult owns. Kinship retains the incomparable category—only Finalists receive the recovered hardware and live deployment—while using public rivalry to produce stress and evidence.

**Relational consequence:** Senn/Rhea carries attraction, humiliation, and exact testimony; Finalist priority consumes peers' bay time and paid labor; Lio acquires an audience worth losing.

**Uncertainty:** fair measures reveal competence while failing to grant jurisdiction over the prize.

## 4. Authorized Safety Inquiry

**Thesis:** a caring institution can correct itself when evidence reaches an accountable adult.

Vey's inquiry turns the public failure into safer doctrine, bounded access, and genuine accommodations. Every lawful query also emits metadata. Access produces discovery while teaching Kinship where to cut. Vey helps, censors, and traces without needing duplicity or omniscience.

**Relational consequence:** Lio's faith receives evidence before her report injures Tem; Omi cannot dismiss the inquiry as theater; Iri must distinguish useful accommodation from ownership.

**Uncertainty:** Vey can recognize surveillance without knowing which office uses it.

## 5. Explicit Protocol

**Thesis:** coercion is an interface defect; precise requests, limits, and abort paths can make formation safe.

The protocol prevents real harm and raises scores. Kinship praises Senn for converting dissent into command reliability. The protocol still cannot classify involuntary lock, pre-verbal aversion, or honest inability to nominate one account. In the hidden trial, the body survives because relation continues after the procedure exhausts its answer.

**Relational consequence:** Senn and Omi become effective collaborators before his language becomes a compliance surface; Iri trusts behavior more than form; Tem challenges the demand for complete articulation.

**Uncertainty:** the protocol may create conditions for trust while remaining insufficient to own it.

## 6. Useful Ghost

**Thesis:** treating unexplained adaptive control as a technical collaborator is safer and more humane than overwriting it.

Fault-dialogue discipline preserves anomalies and yields better recovery paths. Usefulness precedes speech, so respectful maintenance still defines preservation through service. The hidden trial proves AFTER can stabilize a route the pilots choose but cannot authorize them.

**Relational consequence:** Iri treats protective behavior as a claim; Senn as manageable responsibility; Omi as unreviewed authority; Lio as reciprocal care; Tem refuses all singular accounts.

**Uncertainty:** obligations under uncertainty precede any objective consciousness threshold.

## 7. Interchangeable Offices

**Thesis:** fixed specialization is the violence; universal cross-training prevents permanent Seats.

Rotation improves adaptability and feels liberating. The failure is confusing transferable authority with fungible bodies. The hidden trial abandons the planned egalitarian roster when present physical competence demands a different temporary holder.

**Relational consequence:** Lio learns temporary authority need not become anatomy; Iri forces Senn to distinguish learning care from owning it; Omi accepts unequal temporary authority without surrendering equality.

**Uncertainty:** cross-training expands freedom until it begins denying embodied difference.

## Selection

**Primary character engine:** Professional Futures Court Them. Vara and Anik concentrate the academy's two prestigious adult answers and make later containment relationally expensive.

**Household counter-practice:** a small Household Charter expressed through doors, meals, schedules, treatment, debrief ownership, and flight—not policy-seminar dialogue.

**Institutional route:** Authorized Safety Inquiry. It owns access, discovery, leak metadata, and Vey's sincere complicity.

**Public social field:** Fair Competition. Rhea's ordinary Fist owns visible competence and the cost of Finalist priority.

**Bounded pressures:** Explicit Protocol supplies the household's flight grammar; Useful Ghost supplies early AFTER relationship; Interchangeable Offices contributes one failed cross-training assumption. They do not become equal plot engines.

This selection changes the drafting decision: the academy movement is organized around attractive professional futures, not around conspiracy investigation alone. Every investigation turn must occur inside school life, mentorship, household governance, rivalry, medicine, or machine work that the reader would miss after escape.
