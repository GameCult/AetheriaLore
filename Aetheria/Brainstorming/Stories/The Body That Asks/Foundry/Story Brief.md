# Story Brief

## Identity

- **Working title:** *The Body That Asks*
- **Length:** As long as the completed dramatic work requires; no word-count target or ceiling.
- **Genre and tone:** Political mecha-academy thriller, institutional science fiction, and found-family escape story; intimate, technically grounded, politically alert, and funny where prestige makes ordinary need ridiculous.
- **Audience:** Adult science-fiction readers without required Aetheria knowledge.
- **POV and tense:** Close third, past tense. Meridian advances in story time. Six short Calyx interludes present one earlier history in reverse order until the lines meet at first Concordance.
- **Setting:** Deep Post-Elysium; Calyx approximately 3250–3260 and Meridian approximately 3350–3360.
- **Final artifact:** `Aetheria/Fiction/The Body That Asks.md`.

## Promise

A prestigious Daedal flight academy assigns five sophomores to an experimental household intended to reproduce a legendary form of coordinated embodiment. The supposedly blank training hardware already contains AFTER, the surviving learned personhood of the first Concord Figure. The six discover one another inside an institution that provides real care while treating attachment as strategic infrastructure, then are deployed with the academy's two older Finalist cadres into a corporate labor war disguised as public-health rescue.

The emotional destination is a family whose belonging survives refusal, betrayal, mutilation, and departure. Every gain in impossible bodily coordination arises from a specific person becoming safer to refuse, disagree with, disclose to, or return authority to. Meridian's care remains real, so leaving costs love rather than merely exposing fraud.

The repeated pleasures are institutional procedure exposing moral assumptions, technical teamwork becoming intimacy, school life under absurd prestige pressure, and giant-machine action whose maneuvers arise from changes in trust and authority.

## Locked Premise

- **Protagonist:** Senn, Omi, Lio, Tem, and Iri, with AFTER becoming the sixth principal person.
- **Central engine:** each gain in coordination increases institutional value and custody pressure; each discovery makes escape more necessary while making mutual trust harder.
- **Ending class:** hard-won escape and provisional family; emotionally closed on chosen continued relationship, materially incomplete, and politically open.
- **Non-negotiable outcomes:** first paired Concordance; voluntary Calyx disassembly; AFTER's omission and repair; Operation Bright Ledger; full Concordance honoring worker and pilot refusal; AFTER's specific mutilation; immediate Necro pursuit; practiced office exchange; worker-owned evidence and escape path; dormant-handshake ending.
- **Prohibited resolutions:** permanent fusion, restored ancient guardianship, Vey as mastermind, benevolent-force acquittal, conquest, a dead Vesper mind controlling Necro, refusal justified only by improved performance, or exposure instantly defeating the institution.

## POV And Persistence

The five Meridian cadets form the ordinary forward POV pool. Vey, Rhea, workers, and older cadres remain observed unless later private causality proves indispensable. AFTER receives one bounded close-third section after it can formulate personhood, including the final reply. Calyx concentrates on Mara and Ysra; Sol, Eda, and Tern remain outside POV unless a later architecture pass proves an unwitnessed decisive act requires one.

Reverse presentation never reverses character causality. Calyx state replays from first Concordance toward Harrowglass in lived order. AFTER remains one continuous person across Calyx, disassembly, dormancy, Meridian contact, omission, repair, Palisade, and escape.

## Reader Contract

- By ten percent, the reader should enjoy the forced household and want to know what caught Iri.
- By twenty-five percent, the five should feel individually memorable and technically interdependent; AFTER should feel like a presence before becoming an explanation.
- By fifty percent, the reader should want all six to escape while understanding why accepting Meridian's bargain remains tempting.
- Vocabulary is learned through desire, embarrassment, injury, work, and consequence rather than explanatory tours.
- The opening fails if it begins as a lore history, treats selection as generic chosen-one triumph, or delays the social violence of assigned family behind spectacle.

## Process

The run uses the full fourteen-pass Foundry process with autonomous continuation authorized. Each coherent pass is committed and pushed without unrelated worktree changes. Movement drafts receive continuity, causality, character/social-agency, prose, and blind manuscript-only review. No manuscript or planning-volume quota applies.
