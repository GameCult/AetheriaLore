## Revision IDs

Accepted deltas use character-local monotonic revisions:

```text
cc.persona_delta_stream gamecult.persona_delta.v0

delta y01
parent: r0
branch: revision-01-main
event: CH02_FINALIST_SELECTION
coordinate: A0; Chapter 2
...

delta y02
parent: y01
...
```

This is the single materialized stream syntax for Revision 01. A delta header names only the child; its `parent:` field owns ancestry. Narrative-arrow headers and alternate stream schemas are not accepted replay surfaces.

Revision numbers do not imply every chapter changes every character. A character receives a delta only when an observable event materially changes memory, belief, goal, activated disposition, bodily/cognitive load, refusal, candidate action, directional bond, or status read.

## Scene Acceptance Path

```text
valid parent Persona revision
+ scene-local world, academic, institutional, and material state
+ explicit POV and perception boundary
  -> projected character context
  -> drafted or modeled action
  -> observable event
  -> separate participant-local appraisals
  -> proposed deltas
  -> coordinator adjudication
  -> accepted/repaired deltas
  -> next character-local revisions
```

Room-level interpretation cannot enter a teen's Persona merely because it is thematically correct. A character may acquire a changed action, aversion, memory, or relationship pressure before acquiring language for it.

## Adolescent Appraisal Boundary

For Senn, Omi, Lio, Tem, and Iri, every proposed delta distinguishes:

- **observable fact:** what the character actually witnessed or learned;
- **immediate appraisal:** their age-, history-, and domain-bounded interpretation;
- **coordinator reading:** latent significance unavailable to the character;
- **prose availability:** what may appear as thought, speech, gesture, joke, silence, error, or action;
- **later articulation gate:** what evidence and elapsed time would permit a more mature formulation.

A correct coordinator reading is not a memory or belief operation. It remains outside the Persona until earned.

## Invalidation Rules

A scene reorder or material rewrite invalidates every descendant delta whose evidence, parent, coordinate, or participant appraisal changes. Replay begins at the last valid parent.

The following always require replay:

- changing a chapter's POV;
- moving a discovery across a term;
- changing who witnesses a refusal, injury, disclosure, or institutional action;
- changing an academic outcome that affects status, access, or relationship;
- moving a Calyx scene in lived chronology;
- changing AFTER's available substrate or memory;
- joining or splitting a scene so reaction time changes.

## Acceptance Checks

- Every accepted delta cites a Chapter Blueprint event and an observable prose or modeled beat.
- Every child names a valid character-local parent on `revision-01-main`.
- Rejected and deferred operations have no descendants.
- No teen acquires room-level psychological or political language without an articulation gate.
- No formal POV pair creates cross-time knowledge.
- Injuries, access loss, grades, projects, and relationship residue survive term boundaries.
- Endpoint state can be replayed from r0 without consulting the baseline manuscript's old delta order.
