## Global Firewalls

### Knowledge

- The ledger may know the whole history; each POV knows only what the lived parent state, body, memory, and named channels supply.
- No Calyx member knows Meridian, the Final Fist program, later Seat assignees, Vesper, Palisade, or the institutional use of the recovered record.
- No early-lived scene borrows doctrine earned later. “Bounded office,” executive exclusion, personhood, guardianship, and chosen dispersal emerge through work, injury, appropriation, and repetition.
- A paired Meridian chapter receives no knowledge from the historical opener. Adjacency creates reader comparison, not transmission.
- AFTER's knowledge begins as local timing and strain. Addressability, differentiation, a name, custody risk, and dispersal preference must each be acquired on this chain.

### No reincarnation

- Surname and Seat correspondences are Meridian's imposed taxonomy. They are not inherited souls, memories, skills, temperaments, debts, magical affinities, or natural offices.
- Historical and Meridian partners share no signature metaphor, verbal tic, appetite, relationship pattern, or conclusion merely to advertise the comparison.
- Every pair contains disanalogy: Mara returns authority while Senn initially seeks it; Sol's credibility already governs others while Omi discovers dissent as status; Eda seeks public work while Lio seeks recognition; Tern harms through synthesis while Tem often harms through opacity; Ysra has adult clinical jurisdiction while Iri performs a concrete adolescent no.
- AFTER is the only continuous person across eras. It contains no Calyx soul, creates no copies, and transmits no historical personality into the cadets. Dormancy and mutilation subtract capacity from the same distributed person.

In lived order, accumulated shorthand, scars, jokes, and political concepts increase. In presentation order they contract: `C1/L10` shows the oldest household with compressed speech; `C10/L1` reveals younger colleagues overexplaining the apparatus, missing one another's jokes, and naming only immediate work.
