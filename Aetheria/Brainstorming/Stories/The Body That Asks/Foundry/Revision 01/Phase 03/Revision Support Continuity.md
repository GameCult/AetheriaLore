## Supporting-State Rule

- A support person enters a later scene from the last named handle above, not from a generic baseline.
- Support figures may misunderstand the protagonists. Their appraisal does not become coordinator truth.
- Their projects, shifts, grades, Fists, visits, and family exposure continue when the protagonists leave the room.
- A later scene may mint a new child only for an observed transaction. Summary claims such as “they grew closer” do not create a state edge.
