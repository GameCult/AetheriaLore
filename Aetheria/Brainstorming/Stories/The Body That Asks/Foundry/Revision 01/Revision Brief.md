## Operator Goal

Rewrite the novel to spend substantially more time inhabiting the world of a found family of teenagers at a magical super-soldier academy. Their sophomore year begins when Finalist selection thrusts them into public prestige, domestic proximity, bodily danger, and institutional custody. The year should feel like a saga rather than a sequence of plot appointments.

School life must carry independent value: curriculum, repeated training, rankings, meals, jokes, injuries, holidays, romance, family contact, rivalries, ordinary classmates, professional temptation, and the pleasure of becoming excellent. Meridian must become a home worth grieving before escape becomes necessary.

The five must sound and miscommunicate like teenagers. They are unusually skilled and can be exact inside trained technical domains, but they do not possess the story room's psychological, political, or constitutional vocabulary. Emotional insight arrives through jokes, status play, silence, avoidance, bad accusations, physical choices, borrowed adult phrases, and belated partial admissions. They may practice an ethic before they can explain it.

Every newly visible layer of political machinery must already have been operating before discovery and must continue altering access, medicine, reputation, employment, relationships, and institutional tactics afterward. Mysteries overlap without collapsing into one clue chain.

The reverse Calyx story remains active across the entire school year. The hidden No That Moves reveals accidental constitutional fragments, not full Concordance. The braid resolves only when sustained Concordance at Bright Ledger frees the five and AFTER from the institutional form history assigned them. Necro and escape occur after the final Calyx chapter, in formally unbraided time.

## Artistic Promise

Five uneven teenagers become a family through repeated life together, not destiny or assigned roles. The institution can manufacture proximity, prestige, and dependence; it cannot manufacture the freedom that makes their continued relationship meaningful.

The book should deliver both the pleasures of magical school and the political cost of discovering what that pleasure is built to produce. Care, cruelty, conformity, and brilliance remain orthogonal to institutional function. No individual revelation or villain explains the whole machine.

## Protected Identity

- Harrowglass opens the novel and gives readers dramatic irony about chosen disassembly.
- Omi and Lio share ward-sibling history and distinct bonds with Vey; the other three do not.
- Exactly three Finalist cadres coexist: sophomore protagonists, junior Plastics, senior Lifers.
- The Plastics and Lifers remain equally lionized, equally effective, and orthogonally right and wrong.
- AFTER is one continuous sixth person across Calyx, dispersal, dormancy, Meridian, omission, repair, Bright Ledger, and escape.
- Concordance preserves local refusal, temporary and transferable crown, bounded delegated offices, and local bodily expertise.
- Bright Ledger remains a cleanly briefed, dirty corporate counterinsurgency whose objective becomes destructive and pointless without making its immediate dangers unreal.
- Worker actors retain independent agency and dispute the meaning of the cadets' intervention.
- AFTER saves the operation at catastrophic personal cost.
- Three separately lawful receipts trigger the preauthorized Necro contingency immediately after Concordance.
- Vey loves his wards, participates in Kinship, lacks Necro clearance, and preserves the override chain without becoming either mastermind or savior.
- Escape, exposure, continued personhood, and provisional shelter constitute victory. Exposure does not dissolve Kinship, corporate power, or the invasion threat.
- No slogan, reincarnation, permanent fusion, restored ancient guardianship, or benevolent-force acquittal.
- No shared therapy-speech or precocious essay voice. Technical fluency does not confer mature self-knowledge.

## Accepted Structural Scope

The revision may replace the chapter count, movement allocation, scene order, time coordinates, and most connective prose. It may split, relocate, or substantially rewrite every existing chapter. Existing passages survive only when they serve the lived year and preserve correct prerequisites.

The revision may add school rituals, courses, competitions, leave, holidays, peers, staff, family encounters, recurring injuries, career tracks, and institutional procedures. New material must deepen character life, political causality, or both; elapsed days alone do not earn prose.

There is no word-count target or ceiling.

## Evidence Limitations

The initiating reader diagnosis comes from the operator after publication: the novel traverses an alleged school year in 32,000 words and does not make that duration felt. This is decisive structural evidence, but not a full cold-reader report. Phase 1 must include at least one manuscript-only reader who is not shown this brief.
