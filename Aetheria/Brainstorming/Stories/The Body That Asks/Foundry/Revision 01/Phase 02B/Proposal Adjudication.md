## Authority

This file adjudicates all substantive Phase 2A proposals. Accepted instructions govern the [[Chapter Blueprint]]. Raw proposal language remains advisory; writers receive only the blueprint and bounded dossiers.

## Global Rulings

### Accept

- Calendar owns elapsed time.
- Ten Calyx/Meridian pairs use the exact POV correspondences in [[../Phase 02/POV Pairing Ledger|POV Pairing Ledger]].
- Every chapter has one explicit POV.
- Calyx opens each pair; the Meridian counterpart immediately follows.
- The five are technically fluent adolescents with incomplete self-knowledge, not psychology majors or coordinator mouthpieces.
- Ordinary academics, clubs, peers, grades, and projects operate independently of Finalist training and the mystery.
- Palisade-specific knowledge remains quarantined until Bright Ledger assignment.
- The No That Moves is incomplete constitutional evidence.
- Full Concordance and the braid merge occur only at Bright Ledger.
- Familiar constitutional procedure contracts; full terms return only when new, violated, changed, or scaled.
- AFTER remains physically bounded, socially uneven, and continuous.
- Existing injuries, access losses, relationship wounds, careers, and institutional clocks persist across terms.

### Reject

- Any proposal or baseline implication that the strict first twelve chapters remain the full braid.
- Any Palisade, Serein, Halcyon, worker, lot, or plant-geometry breadcrumb before assignment.
- Any generic year montage used as proof of relationship or recovery.
- Any scene whose only new work is another complete assent/provenance/non-absolution liturgy.
- Any teen dialogue that states a correct room-level psychological or institutional synthesis merely because the sideband contains it.
- Any chapter count treated as a target rather than the current transaction boundary.
