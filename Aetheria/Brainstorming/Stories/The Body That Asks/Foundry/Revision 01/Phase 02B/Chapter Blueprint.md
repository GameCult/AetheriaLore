# Chapter Blueprint

This is a transaction map, not a length quota. Sixty-nine provisional chapters keep distinct POV, relationship, academic, institutional, and operational work from collapsing into summaries. Draft evidence may split or join standalone Meridian chapters. Calyx/Meridian pair adjacency is fixed.

Every chapter packet must name the POV's entrance Persona revision, available knowledge, primary desire, technical competence, emotional blind spot, observable transaction, participant-local deltas, mystery half-life state, academic or material clock, protected language, and exit pressure.
