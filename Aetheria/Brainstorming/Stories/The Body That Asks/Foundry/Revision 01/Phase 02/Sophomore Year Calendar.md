# Sophomore Year Calendar

## Coordinate System

Meridian's academic year uses story-local coordinates until wider calendar canon supplies month names:

- `A0` — return and selection week;
- `A1–A5` — autumn instruction, competition, failure, inquiry, and leave planning;
- `W1–W4` — midwinter exams, residence interval, and household consolidation;
- `S1–S6` — spring specialization, constitutional accident, investigation, rupture, repair, and qualification;
- `F1–F3` — finals, placement rituals, and Bright Ledger departure;
- `B1–B3` — Bright Ledger operation and Concordance;
- `U1–U3` — unbraided Necro, escape, and temporary infrastructure.

The prose need not print every coordinate. It must make the intervals reconstructable through recurring courses, assessments, leave, injuries, rankings, changing light and station use, ceremonies, and material consequences.
