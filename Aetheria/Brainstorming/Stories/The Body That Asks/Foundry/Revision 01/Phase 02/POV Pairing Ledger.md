## Chapter Form

- Each paired chapter prints or otherwise makes its POV unambiguous at entry.
- The Calyx member opens the pair. The corresponding Meridian POV answers in the next chapter.
- Standalone Meridian school chapters may appear between paired units. They also use one explicit POV.
- A paired Meridian chapter may contain group action, but its perception and interpretation remain bounded to the named cadet.
- AFTER receives no historical paired POV. A bounded AFTER POV may occur only after named personhood and outside a Calyx pair.
- Vey, workers, upper cadres, and ordinary cadets remain observed unless the accepted scene ledger proves private causality requires a bounded POV exception.

## Contrast Rule

The pair must compare action, pressure, or institutional use rather than repeat language. No paired characters receive the same metaphor, emotional conclusion, or explanatory paragraph merely to make correspondence legible. Juxtaposition does the work.

Each pair brief records:

- historical POV desire and incomplete knowledge;
- Meridian POV desire and incomplete knowledge;
- the role history or Meridian attempts to impose;
- the different choice each person actually makes;
- what the reader can compare that neither POV can know;
- the relationship or ordinary-life consequence carried out of the Meridian chapter.

## Verification

- Every Calyx chapter has exactly one named corresponding Meridian POV chapter.
- The five pairings occur twice before convergence.
- Calyx lived Persona ancestry remains `L1 → L10` even though presentation runs `C1 → C10`.
- No Meridian character gains historical knowledge through formal pairing.
- At least one pair in each cycle frustrates the obvious analogy.
- The final `P10` pair ends Calyx; no historical chapter follows it.
