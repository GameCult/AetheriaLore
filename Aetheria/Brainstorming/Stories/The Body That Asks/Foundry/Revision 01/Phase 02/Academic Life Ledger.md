# Academic Life Ledger

Meridian is an academy, not a year-long selection exercise. Finalist obligations sit on top of a sophomore curriculum shared with ordinary cadets. Courses, grades, projects, rivalries, clubs, and faculty demands continue when no mystery advances and frequently obstruct investigation.

This ledger owns the academic background pressure consumed by the [[Sophomore Year Calendar]]. It does not make every class a Bright Ledger prerequisite.

## Shared Sophomore Core

### Flight Dynamics II

Individual Figure handling, orbital geometry, momentum budgeting, approach law, emergency separation, and recovery from bad vectors. Lio excels in live geometry and sometimes conceals the bodily price. Senn is strong when the problem has a group shape and less comfortable when evaluated alone. Ordinary cadets can beat Finalists because Finalist hardware does not improve judgment.

### Aetheric Somatics

Body-machine calibration, pain and load reporting, phase shear, augmentation variance, and the difference between certified limits and lived capacity. The course creates ordinary academic conflict over whose self-report counts as evidence. Iri's accommodation changes later labs. It is useful care and a pathway into fitness classification.

### Sensor Epistemology And Reconstruction

Conflicting telemetry, damaged instruments, adversarial observation, uncertainty notation, and the politics of producing one actionable picture. Tem is brilliant and frequently penalized for refusing the requested singular answer. Omi enjoys the interpretive disputes until personal suspicion makes them dangerous.

### Rescue, Damage Control, And Triage

Recovering bodies, Figures, stations, and cargo while preserving local authority and limited consumables. Iri begins with practical authority from repair labor. Anik and the Lifers teach real continuity craft. Students disagree about whether leaving damaged equipment or people behind can be responsible.

### Route History And Civic Obligation

Calyx, the Unseating, defense accession, emergency compacts, labor obligations, guardianship, and famous Finalist service. The official curriculum is not wholly false; it selects admissible causes and outcomes. Assigned Seat names make every seminar answer from the five socially charged. Reader dramatic irony should make ordinary homework quietly vicious.

### Fist Studio

Every sophomore belongs to a five-cadet working group for planning, simulation, peer assessment, and recovery league. The protagonists' old provisional Fists continue as academic and social units after Finalist selection breaks their membership. Studio provides the repeated comparison between assigned household and chosen working relationships.

## Elective And Professional Tracks

- **Senn:** route design, recovery coordination, and command certification. Her desired future is visible skilled work with a return condition, not permanent household crown.
- **Omi:** compact terms, service advocacy, evidence custody, and propulsion planning. He must sometimes produce a usable answer instead of only finding the termination clause.
- **Lio:** advanced drive geometry, augmentation performance, and Plastic live-response work. Medicine and research riders attach to genuine pleasure and excellence.
- **Tem:** survey reconstruction, sensor design, and contradictory-model work. His peers value him before Mika turns every archive into biography.
- **Iri:** rescue medicine, rigger practice, interface repair, and occupied-infrastructure safety. She must receive instruction and care rather than becoming the class's unpaid maintenance organ.

Electives pull the five apart on purpose. No one course should conveniently contain the whole household or conspiracy.

## Recurring Academic Dramas

### The Broken Provisional Fists

Selection removes five students from existing studios after schedules and projects are already assigned. Former teammates must replace labor, rewrite capstones, and compete against the students who received better hardware and attention. Rhea's Fist remains intact and excellent. Other former teammates respond with pride, resentment, opportunism, relief, or refusal to become supporting cast.

### Recovery League Season

Several scored events run across the year: disabled-drone recovery, cargo-shell arrest, damaged-partner tow, blind sensor relay, and civilian-volume evacuation. The league creates standings, jokes, grudges, spectators, and tactical history. Some rounds have no mystery payload. Rule changes after the inquiry make the Finalists safer and slower; later improvement becomes politically legible without erasing ordinary competitive stakes.

### Mixed-Course Habitat Project

Small groups survey, model, and propose recovery of a retired teaching habitat. Membership crosses Fists and status. The project produces ordinary delegation failures, plagiarism suspicion, missed meetings, unequal access to expensive simulators, and one genuinely beautiful solution that belongs to a non-Finalist student. Its final presentation falls during spring rupture, forcing the protagonists to finish work with people they have neglected.

The project resolves on academic and social terms. Any habits it teaches belong to the students' general education rather than a covert rehearsal for the climax.

### Calyx Seminar Dispute

Students must defend competing interpretations of one historical intervention using the academy archive. The protagonists are expected to speak from assigned Seats; classmates accuse them alternately of exploiting, betraying, or embodying the legend. The assignment recurs as sources, grades, and public presentations change. It teaches readers official memory through a teenager's need to pass a course.

### Scholarship And Qualification Thresholds

Grades, conduct findings, medical participation, and Finalist certification affect housing, treatment, summer travel, component access, and placements through different offices. One bad term is not expulsion, but it changes who must request exceptions and what gratitude is expected. Ordinary students also depend on these thresholds, preventing the five from treating dependency as uniquely conspiratorial.

### Faculty Conflict

Instructors disagree about teaching rather than sorting into allies and villains. One rewards decisive answers; one permits uncertainty but grades unusable work harshly; one treats bodily report as primary data; one fears accommodations will leave cadets unprepared; one regards Finalist publicity as theft from the classroom. Their disputes affect assignments and recommendations without any instructor seeing Kinship whole.

### Academic Integrity Incident

A sponsor-supplied solution aid or restricted simulator trace circulates among students. The resulting dispute concerns unequal resources, collaboration, authorship, and whether using institutional privilege counts as cheating when the institution supplied it. The incident need not connect to AFTER or Kinship. It gives Omi, Tem, ordinary cadets, and faculty something consequential to be wrong about that is not the main mystery.

### Clubs And Unscored Craft

At least two recurring activities exist outside formal evaluation:

- an open repair cooperative, fabrication night, or component-art shop where apprentices and cadets exchange work under local rules;
- a music, game, flight-film, fashion, food, or spectator culture in which technical excellence is enjoyed rather than certified.

Participation changes across terms. AFTER may encounter one activity only through a physically connected household or bay surface and may dislike it. Clubs are not covert training programs.

## Character-Level Academic Pressure

Each protagonist needs:

- one course they enjoy for reasons independent of the household;
- one recurring academic weakness or conflict that competence cannot erase;
- one non-Finalist collaborator who can refuse, disappoint, or outgrow them;
- one grade, recommendation, or project outcome that matters materially;
- one moment when investigation loses because schoolwork or another student's need legitimately comes first;
- one academic success that remains worth having even after Meridian is refused.

Academic scenes are a primary voice test. Cadets complain about workload, compare scores, bluff preparation, copy one another's phrasing, show off, misunderstand instructions, resent group partners, and care about humiliatingly small distinctions. Their intelligence is visible in what they notice and do, not in universal access to polished emotional analysis.

## Verification

- Across every three consecutive school chapters, at least one active academic obligation exists that is not Finalist integration or conspiracy work.
- At least four background academic dramas recur across terms.
- At least one substantial academic conflict never joins the Kinship mystery.
- Ordinary cadets own excellent solutions, grievances, mistakes, and futures.
- Grades and qualifications affect material life through named offices without becoming a single master score.
- No class exists only to explain lore or preload a climax technique.
- The final deployment visibly spends some education while leaving other beloved knowledge gloriously irrelevant to combat.
