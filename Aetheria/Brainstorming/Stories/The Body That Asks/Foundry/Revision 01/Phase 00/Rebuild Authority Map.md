# Rebuild Authority Map

## Owner

The **Sophomore Year Calendar** will own Meridian narrative time. It will be created during synthesis and will record term, month, curriculum, public status, household routine, relationships, institutional processes, mystery state, and Calyx correspondence.

The calendar does not own character choices, institutional authority, or reader disclosure. It supplies the time substrate those owners consume.

## Inputs

- stable story constraints and protected events;
- Meridian's academic structure and three-cadre social body;
- Calyx lived chronology and reverse presentation;
- character Persona baselines and accepted relationship asymmetries;
- institutional clocks, authority-by-verb map, material state, and mystery prerequisites;
- the baseline manuscript's successful scenes, language, and causal transactions.

## Outputs

- dated school intervals with academic and domestic life;
- a revised braid extending Calyx through Bright Ledger Concordance;
- a scene transaction ledger derived from the calendar;
- replay coordinates for Meridian, Calyx presentation, and AFTER's continuous history;
- chapter boundaries chosen after scenes have earned their duration.

## Derived State

- chapter count and movement length;
- reveal order;
- training progression;
- public Finalist reputation;
- internship and career opportunity;
- relationship summaries;
- current Persona projections;
- reader and Meridian knowledge ladders.

These surfaces inspect or present the calendar and accepted deltas. They do not independently manufacture elapsed time.

## Forbidden Writers

- the old twenty-eight-chapter order cannot decide the revised calendar;
- a revelation cannot advance the term merely because its prerequisites are ready;
- montage cannot claim relationship history absent repeated consequential scenes;
- completed Pass 1–14 reports cannot be edited into retrospective agreement;
- old Pass 7 deltas cannot be re-timestamped and treated as replayed state;
- the EPUB and web reader cannot supply manuscript authority.

## Shared Paths

Selection, classes, training, medicine, discipline, holidays, family contact, covert investigation, hidden practice, deployment preparation, Bright Ledger, Concordance, and escape all use explicit narrative coordinates. Institutional actions retain their own owners but record when they become latent, visible, and consequential on the same calendar.

Every mystery uses three states:

1. **Latent operation:** machinery acts before protagonists recognize it.
2. **Bounded discovery:** one or more characters learn part of the mechanism.
3. **Afterlife:** policy, access, medicine, reputation, employment, or relationship pressure continues changing afterward.

## Central Structural Cut

The old paired midpoint first-Concordance is divided:

- **The No That Moves:** an early-spring accident in which Iri refuses, the incomplete body routes around her, crown slips, and the five preserve reproducible fragments they cannot explain. It is not sustained Concordance and does not merge the timelines.
- **Bright Ledger Concordance:** the end-of-year act in which practiced office exchange, local refusal, worker conditions, and AFTER's complete embodiment become one sustained constitutional body. Calyx reaches its first request beside this act. The braid ends because Meridian has produced a present form history cannot prescribe.

Necro and escape then occur with no Calyx interludes. The five and AFTER are free of inherited narrative guidance precisely when catastrophic failure remains imminent.

## Cut Line

Keep the completed run as evidence. Cut its claim to current completeness. Cut chapter order as the owner of time. Cut midpoint Concordance as the braid merge. Cut every summary that substitutes assertion of duration for lived transactions.

Preserve the causal skeleton, character invariants, institutional distribution, Harrowglass opening, major academy incidents, Bright Ledger, AFTER's sacrifice, Necro, and escape. Relocate and expand them only after the calendar, mystery half-lives, and Persona replay agree.

## Verification Layer

The revision must eventually prove:

- readers can reconstruct a full academic year without relying on the `3358` label;
- each term changes curriculum, relationships, reputation, and available futures;
- every major discovery has visible latent operation and later afterlife;
- Calyx remains active until Bright Ledger Concordance and never leaks future character state backward;
- The No That Moves cannot be mistaken for complete Concordance;
- every climax authority transfer was practiced earlier;
- Meridian feels sufficiently like home that escape entails grief as well as relief;
- endpoint Personas replay from valid branch parents;
- the promoted manuscript, EPUB, and reader all contain the same accepted revision.
