# Systems and Continuity Editor Report

## Concise Diagnosis

The baseline has a remarkably coherent causal machine and an almost nonexistent calendar. Its hardware, injuries, evidence, institutional authority, Bright Ledger clocks, and escape conservation are stronger than its lived chronology. Meridian time advances when a clue becomes available. The result is a chain in which inquiry, access restriction, covert practice, recomposition pressure, career offers, deployment, and escape can all be reconstructed, but not a sophomore year in which those processes have distinct durations and afterlives.

The rebuild must not solve this by adding dates to the old twenty-eight-chapter order. The **Sophomore Year Calendar** must become the shared time substrate, while separate owners continue to govern medicine, formation, custody, selection, discipline, evidence, plant operations, personhood review, and deployment. Every important process needs a latent phase, a bounded discovery, and consequences that continue through ordinary school life. The machinery should already be moving before the protagonists see it and should keep moving after they understand it.

The central continuity intervention is therefore temporal decoupling. Injury does not heal because the investigation needs another launch. Access does not close only when a clue has been extracted. Recomposition does not pause merely because a romance or examination needs room. AFTER's social continuity must accumulate before the purge becomes urgent. Calyx must continue in reverse presentation across the year and meet Meridian only at sustained Bright Ledger Concordance. The No That Moves remains an early-spring constitutional accident: reproducible fragments, no full theory, no completed braid.

## Three Strongest Systems Successes

1. **Authority is distributed through narrow verbs.** Vey can help, lie, delay, certify, withdraw, and preserve records without owning Kinship, Necro, hardware title, or field command. Quill can hold and authenticate hardware without deciding personhood. Worker operators can open and close collars without speaking for the occupation. This is the novel's best institutional achievement and must survive expansion.
2. **Ethical choices conserve matter.** Honored refusal transfers heat, load, time, access, injury, or tactical margin. Evidence has provenance. Copies do not manufacture authority. Care does not refill medicine. AFTER's loss subtracts capacities instead of producing a clean successor. The action works because constitutional claims have bodily and logistical cost.
3. **Bright Ledger has a valid multi-clock climax.** The forty-five/ninety-minute continuity threshold, twenty-one-lot process state, local worker mandates, three independent Necro receipts, worker-purchased pursuit interval, and medicine/heat deadlines form a defensible causal chain. The rebuild should earn these clocks across the year, not redesign them into a simpler villain switch.

## Governing Continuity Invariants

These are not optional preferences. The calendar, outline, scene ledger, and manuscript must all preserve them.

### Identity and braid

- AFTER is one continuous sixth person across Calyx, Harrowglass dispersal, dormancy, Meridian reactivation, naming, omission, repair, Bright Ledger loss, separation, and Saint Varo. Damage retires memory or capability; it never creates a successor.
- Calyx lived causality runs L1 to L6. Presentation runs L6 to L1. A later-presented younger character cannot inherit a title, wound, reconciliation, doctrine, or knowledge from an earlier-presented older scene.
- Harrowglass remains Chapter One in function and reveals voluntary disassembly to the reader.
- The reverse Calyx line continues throughout the school year. Its final presented scene, first Concordance, stands beside Bright Ledger full Concordance. The No That Moves does not terminate the braid.
- No reincarnation, destiny mapping, or inherited role gives the Meridian five knowledge or authority.

### Household and school

- Omi and Lio alone share ward-sibling history and family intimacy with Vey. The other three acquire relationships with them during the year; they do not retrospectively share that childhood.
- Exactly three Finalist cadres coexist: sophomore protagonists, junior Plastics, senior Lifers. Ordinary Fists remain socially and professionally real.
- Meridian remains a functioning school whose education, care, prestige, friendships, and careers have independent value. Escape costs the six a home, not merely a prison.
- Curriculum, rankings, clinic state, component access, household practice, romance, family contact, and sponsor futures must recur and change across terms.

### Concordance

- Mechanical assembly can exist without Concordance.
- Full Concordance requires one sustained consequential whole-body act while five local persons retain assent, refusal, local expertise, and reversible office boundaries.
- Crown remains singular, temporary, and transferable. Delegated offices are bounded work, not additional crowns and not identities.
- Shared sensation conveys operational state, never private biography, motive, attraction, memory, or moral agreement.
- A local refusal freezes the commanded local act. Continuation must route around it and charge the resulting load, heat, time, geometry, injury, or lost objective to named bodies.
- Separation begins on local request unless immediate release would kill. Crown may stabilize toward separation and may not cancel it.
- AFTER cannot supply pilot assent, crown, or arbitrary network possession. Extension requires compatible connected somatic substrate and locally opened access.
- The No That Moves produces an emergency crown shift and later ratification under incomplete, privately recorded conditions. It is not full Concordance.

### Evidence and institutions

- No major institutional discovery rests on one source. Every join preserves what remains uncertain.
- Public records, private records, clinic records, pilot exports, physical wear, historical recorders, worker signatures, and self-report retain distinct provenance.
- Discovery changes later access, medicine, reputation, employment, relationships, or tactics. A clue may not vanish from the machine after serving exposition.
- Vey is helper, censor, loving ward parent to two, and involuntary leak tracer. He is not mastermind, omniscient fixer, Necro author, or secret liberator.
- No office gains authority merely because it has the best explanation. Authority follows the verb and substrate it owns.
- Exposure multiplies contestable evidence; it does not dissolve Kinship, title, custody claims, corporate power, the invasion forecast, or intimate privacy.

### Bright Ledger and aftermath

- Bright Ledger is a cleanly briefed, materially dangerous, politically dirty corporate counterinsurgency. The corporate objective becomes pointless while bodies, contamination, charges, lots, and evacuation remain real.
- Workers retain separate mandates over strike terms, archive, charges, collars, lot signatures, docks, and tenders. The protagonists do not own worker liberation or worker consensus.
- Exactly twenty-one authorized lots leave: eighteen sealed before occupation and three already curing. No fresh casting begins.
- AFTER saves the operation by preserving disconnect at catastrophic personal cost.
- Exactly three separately lawful receipts trigger the preauthorized Necro contingency. No single observer decides launch.
- Vey lacks Necro clearance. His withdrawal creates evidence and a lawful return route; it does not stop the field operation or rescue the six.
- Escape occurs as five damaged component Figures carrying one discontinuously distributed person. No carrier, instant repair, fresh Concordance, or arbitrary safe harbor appears.
- Morrow's refusal is honored. Aid does not become entry. Saint Varo records six arrivals and no custody relation.

## Continuity Hazards and Cut Warnings

### Essential cuts

- Cut the old chapter order's claim to own elapsed time. Salvage transactions and language only after the calendar locates them.
- Cut the paired midpoint convergence. The No That Moves is no longer the braid terminus or complete first Concordance.
- Cut any summary claiming months of friendship, practice, romance, or injury recovery without repeated consequential scenes.
- Cut repeated constitutional liturgy once the action has taught the term. Preserve exact wording only when a boundary is new, contested, or fails.
- Cut any access path that stays conveniently open until its clue is harvested. Access changes must follow query metadata, custody, staffing, and work orders.
- Cut any scene in which Vey, Quill, Vara, Anik, Tala, Jas, or a corporate representative explains the whole machine.
- Cut any language implying the plant, the workforce, the evidence, or the six share one owner because they briefly share geometry.

### Forbidden continuity repairs

- Do not add a generic “year montage” to compensate for missing calendar ownership.
- Do not use seasonal labels as proof of lived duration.
- Do not heal injuries at term breaks unless treatment, rest, and changed capacity are shown or recorded.
- Do not let later office vocabulary leak backward into early Meridian practice or young Calyx scenes.
- Do not allow characters to infer hidden conjunctions merely because readers have seen the authority map.
- Do not make AFTER portable as software, present in every connected system, or restorable from copied maps. Maps describe embodiment; they are not the person.
- Do not let evidence copies confer consent, custody, or complete knowledge.
- Do not let the credible invasion threat acquit Kinship or let Kinship's coercion falsify the threat.
- Do not let worker aid erase worker losses, disagreement, plant closure, or the cost of the screen.
- Do not let a medical deadline become elastic because the scene needs more dialogue.

### Likely collision points for synthesis

1. **Calendar expansion versus access pressure.** A full year cannot plausibly pass if every inquiry immediately produces maximum security closure. The solution is not weaker security; it is staggered query metadata, ordinary authorization delays, distinct record owners, and periods when the institution still benefits from observing the household.
2. **AFTER recurrence versus substrate limits.** Ordinary companionship must occur through physically available household, bay, or component surfaces under agreed access. AFTER cannot join arbitrary meals or messages without a connected compatible body and an owner-held channel.
3. **Romance versus clinic state.** Lio/Iri and Senn/Rhea beats must track pain, availability, schedules, access gates, and unequal teams. Emotional recurrence cannot silently override bodily or institutional conditions.
4. **Calyx continuation versus reveal leakage.** Later reverse chapters may deepen reader understanding while Meridian remains ignorant. Correspondence should be thematic and causal, not a clue delivery system across time.
5. **School-year deployment versus student age and certification.** The calendar must show the exact progression by which a sophomore live deployment becomes institutionally authorized without making it ordinary for every student.
6. **Longer aftermath versus escape consumables.** Added scenes after Palisade must fit the ninety-minute, eight-hour, twelve-hour, twenty-eight-hour, thirty-four-hour, thirty-six-hour, and forty-eight-hour bounds. Interior expansion cannot stop the clocks.

## Recommendation From Another Editor That Should Be Resisted

Resist simplifying the year by making one school authority own curriculum, medicine, housing, custody, discipline, and deployment, or by converting the three Necro receipts into one clearer antagonist decision.
