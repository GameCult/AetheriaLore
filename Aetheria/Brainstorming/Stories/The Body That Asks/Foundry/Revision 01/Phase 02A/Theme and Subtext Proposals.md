# Theme and Subtext Proposals

## Authority And Scope

These proposals implement the accepted [[../Phase 02/Council Synthesis|Council Synthesis]], [[../Phase 02/Sophomore Year Calendar|Sophomore Year Calendar]], [[../Phase 02/Academic Life Ledger|Academic Life Ledger]], [[../Phase 02/Mystery Half-Life Ledger|Mystery Half-Life Ledger]], and [[../Phase 02/Protected Material Ledger|Protected Material Ledger]]. They do not reopen calendar ownership, the incomplete status of The No That Moves, or full Concordance at Bright Ledger.

The proposal unit is a scene transaction or a bounded rewrite instruction, not a chapter number. Chapter stewards may combine adjacent transactions after coordinator adjudication. Every school-life addition below must either change attachment, desire, embarrassment, relationship, academic standing, or material access. It need not and often must not advance Kinship, AFTER, Calyx, Vesper, Mika, Bright Ledger, or Necro.

Palisade, Serein, Halcyon, Tala, Jas, the strike, the sterility ledger, and the twenty-one lots enter only with the Bright Ledger assignment in `F2/B1`. No school-year class, game, project, rumor, sponsor display, or Calyx interlude names or hints them. Meridian teaches broadly useful craft. Palisade is not a lesson plan waiting to become scenery.

## Global Rewrite Rules

### G1 — Constitutional language contracts with familiarity

- **Exact anchors:** Baseline Chapter 2 household rules; Chapter 8 safety inquiry; Chapter 10 first contact; Chapter 12 hidden trial; Chapters 16–23 document and assent sequences; Chapter 26 station-by-station terms.
- **Instruction:** Keep full procedural language only when a boundary is first established, violated, altered, or extended to a new constituency. After Finalist Dinner, familiar household practice should usually appear as a short request, visible cutout, changed seat, withheld touch, returned object, named cost, or refusal acted upon. Restore full terms at the inquiry, first AFTER contact, repair after the forced audit, and worker/plant-scale Concordance.
- **Rationale:** Repeated constitutional liturgy currently lets explanation outrun drama and makes five teenagers sound like one legal intelligence.
- **Grounding:** Council Synthesis, “Prose Rulings”; Protected Material Ledger, “Prohibited Damage”; baseline Chapters 2, 8, 10, 20–21, and 26.
- **Expected benefit:** Sharper subtext, more differentiated speech, and greater force when explicit terms truly matter.
- **Risk:** Over-compression could obscure Concordance mechanics. Systems review must mark first establishment, change, violation, and scale change before cuts.
- **Priority:** Essential.
- **Dependencies:** Prose/Dialog and Systems proposals; scene order must be stable enough to know whether a rule has already been taught.
- **Protected unchanged:** `Concern isn't a master key`; `Care ends when the care ends`; first-contact terms; repair terms; Palisade collar conditions.

### G2 — Scene endings stop certifying the theme

- **Exact anchors:** Baseline closing paragraphs of Chapters 13, 16–23, 25–28; any new scene ending in an institutional reversal, maxim, or narrated denial of absolution.
- **Instruction:** Where an object, schedule change, bodily consequence, refusal, or altered relationship already carries the point, cut the final explanatory sentence. Across any five consecutive scenes, allow at most two endings built on a maxim, institutional irony, or explicit statement that nobody is forgiven/acquitted.
- **Rationale:** The manuscript repeatedly lands a strong action and then explains its political meaning.
- **Grounding:** Council Synthesis, “Prose Rulings”; raw Theme report, Finding 5.
- **Expected benefit:** Readers perform interpretation; aphorisms retain voltage.
- **Risk:** Some causal distinctions are load-bearing rather than thematic gloss. Systems editor must protect trigger, authority, and knowledge facts.
- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Dependencies:** Final scene drafts and continuity review.
- **Protected unchanged:** “The plant was sold through verbs”; “Care arrived without entry”; “The machine had become small enough to hold.” Do not manufacture rival closing aphorisms around them.

### G3 — Palisade quarantine line

- **Exact anchors:** All `A0–F1` scenes, particularly mixed habitat project, rescue courses, industrial safety, Vey's forecast, sponsor gala, and deployment qualification.
- **Instruction:** Delete or reject any pre-assignment reference to Palisade, Serein, Halcyon, Tala, Jas, the strike, sterility production, the twenty-one lots, or a conveniently identical plant geometry. Do not let a school exercise reproduce axial spine, radial collars, executive/medical routes, archive charges, or the precise conflict among new production, existing lots, evacuation, and custody. `F2` Bright Ledger assignment is the first entry point.
- **Rationale:** Workers and plant politics must own lives not arranged as protagonist preparation. Education becomes useful incidentally.
- **Grounding:** Council Synthesis, “Palisade enters with the assignment”; Academic Life Ledger, habitat-project and verification rules.
- **Expected benefit:** Bright Ledger remains a political shock rather than an exam answer. Worker authority arrives as genuinely external reality.
- **Risk:** The climax may feel insufficiently prepared if broad craft is also removed. Preserve general rescue, occupied-infrastructure, industrial, medical, evidence, and office skills without matching the case.
- **Priority:** Essential and non-negotiable.
- **Dependencies:** Structural, Systems, and Culture/Collective-Personhood proposals.
- **Protected unchanged:** Baseline Bright Ledger orientation and worker-owned terms once assignment begins.
