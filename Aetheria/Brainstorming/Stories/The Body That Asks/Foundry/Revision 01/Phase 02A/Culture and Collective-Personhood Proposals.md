Priorities are `Essential`, `High`, or `Optional`. “Protected unchanged” names a span whose cultural or collective-personhood transaction should survive; compatible structural or prose revision may still alter its container.

## Locked Braid And POV Direction

Braided chapters use explicit corresponding-role pairs imposed by Meridian's institutional analogy: **Mara/Senn, Sol/Omi, Eda/Lio, Tern/Tem, Ysra/Iri**. This is not reincarnation, inherited essence, personality equivalence, or proof that the Seats describe either historical or contemporary people completely. The likely two cycles should first make the analogy socially seductive, then expose where lived persons exceed it.

### CC-00 — The Pair Comparison Must Produce Cultural Friction, Not Five Mirrors

- **Anchor:** Two braided cycles distributed from `A0` through `B2`; exact order belongs to the braid steward.
- **POV:** Every braided chapter names both close-third owners explicitly and keeps a visible break between them. Corresponding pairs only: Mara/Senn, Sol/Omi, Eda/Lio, Tern/Tem, Ysra/Iri.
- **Intervention:** In the first cycle, let school culture teach the role analogy through Seat assignments, seminar expectations, sponsor film, peer shorthand, and cadre mentoring. Place one mundane Calyx practice beside one Meridian practice without asserting equivalence: Mara's tea against Senn's scheduling; Sol repairing Eda's kettle against Omi negotiating shared work; Eda/Tern serialized-drama arguments against Lio's public performance culture; Tern's music queues against Tem's playful false histories; Ysra's haircuts against Iri's ornamental repair. In the second cycle, make each imposed comparison fail at a consequential edge. Senn wants coordination work but is not Mara's crown; Omi's dissent is shaped by ward custody rather than Sol's institutional credibility; Lio desires Plastic excellence unlike Eda's sponsor liaison politics; Tem's plural perception grows through Mika and dual domicile rather than Tern's archival ambition; Iri's repair labor and family pressure are not Ysra's medical legend. Peers and staff may use the analogy; narration does not ratify it.
- **Grounding:** Locked operator direction; Council anti-reincarnation ruling; Calyx Fivefold mundane bonds; Academic Life Ledger, Calyx seminar dispute.
- **Benefit:** The braid becomes a social machine that pressures identity while comparison yields contrast, historical irony, and role critique.
- **Risk:** Symmetrical chapters, reincarnation coding, or contemporary characters becoming corrected versions of historical ones.
- **Priority:** Essential lock.
- **Dependencies:** Structural braid order; Character/POV owns pair-specific delta ancestry; prose must avoid parallel phrasing that claims sameness.
- **Protected unchanged:** AFTER is the only continuous person across the lines. The ten humans are distinct; Seat analogy is institutional classification.
