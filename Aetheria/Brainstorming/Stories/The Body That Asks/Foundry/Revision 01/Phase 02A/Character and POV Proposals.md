# Character and POV Proposals

## Proposal Authority

These proposals consume the accepted [[../Phase 02/Council Synthesis|Council Synthesis]], [[../Phase 02/Sophomore Year Calendar|Sophomore Year Calendar]], [[../Phase 02/Academic Life Ledger|Academic Life Ledger]], and [[../Phase 02/Protected Material Ledger|Protected Material Ledger]]. Priorities are `Essential`, `High`, or `Optional`. “Protected unchanged” means no character/POV change proposed beyond the named span; it does not forbid another accepted specialty from proposing a compatible structural alteration.

## Locked Voice And POV Directions

### Adolescent Fluency Is Not Self-Knowledge

The five are elite technical students. They can speak precisely about load, route, provenance, bodily limits, treatment, component behavior, evidence custody, and the formal terms placed in front of them. They cannot therefore speak with equal precision about why they are jealous, frightened, attracted, grateful, ashamed, lonely, controlling, avoidant, or cruel. No scene may grant the household one shared therapy vocabulary, psychology-major fluency, or room-level constitutional analysis.

Correct appraisal should usually appear first as behavior: changed status play, a joke that lands badly, avoided touch, a missed invitation, overwork, withheld help, a technical objection aimed at the wrong grievance, a bad accusation, an impulsive report, or a choice whose meaning becomes only partly legible later. Urgent safety facts may be exact while emotional interpretation remains incomplete.

| Cadet | Distinct inarticulacy | What replaces correct early appraisal | Later partial insight allowed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Senn** | Cannot easily say she wants recognition, fears being unnecessary, resents exclusion, or is attracted to Rhea. Converts desire into preparation and hurt into an unsolicited plan. | Rewrites a schedule nobody asked her to own; volunteers for visible work; corrects Rhea's method after losing; omits herself from the dinner sentence; becomes formal when jealous. | Can admit one concrete selfish want or that she took work because she wanted to be seen. She does not diagnose “care becoming command” at room scale. |
