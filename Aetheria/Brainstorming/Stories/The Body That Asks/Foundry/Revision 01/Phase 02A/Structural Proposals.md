Standalone Meridian chapters may sit between completed paired units, never between the Calyx opener and its Meridian answer. ## Detailed Proposals

### ST-01 — Return Before Selection

- **Exact anchor:** `A0`, around locked pair `P1`: Mara's `C1/L10 — Harrowglass` immediately followed by the explicit Senn-POV selection chapter. Additional return scenes follow the completed pair as standalone Meridian chapters.
- **Intervention:** Open Meridian in Senn POV at public selection, where her expectation of continuing with Rhea is broken and her arranging instinct is rewarded before the new household can answer. Add two compact standalone return/moving scenes afterward, each with explicit POV: recommended Omi for ward shorthand and displaced studio labor, then Iri for component storage, old labor relations, and Lio's expected drive path. Every protagonist must make one small choice involving someone outside the future household across the unit.
- **Rationale:** Selection cannot break a social body the reader has never seen. The accepted calendar requires old rooms, provisional Fists, friends, elective expectations, and summer changes before prestige rearranges them.
- **Grounding:** Calendar `A0`; Academic Life Ledger, “Broken Provisional Fists”; baseline Chapter 2 begins after the institution has already moved their belongings.
- **Expected benefit:** The manufactured household has visible opportunity cost. Rhea's intact Fist becomes a counterfactual life rather than a scoreboard.
- **Collateral risk:** Five simultaneous introductions could front-load names and stall the Harrowglass hook.
- **Priority:** Essential.
- **Dependencies:** Character/POV must assign the two scene owners and identify one bounded outside relationship per protagonist; Reader Experience must cap name load.
