# Systems and Continuity Proposals

## Remit And Controlling Rulings

These proposals consume the accepted [[../Phase 02/Council Synthesis|Council Synthesis]], [[../Phase 02/Sophomore Year Calendar|Sophomore Year Calendar]], [[../Phase 02/Academic Life Ledger|Academic Life Ledger]], [[../Phase 02/Mystery Half-Life Ledger|Mystery Half-Life Ledger]], and [[../Phase 02/Protected Material Ledger|Protected Material Ledger]], plus the raw [[../Phase 01/Systems and Continuity Editor|Systems and Continuity Editor Report]], baseline manuscript, and stable sideband.

The calendar owns Meridian elapsed time. Chapter count remains derived. The No That Moves is an incomplete constitutional accident at `S1`; full Concordance occurs only at `B2`; Calyx continues until that act and is absent afterward.

### Locked braid pairing

Braided chapters use explicit POV and direct corresponding-role pairs. Every Calyx chapter is immediately followed by a Meridian chapter from its assigned institutional counterpart:

- Mara → Senn;
- Sol → Omi;
- Eda → Lio;
- Tern → Tem;
- Ysra → Iri.

The braid runs two complete cycles across the year. Calyx presentation moves from lived coordinate `L10` backward to `L1`. The final `Ysra/L1 → Iri/B2` pair carries historical first Concordance followed by present full Concordance. Standalone Meridian school chapters may sit between completed pairs and always name explicit POV. A Calyx chapter and its paired Meridian chapter must remain adjacent; no standalone chapter may interrupt the pair.

The pairing is an imposed institutional analogy made available to the reader, not reincarnation, equivalence, inherited memory, causal transmission, or proof that the assigned Seats correctly describe either person. A Meridian counterpart gains no knowledge from the preceding Calyx chapter. Only records available inside Meridian may alter Meridian knowledge.

### Palisade quarantine

Palisade, Serein, Halcyon, Tala, Jas, the strike, the twenty-one-lot fact, the corporate option, and the plant's specific geometry do not enter the manuscript before the Bright Ledger assignment at `F3/B1`. School-year scenes may teach generally useful flight, rescue, industrial, medical, evidentiary, and civic craft. They may not secretly rehearse, name, research, model, visit, or foreshadow the specific operation. The assignment creates the first lawful knowledge path to Palisade.

### Proposal contract

Each proposal below names:

- an exact calendar and scene anchor;
- the operation performed;
- rationale and expected reader benefit;
- collateral risk, priority, and dependencies;
- authority, knowledge, bodily/material, and access state changes;
- protected material that remains unchanged.

The proposals are scene transactions, not chapter assignments.
