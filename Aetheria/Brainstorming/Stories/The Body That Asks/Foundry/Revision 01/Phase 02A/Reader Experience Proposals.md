# Phase 2A — Reader Experience Proposals

## Mandate

These proposals translate the accepted Phase 2 architecture into reader-facing interventions. They assume the [[../Phase 02/Sophomore Year Calendar]] owns elapsed time, the [[../Phase 02/POV Pairing Ledger]] owns the explicit Calyx/Meridian comparison, and the [[../Phase 02/Protected Material Ledger]] remains binding.

Priorities use:

- **P0** — necessary for the rebuilt manuscript to deliver its accepted form;
- **P1** — high-value reader experience work;
- **P2** — useful refinement after the causal scene ledger is stable.

## Proposal 1 — Establish the school lives that selection interrupts

**Priority:** P0

**Exact anchor:** `A0`, between opening `C1/L10 — Harrowglass` and the baseline material of “Finalist Dinner.”

**Intervention:** Add a bounded return-and-selection sequence before the five move into the Finalist household. Give each cadet one existing attachment and one expected sophomore future in action:

- Senn completes a provisional-Fist planning handoff with Rhea and assumes she will keep doing this work with her.
- Omi and Lio use ward-family shorthand with Vey while disagreeing about what his help costs.
- Tem returns to a studio or domicile relationship in which his contradictory accounts are amusing or useful rather than already ominous.
- Iri arrives through paid or reciprocal repair work and has a material obligation that selection will displace.
- Lio experiences individual Figure flight or drive work as pleasure before Finalist medicine and sponsorship attach to it.

Selection then breaks schedules, project groups, access expectations, and social status in public. Moving day and Finalist Dinner inherit those live losses.

**Reader benefit:** The household becomes a manufactured family because the reader has seen what was manufactured over. The academy immediately feels like a school with a past, not a facility that begins when the plot selects its cast. Rhea, Vey, former teammates, money, treatment, and electives enter as relationships rather than later explanatory additions.

**Risk:** Five miniature introductions can become a roll call or delay the excellent dinner scene.

**Control:** Build two or three shared scenes with intersecting obligations, not five origin vignettes. Each cadet receives one memorable action, not a biography.

**Dependencies:** Calendar `A0`; Academic Life Ledger's broken provisional Fists, Fist Studio, and electives; exact named recurring peers from later character packets.

**Unchanged protections:** Harrowglass remains the opening. Readers still know chosen disassembly before Meridian does. Finalist Dinner retains the sixth chair, privacy rules, “Concern isn't a master key,” and “Care ends when the care ends.”

## Proposal 2 — Teach the body vocabulary through one mixed somatics lab

**Priority:** P0

**Exact anchor:** `A1`, before the first recovery-league fixture and well before `A2` “Eighty-Two Percent.”

**Intervention:** Stage a mixed Aetheric Somatics lab in explicit single POV. The exercise should make four relations physically obvious without a glossary:

1. one pilot owns one **Figure**;
2. five cadets form a **Fist** or household for school work;
3. five Figures may physically assemble into a **Concord**;
4. **Seats** are inherited academy role labels, not bodies, permanent offices, or proof of aptitude.

Let an ordinary cadet correct or mock a Finalist's misuse of one term. Let an instructor use the official Seat vocabulary while the actual exercise gives a different student temporary competence. End on a social or scoring consequence, not a definition recap.

**Reader benefit:** Removes the baseline's largest early orientation tax before assembly becomes dangerous. It also establishes that ordinary students share the technical world and can outperform prestige hardware.

**Risk:** A transparent tutorial scene could feel written for the reader.

**Control:** Make the teaching objective somatic calibration and group status, with terminology embedded in mistakes, bets, and physical handling. Nobody recites the system correctly for a page.

**Dependencies:** Academic Life Ledger's Aetheric Somatics and Fist Studio; the selected POV for the standalone `A1` chapter; component mechanics preserved from baseline Chapters 4 and 12.

**Unchanged protections:** Seat assignment remains institutional imposition, not reincarnation or natural destiny. Technical sophistication remains real.

## Proposal 3 — Make the two-cycle POV comparison explicit at chapter scale

**Priority:** P0

**Exact anchor:** all ten ledger pairs `P1–P10`.

**Intervention:** Every pair packet should contain a short reader-comparison card before drafting:

- named Calyx POV desire and blind spot;
- named Meridian POV desire and blind spot;
- imposed role;
- choice made differently;
- one image or object that belongs only to the Calyx chapter;
- one ordinary-life consequence that exits the Meridian chapter;
- the inference available to the reader but unavailable to both characters.

At chapter entry, make POV unmistakable within the first paragraph through embodied attention, not a decorative nameplate alone. Preserve adjacency: Calyx opens, its named Meridian counterpart answers next. Standalone school chapters may follow, but no unrelated chapter should split a formal pair.

Use the first cycle to make roles appear fixed and the second to loosen offices. At least two pairs should frustrate surface analogy:

- `P2` should not make Omi simply “another Sol”; Omi's appetite for dissent can help certify the household where Sol's public credibility extends guardianship.
- `P8` should let Lio admire Vesper's beauty and want Vara's future even after recognizing Eda's discarded objection inside the architecture.

**Reader benefit:** The braid becomes a legible comparative engine rather than alternating lore delivery. Repeated surnames stop being cast burden and become dramatic pressure. Readers can feel historical rhyme without being told reincarnation is false every time.

**Risk:** Over-signalling can make pairs schematic or produce duplicated metaphors and conclusions.

**Control:** Pair action and institutional use, never prose. Ban mirrored closing lines and repeated explanatory paragraphs. The Meridian chapter must leave a contemporary relationship or school obligation changed.

**Dependencies:** POV Pairing Ledger; calendar placement; final scene transaction ledger; Calyx historical chronology `L1–L10`.

**Unchanged protections:** Pairing transfers no knowledge across time and implies no inherited debt. AFTER receives no historical paired POV. Calyx ends at `P10/B2` and never returns after full Concordance.

## Proposal 4 — Make a full year reconstructable through returning surfaces

**Priority:** P0

**Exact anchor:** `A1`, `A4–A5`, `W1–W2`, `W3–W4`, `S1 aftermath`, `S2–S3`, `S6–F2`.

**Intervention:** Use six recurring surfaces as the reader's calendar:

- the common table and sixth place;
- recovery-league standings and cheap-gallery crowd behavior;
- the mixed-course habitat project;
- Quill's bay schedule and apprentice access;
- clinic/treatment routines;
- family, leave, and placement messages.

Each return must visibly alter at least two things: who sits or works there, what object remains, what body can do, who is welcome, what obligation is late, or which future is now possible. Give terms distinct material weather:

- autumn: new uniforms, crowded galleries, diagnostic shine, schedule abundance;
- midwinter: reduced staffing, residence food, dimmer public spaces, make-up work, more private noise;
- spring: worn components, missed meetings, track separation, rumor, restricted bays, accumulated objects;
- finals: borrowed formal clothing, packed cabins, exhausted shorthand, placement traffic, interrupted summer plans.

Print few or no calendar coordinates. Let readers reconstruct duration from academic recurrence and changed use.

**Reader benefit:** The accepted school-year saga will feel lived rather than announced. Injury, attraction, resentment, and household habit gain time to acquire afterlives. Bright Ledger then spends a year the reader wants to preserve.

**Risk:** Recurrence can become episodic padding or a school-event checklist.

**Control:** No return exists only to prove time passed. It must change relationship configuration, material access, academic standing, or bodily capability. At least one pleasure per interval has no mystery yield.

**Dependencies:** Sophomore Year Calendar; Academic Life Ledger; injury/access continuity; final chapter grouping.

**Unchanged protections:** No generic montage and no one-chapter-per-month grid. Meridian's pleasures and education remain genuine rather than disguised clues.

## Proposal 5 — Give each cadet technically fluent but adolescent emotional syntax

**Priority:** P0

**Exact anchor:** all new ordinary school scenes; intensive audit at `A0–A5`, `W1–W4`, `S1 aftermath`, and `S2–S3` rupture chapters.

**Intervention:** Draft and revise ordinary dialogue/interiority against five distinct failure modes:

- **Senn arranges:** changes seats, schedules, routes, or task ownership when she cannot say she is afraid, jealous, or attracted.
- **Omi prosecutes or jokes:** finds the invalid clause, exaggerates the evidentiary stakes, or makes a joke sharp enough to avoid asking for reassurance.
- **Lio performs:** displays competence, brightness, flirtation, or controlled pain; she often knows what an audience sees before she knows what she feels.
- **Tem multiplies:** supplies rival interpretations when one personal claim would expose him; his uncertainty may be brilliant, annoying, playful, or cowardly.
- **Iri works or leaves:** repairs the hinge, names the immediate physical requirement, changes who may touch a body, or exits before offering a theory of the relationship.

Permit polished language where training earns it: machinery, scores, medicine, evidence formats, route law, and school rules. Remove or redistribute dialogue in which teenagers spontaneously state the complete psychological or institutional diagnosis. Put the correct appraisal into changed routine, adult observation, later records, or reader inference.

For every major relationship injury, require this sequence:

1. action or omission;
2. changed behavior visible in a later school obligation;
3. partial, biased explanation;
4. only later, if earned, a more accurate formulation.

**Reader benefit:** Preserves the cast's intelligence while restoring age, status anxiety, embarrassment, attraction, and uneven self-knowledge. Dialogue becomes character-specific rather than five variations of constitutional counsel.

**Risk:** Deliberate inarticulacy can make conflict feel artificially prolonged.

**Control:** Safety facts stay clear immediately. Adolescents may correctly state a stop, dose, source, or rule while misunderstanding why the conflict hurts. The uncertainty concerns feeling and relationship, not basic plot comprehension.

**Dependencies:** POV assignments; academic scene packets; relationship continuity ledger; adult-voice differentiation.

**Unchanged protections:** The five remain elite cadets. Iri's explicit refusals, Lio's medical knowledge, Tem's epistemic skill, Omi's procedural fluency, and Senn's coordination competence are not made childish.

## Proposal 6 — Use ordinary school pressure for modulation, not camouflage

**Priority:** P1

**Exact anchor:** `A1`, `A3`, `A4–A5`, `W1–W4`, `S2–S3`, and `S6–F2`.

**Intervention:** Build a modulation circuit from four recurring dramas:

- recovery league;
- mixed-course habitat project;
- one academic-integrity dispute unrelated to Kinship;
- one unscored craft or spectator culture.

Distribute them so no more than two consecutive school chapters foreground inquiry, custody, or constitutional procedure. Let some school scenes end on a grade, humiliating joke, lost collaborator, attraction beat, broken fabrication, or ordinary victory. AFTER may encounter one unscored activity through compatible household or bay substrate and may dislike it, fail at its humor, or request silence.

Use these scenes to create later cost, not secret preparation. A non-Finalist owns the habitat project's best solution. An ordinary teammate's deadline can legitimately defeat an investigation opportunity. The academic-integrity incident gives Omi and Tem something consequential to misjudge that does not reveal Kinship.

**Reader benefit:** Prevents the year from feeling like stretched mystery runway. Gives the target audience the school-story pleasures promised by the setting and lets humor, status, boredom, and craft reset cognitive load.

**Risk:** Side plots can proliferate or become suspiciously convenient competency training for Palisade.

**Control:** Each drama receives one owner, one recurrence, and one resolution on its own terms. At least one beloved skill remains gloriously irrelevant to combat.

**Dependencies:** Academic Life Ledger; named ordinary peers; term schedule; AFTER substrate rules.

**Unchanged protections:** School is not fake, and ordinary cadets are not a chorus for the protagonists. AFTER remains physically bounded, not dorm-wide or cute by default.

## Proposal 7 — Compress the investigation by moving repetition into consequence

**Priority:** P0

**Exact anchor:** `S2–S5`, replacing the baseline concentration of “The Receipt,” “The School Continues,” “Three Histories Become Evidence,” “The Protective Turn,” “A Name in the Hardware,” “Harrowglass Recorder,” “The Missing Body,” and “The Terms of Repair.”

**Intervention:** Preserve the accepted discoveries but prohibit consecutive scenes whose primary transaction is document joining. Use this sequence:

1. **Lio's comparison screen:** internship pleasure produces the Mika code; she preserves context and sends it without commentary.
2. **Later Vey/Tem scene:** receipt chain and Lio's confession rupture trust. Do not discover the cohort pattern here.
3. **School consequence:** altered seating, withdrawn lab support, compromised group work, and Omi/Lio gratitude conflict occur before the next evidence advance.
4. **Tem's later disclosure/cohort join:** ordinary public records reveal recomposition as a pattern without locating Mika.
5. **Vesper bodily evidence:** move from rows back into a famous maneuver, wear, and executive exclusion.
6. **AFTER name/purge and Harrowglass recorder:** separate recognition from mortal danger long enough for named household life to change.
7. **Missing-body audit and repair:** keep the full physical violation and map surrender; compress familiar confirmation procedure.

Once the household has established a known practice, summarize it in character shorthand unless a term changes, someone violates it, or the practice scales to a new constituency. Replace repeated “not forgiveness” narration with later behavior: who no longer watches whose channel, who is not invited, which chair stays empty, whose access remains restricted.

**Reader benefit:** Keeps every mystery half-life and relationship wound while eliminating the baseline middle's procedural blur. Evidence becomes memorable because it changes school life before the next document arrives.

**Risk:** Overcompression could make the recomposition discovery seem too easy or weaken provenance credibility.

**Control:** Retain one fully legible custody chain—the Mika receipt—and one fully legible independent-copy repair—the embodiment map. Other joins may be summarized through consequence and authenticated result.

**Dependencies:** Mystery Half-Life Ledger; S2–S5 calendar; relationship aftermath scenes; Vey query consequences; Quill access chronology.

**Unchanged protections:** Mika remains unresolved. No mastermind emerges. “I forgot it on purpose,” “I believe you. I still don't trust you,” `YOU CUT WHERE I AM`, and repair through complete map plus surrendered sole route control remain load-bearing.

## Proposal 8 — Delay finished doctrine; shorten familiar practice

**Priority:** P1

**Exact anchor:** from Finalist Dinner through `S1` “The No That Moves,” `S4–S5` repair, and `B2` full Concordance.

**Intervention:** Give Finalist Dinner the full language because the household is establishing first principles. Thereafter, let the five imitate, misuse, weaponize, or abbreviate those words. “Held” may become intimate shorthand, irritated compliance, or a joke before it becomes mature operational ritual.

At `S1`, do not let the group derive the complete office constitution from the crown slip. They know only:

- Iri said no;
- the proposed motion stopped;
- the body continued around the refusal;
- crown moved before consent was formulated;
- ratification stabilized a dangerous fact after it occurred;
- their evidence is partial and their discipline consequences are real.

Reserve complete formulations about owner, inputs, return, and shared paths for later cross-office practice, the embodiment-map repair, and Bright Ledger's scaling to worker operators. In emergencies, use action language first; put full causal reconstruction in later records only where the reader needs it.

**Reader benefit:** The characters grow into their constitutional competence across a year rather than appearing to possess the novel's thesis in the first term. Repeated procedure becomes faster and more emotionally varied.

**Risk:** Readers may lose track of the authority model if familiar practice becomes too elliptical.

**Control:** Keep a visible owner and physical consequence in every major transfer. Readers need not hear the whole rule if they can see who acts, who may stop, and when authority returns.

**Dependencies:** Cross-office curriculum; paired `P6`, `P9`, and `P10`; prose-level repetition audit.

**Unchanged protections:** The No That Moves retains explicit refusal, crown slip, ratification, abandoned objective, and separation. Full Concordance occurs only at Bright Ledger.

## Proposal 9 — Introduce Palisade late, then repeat one spatial sentence

**Priority:** P0

**Exact anchor:** `B1` assignment briefing and opening of Bright Ledger; repeated at major geometry changes in `B1–B2`.

**Intervention:** Do not seed Palisade, Serein, Halcyon, Tala, Jas, or the strike into the school year. At assignment, give the reader a simple four-part map and repeat its vocabulary consistently:

- executive dock and volatile stores **sunward**;
- medical corridor and worker tenders **anti-sunward**;
- control spine **axial**;
- occupied maintenance ring **around it**.

Use the same orientation at entry, after the executive breach, when title transfers, and immediately before full assembly. Each restatement should say what has changed, not redescribe the plant. Keep collar numbers subordinate to named local owners unless a number has immediate tactical consequence.

Introduce Tala through the first conditional collar and Jas through the archive/charge boundary. Let workers correct the academy's political categories in present action rather than through school foreshadowing.

**Reader benefit:** Readers enter the climax with a stable mental map while sharing the cadets' political unreadiness. Late worker entry protects worker autonomy and prevents the year from turning them into protagonist setup.

**Risk:** Palisade may feel like a new novel arriving at the climax.

**Control:** The place is new; the skills and relationships used there are not. Entry should visibly activate year-long training, injuries, cadre doctrines, and office practice without implying Meridian secretly rehearsed this conflict.

**Dependencies:** Final Bright Ledger scene ledger; Academic Life skill continuity; worker authority packets; component geometry.

**Unchanged protections:** “The plant was sold through verbs,” existing-versus-fresh production, distinct worker mandates, conditional collars, and the political objective dying while physical emergencies remain all survive.

## Proposal 10 — Make the three receipts comprehensible before Necro moves

**Priority:** P0

**Exact anchor:** late `B2`, immediately after AFTER returns smaller and before `B3/U1` Necro launch.

**Intervention:** Seed the three interlocks separately during final certification as ordinary return conditions, without showing their conjunction:

- mission deviation/recovery hold;
- full Concordance outside Kinship control;
- unregistered Aetheric agency through public infrastructure.

At activation, give Omi one visual or procedural page with three separate owners, three green receipts, and one newly visible conjunction: all three being true authorizes local contingency release; no single owner can revoke the combined result alone. State that the deterrence tender is already cold-staged at Serein's executive berth before or as the berth opens.

The reading sequence should be:

1. each receipt is locally lawful;
2. the conjunction is newly visible and already evaluated;
3. Vey can withdraw certification but does not own any receipt;
4. Necro launches locally rather than being summoned by a mastermind.

**Reader benefit:** Preserves the horror of distributed authority while preventing the climax from reading as surprise bureaucracy. The reader can understand causality before needing to understand combat.

**Risk:** Too much advance explanation will spoil the launch; too little will repeat the baseline's reconstruction burden.

**Control:** Seed individual interlocks as mundane paperwork. Reveal only the conjunction and staging at execution audit.

**Dependencies:** `F2` readiness scene; Omi POV or bounded perception at activation; exact receipt owner names; Necro staging mechanics.

**Unchanged protections:** Three independently lawful receipts launch Necro. Vey neither knows about nor controls the asset. Kinship, Bright Ledger, custody, and Vey do not collapse into one authority.

## Proposal 11 — Give the climax two pressure releases before escape

**Priority:** P1

**Exact anchor:** within `B2` full Concordance and between `B2` and Necro engagement.

**Intervention:** Preserve sustained catastrophic pressure but insert two short perceptual rests:

- after station-by-station assent and before the largest load, allow one beat in which the body works imperfectly and the cadets recognize a year of learned craft without summarizing it;
- after Palisade's act completes and AFTER's losses are first inventoried, allow the twenty-one lots, exposed people, closed production line, and worker-owned records to exist as outcomes before the receipts conjoin.

These are not safety pauses. Material clocks continue. The purpose is to let readers register achievement and cost before the next authority layer fires.

**Reader benefit:** Prevents emotional events from cancelling one another through uninterrupted intensity. AFTER's sacrifice, worker success, Vey's withdrawal, and Necro each retain distinct weight.

**Risk:** Any pause can reduce urgency or resemble a victory lap.

**Control:** Keep a visible heat, medicine, pursuit, or stability clock in both intervals. Characters perform immediate work rather than congratulate themselves.

**Dependencies:** B2 tactical sequence; AFTER loss inventory; worker convoy timing; three-receipt activation.

**Unchanged protections:** No gallery, score, clean victory, rescue ownership, or restored guardianship appears.

## Proposal 12 — Expand aftermath by separating clocks and constituencies

**Priority:** P0

**Exact anchor:** `U2–U3`, from worker-screen escape through Saint Varo's final message.

**Intervention:** Keep the baseline aftermath sequence but give its major transactions separate scene space and explicit clock ownership:

1. **Worker clock:** ninety-minute screen, independent ledgers, Jas's boundaries, Palisade deaths, employment claims, closure, and correction of liberation language.
2. **Medical clock:** Tem's burn window, Lio's medicine, Iri's joint, Senn's seizure risk, Omi's fouled propulsion, tender triage, and why Orison's real clinic is declined.
3. **School/relationship clock:** Vey's signed withdrawal, Marae notification, Rhea's testimony and recovery card, Quill/access consequences if available through authenticated messages, Vara and Anik preserving their own records without joining the escape.
4. **Harbor/consent clock:** Morrow refuses entry, offers inspected pallet aid, incurs a debt without custody, and sends Saint Varo only after the five turn away.
5. **AFTER clock:** damaged islands are inventoried through specific missing capacities, intermittent reach, and choices about which message to open.

Do not combine these into one universal evidence session. Let the same sentence or event be used differently by different relationships, as Vey's loving message is family for Lio and evidence for Omi. Preserve sleep, dressing change, food, silence, and component docking as material actions.

At Saint Varo, retain six separate entries and delay the final machine-person exchange until the reader has felt that nobody must take another immediate load. Open only the requested first channel; keep the other two latent.

**Reader benefit:** The novel ends with consequences rather than summary. Workers, school relationships, medical vulnerability, harbor politics, and machine personhood remain separate realities. The reader receives decompression without false safety and a strong desire to continue.

**Risk:** Expanded aftermath can feel like several endings or drain the final question's force.

**Control:** Each scene closes one immediate clock while opening no replacement climax. The movement trends from shared emergency toward separate choice. The contemporary machine person's request remains the last widening action.

**Dependencies:** U2–U3 calendar; worker casualty/employment ledger; injury continuity; message provenance; sequel boundary.

**Unchanged protections:** Jas corrects liberation; Morrow refuses entry and gives aid; “Care arrived without entry”; Orison's clinic is real; Saint Varo accepts six entries without custody relation; the sender proves presence, not benevolence or kinship.

## Proposal 13 — Conduct a reader-load compression pass after scene architecture locks

**Priority:** P2

**Exact anchor:** whole manuscript, with concentration on early technical chapters, `S2–S5`, and `B1–U1`.

**Intervention:** After the new scene ledger is drafted, perform three mechanical audits:

### Term audit

For every specialist term, mark first concrete use, first consequential use, and first shorthand use. If two terms occupy the same reader function before they diverge, delay one. Do not add a glossary to compensate for scene-level ambiguity.

### Procedure audit

Mark every occurrence of assent, terms, office transfer, provenance preservation, non-absolution, and separate decision. Keep full procedure only at first establishment, violation, material change, or scale change. Shorten competent recurrence.

### Ending audit

Across any five consecutive chapters, identify maxim endings, institutional reversals, explicit non-forgiveness statements, and withheld-answer beats. Fewer than three should use the same rhetorical closure. Replace surplus conclusions with changed objects, seating, access, body behavior, or unfinished school work.

**Reader benefit:** Prevents expansion from multiplying the baseline's fatigue patterns. Lets strong repeated language such as “Held” retain ritual force.

**Risk:** Mechanical trimming can cut necessary reinforcement or obscure institutional causality.

**Control:** Cut only after owners, inputs, outputs, and consequences are stable. Preserve one clear causal explanation for each late authority action.

**Dependencies:** Complete chapter draft; terminology map; prose editor pass; protected-line check.

**Unchanged protections:** No protected line is moved merely to save it. No ambiguity is resolved by glossary fiat. Distributed causality remains inspectable.

## Global Dependencies

- The coordinator must settle scene transactions and POV ownership before prose expansion.
- Character packets must name bounded recurring peers, former Fist members, and family/contact surfaces; reader experience cannot be achieved with anonymous recurrence.
- Injury, access, object, and relationship state must persist across calendar intervals.

- The authority map must identify the three receipt owners and conjunction without creating a shared mastermind page inside the story world.
- The final compression pass must occur after, not before, full-year recurrence exists.

## Unchanged Protections

All proposals preserve the following:

- Harrowglass opens the novel and establishes chosen dispersal for the reader.
- Calyx remains shorter than Meridian, runs backward in presentation, and ends only at full Bright Ledger Concordance.
- Corresponding-role pairs are reader comparisons and institutional impositions, never reincarnation.
- Meridian teaches real craft, offers real care and futures, and remains worth grieving.
- The No That Moves is incomplete and costly, not finished doctrine or full Concordance.
- AFTER remains one bounded, continuous, socially uneven person capable of fear, omission, injury, repair, refusal, and irreversible loss.
- Iri's refusal may worsen outcomes and still stands.
- Senn/Rhea and Lio/Iri remain unresolved relationships.
- Tem may believe Lio without trusting her; Mika's disposition remains unknown.
- Vey's love and complicity remain simultaneously material.
- Institutional harm remains distributed across narrow authorities.
- Palisade workers own their lives, mandates, evidence, access, and escape conditions.
- Existing clinic lots are not fresh production.
- Necro launches through three preauthorized receipts, not a newly revealed villain.
- The ending opens contemporary relationship rather than restoring Calyx or awarding custody.
