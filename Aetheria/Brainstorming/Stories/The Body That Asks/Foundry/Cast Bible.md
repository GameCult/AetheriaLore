## Competence Boundaries

- Omi owns local component propulsion surfaces and later learns separation geometry.
- Lio owns drive load, high-load balance, and receiving/yielding crown.
- Tem owns perception and learns the bounded whole-body propulsion-vector office.
- Senn owns integration history and temporary whole-body balance when holding crown.
- Iri owns joint integrity, repair, rescue, and local bodily stop.

Office transfer moves decision authority and relevant perception. It does not transfer accumulated component skill, physical tolerance, or the whole person.

## Guardrails

- Every cadet needs at least one consequential action driven by appetite, vanity, attraction, fatigue, jealousy, or play rather than their thematic office.
- Iri must acquire a Tem-facing bond or conflict.
- Tem's playful fabrication, sincere uncertainty, imposed contradictory record, and strategic concealment remain morally distinct.
- Omi's exact refusal can transfer risk downward and is not automatically pure.
- Lio's gratitude remains perceptive; only the inference from care to authority changes.
- Senn's centrality cannot reinstall permanent crown through POV gravity.
