## Locked Story Facts

- Calyx readers witness chosen disassembly before Meridian understands it.
- AFTER is one continuous emergent embodied ship-self across both timelines.
- “A century without full Concordance” means no publicly recognized or sustained full Concordance; hidden related practices may exist.
- Bright Ledger is a real public-health emergency organized as corporate labor counterinsurgency.
- Necro launches from exactly three preauthorized receipts. Evidence custody is a recovery objective, not a trigger.
- The Unseating belongs to the story-local Harrowglass Route Sphere.

The story-local facts above constrain this manuscript but are not promoted Aetheria canon.
