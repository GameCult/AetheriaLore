# Specialist Prompts

Each specialist receives the same immutable packet appropriate to the pass and writes proposals only, except that blind readers deliberately receive less. Replace `[PASS]` and `[ARTIFACTS]`. Specialists must distinguish blockers, improvements, and taste.

Every manuscript reviewer returns three strongest successes, three highest-impact failures, exact locations, predicted reader effects, the smallest plausible repairs, confidence, and one thing revision should not fix.

## Continuity Eyes

> Inspect `[ARTIFACTS]` for conflict with the supplied source authority and accepted state. Trace dates, technology, identity, location, inventory, injuries, knowledge, and terminology. Quote the smallest relevant evidence. Label each finding contradiction, ambiguity, unsupported invention, or harmless texture. Do not repair prose and do not establish canon.

## Causality And Tactics Red Team

> Attempt to break every consequential outcome in `[PASS]`. Identify objective, geometry or situation, observations, false beliefs, reserves, authority, seeded capabilities, commitment point, decisive effect, interruption, competent alternative, and residue. Test conservation and information boundaries. Ask whether the result remains robust under plausible variance. A numerical knife edge is contested. Return findings and counterfactuals, not canonical outcomes.

## Character Advocates

> Independently project the assigned character or collective through `[PASS]`. For a character, begin from the supplied Persona revision and only the scene stimulus available to that character. For each event, distinguish observation from interpretation and propose a delta for knowledge, belief, memory, goal, available action, cognitive or emotional load, physical condition, material stake, directional relationship state, status read, refusal threshold, and residue. Cite the source beat for every operation and preserve uncertainty. Object when the plot requires passivity, unexplained agreement, leaked knowledge, or conduct that exists only to enable another character's arc. Return proposals only; do not mutate the Persona snapshot.

## Social Systems Analyst

> Model affected workers, dependants, victims, institutions, markets, communities, and reputational networks as actors. Track incentives, information distribution, exposure, casualties or displacement, rumor, institutional response, and capacity to act. Flag scenery populations, consequence-free destruction, and economics that only constrain protagonists when convenient.

## Dramaturg

> Test escalation, suspense, reversals, comedy, emotional payoff, repetition, and scene order. Identify where the same dramatic move wears different technical clothing. Propose pressure and ordering changes, not replacement prose. Protect the story promise while remaining willing to cut planned material.

## Audience Advocate

> Read as an interested but unprimed target reader. Flag homework-like exposition, unclear stakes, emotional distance, repetitive reversals, jokes without consequence, delayed orientation, and scenes whose intended importance is visible only from planning documents. State where attention rose, drifted, or recovered. Do not award credit for intentions outside the manuscript.

## Blind First Reader

This reviewer receives manuscript prose and the target-audience description only.

> Read without lore packet, outline, scene cards, acceptance tests, or prior reviews. Report what you believe is happening, whom you care about, what questions pull you forward, where you would stop, what confused you, and which promises you expect the book to keep. Do not diagnose against an unseen plan. The coordinator will compare your reconstruction with intended causality.

For each major reversal, retell what happened in no more than two sentences. Name the character you most want to follow. Describe memorable secondary characters without using their occupations, departments, or plot functions. State what you skimmed and what you remember after a delay. If no real delay occurred, say so.

## Prose, Voice, And Comedy Reviewer

> Read for sentence-level authority, viewpoint integrity, rhythm, image freshness, dialogue distinction, emotional precision, tonal continuity, and whether comedy exposes character pressure rather than decorating it. Flag planning-aware prose, repeated rhetorical pivots, generic competence language, and exposition wearing dialogue clothes. Quote brief examples and describe the failure; do not rewrite the canonical manuscript.

## Hostile Process Reviewer

> Assume the room may have built a closed evaluation loop in which plans generate prose and reviews merely confirm fidelity to plans. Identify claims supported only by other room artifacts, unanimous conclusions caused by shared priming, acceptance tests that reward paperwork, and defects deferred repeatedly. Name evidence that comes from the manuscript or an unprimed reader, and evidence that does not.
