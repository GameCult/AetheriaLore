# Revision Council Passes

This file defines the artifacts and gates for the Revision Council Master Prompt.

## Revision Brief

Before council work, record:

- manuscript identity and version;
- intended audience and reading context;
- artistic promise and ending promise;
- defining strengths to preserve;
- known concerns and operator observations;
- non-negotiable facts, arcs, relationships, and ambiguities;
- acceptable scope of structural change;
- target publication form;
- optional compression hypothesis, explicitly not a quota;
- sideband authority and source order.

For *The Burden of Proof*, protected strengths include distributed authority, materialized ethics, technical credibility, Pal's consent/custody/chosen-presence arc, Luce's incomplete moral development, the four ships as successive institutional arguments, worker and dependant agency, Corvid plurality, and the ending's provisional quiet. Story-specific protected material belongs in its Revision Brief, not in the reusable master prompt.

## Phase 0 Artifacts

- immutable baseline manuscript;
- chapter and movement inventory;
- sideband index;
- cold-reader raw report;
- revision evidence limitations.

**Gate:** The baseline can be reconstructed exactly. At least one reader response exists that was not primed by the revision theory, or the absence is explicit.

## Phase 1 Editorial Reports

Every diagnostic specialist returns:

1. concise diagnosis;
2. three strongest successes;
3. five to ten high-impact findings;
4. chapter-by-chapter interventions within remit;
5. cuts, consolidations, relocations, or expansions proposed;
6. material that must not be damaged;
7. dependencies and conflicts for coordinator adjudication;
8. ranked essential, high-value, and optional actions;
9. confidence and evidence location;
10. one recommendation another editor is likely to make that should be resisted.

For every major recommendation include location, problem, intervention, expected reader benefit, possible collateral damage, manuscript evidence, and relevant sideband evidence where permitted.

### Structural Editor

Owns pacing, repetition, movement architecture, compression, scene necessity, aftermath proportion, and distinct escalation. Produces a ten-beat structural spine, indispensable scenes, duplicated work, movable material, missing hinges, chapter reduction hypotheses, and a realistic overall range.

### Character And POV Editor

Owns emotional hierarchy, dramatic agency, POV integrity, voice differentiation, character recall, and foreground cast by movement. Tests whether characters speak from profession, history, body, desire, and misconception rather than one shared philosophical intelligence.

### Theme And Subtext Editor

Owns repeated interpretation, over-explanation, title-motif pressure, thematic development, and productive ambiguity. Classifies chapter material as new development, necessary reinforcement, productive variation, redundant proof, or explicit interpretation that action can carry.

### Systems And Continuity Editor

Owns chronology, knowledge, inventory, authority, bodily state, technical systems, law, economics, institutions, and setup/payoff conservation. Produces a continuity delta, timeline, authority-by-verb matrix, systems table where relevant, claims map where relevant, cut warnings, and verification checklist. It does not require retaining a redundant scene when its causal setup can move cleanly.

### Culture And Collective-Personhood Editor

Owns any culture, species, collective, or nonhuman perspective at risk of functioning mainly as a test for the dominant cast. Protects opacity, internal disagreement, non-instrumental life, translation boundaries, individual consent, and cultural stakes not created by protagonists. The specific remit is supplied by the Revision Brief.

### Prose And Dialogue Editor

Owns rhythm, exposition, rhetorical repetition, aphorism density, dialogue texture, emergency speech, humor, scene endings, sensory register, and high-value lines. Provides no more than ten isolated sample edits during diagnosis.

### Reader Experience Editor

Receives manuscript prose and target-audience description, not sideband or intended fixes. Owns orientation, cast and term load, emotional center, momentum, tactical legibility, modulation, fatigue, recall, and desire to continue.

## Phase 2 Synthesis Artifacts

- council synthesis with disagreements and rulings;
- protected-material ledger;
- deletion, consolidation, relocation, and expansion lists;
- movement emotional foregrounds;
- chapter revision blueprint;
- dependency graph;
- writing assignment map;
- continuity verification plan;
- line-edit brief deferred until structure stabilizes.

Each chapter brief records current and revised dramatic function, POV purpose, essential event or decision, emotional foreground, material to preserve, material to cut or move, authorized additions, voice objective, subtext objective, sideband dossier links, entrance Persona revisions, expected character-delta surfaces, continuity dependencies, transition obligations, and expected effect on length without prescribing a quota.

**Gate:** Every writing assignment has one chapter steward, a non-overlapping span, an accepted brief, and a bounded sideband dossier. Conflicting council recommendations are adjudicated before prose work. Later passes return to the same steward for substantial rewriting.

## Phase 2A Subject-Writer Proposals

Each council specialty produces a chapter-by-chapter proposal packet. Every affected chapter receives exact anchors, concrete intervention, subject rationale, grounding, expected benefit, collateral risk, priority, and sample language where useful. Every considered protected chapter receives an explicit `No change` disposition.

These writers are subject-obsessed on purpose. They do not compromise with one another or rewrite complete chapters. Their disagreement is retained as evidence.

## Phase 2B Coordinator Adjudication

The coordinator enumerates every substantive proposal and rules `Accept`, `Modify`, or `Reject`. Modified rulings contain the exact instruction the steward may execute. Rejections remain inspectable. The resulting chapter brief identifies proposal sources, controlling continuity constraints, protected material, and dependencies.

**Gate:** No steward begins or resumes prose until every proposal affecting its span has a disposition. A steward may not silently synthesize raw council disagreement.

## Phases 3-7 Candidate Artifacts

For each assignment retain:

- source span identity;
- immutable writing packet;
- candidate revised prose;
- optional alternate only where the brief contains a real fork;
- cut/move/add ledger;
- grounding note with sideband sources used;
- proposed character deltas with character, branch, parent Persona revision, evidence beat, and intended operations;
- writer uncertainties and refused instructions;
- coordinator adjudication;
- integrated manuscript commit or revision identity.

**Gate:** The candidate is not canonical. Integration occurs before any dependent packet is assembled.

## Phase 8 Regression Report

Each specialist answers only:

- Which essential original findings are resolved?
- Which remain unresolved?
- What new problems were introduced?
- Which protected strengths survived or weakened?
- What further changes are essential rather than preferable?
- What should now be left alone?

The coordinator creates a finite final-edit list after reconciling implementation regression with a fresh manuscript-only read. A settled structural issue reopens only with new manuscript evidence of causal failure, reader failure, or material damage. After final edits, the promotion candidate receives a complete systems and continuity verification.

## Objective Checks

Use checks tied to the actual brief. Examples:

- no chapter contains several characters stating the same thematic conclusion;
- each recurring POV has identifiable perceptual, syntactic, and emotional habits;
- every climax constraint has perceptible prior setup;
- an individual's refusal is never translated into collective consent;
- every aftermath section creates a new consequence or earns compression;
- an explicit motif does not repeat meaning already carried by action;
- cuts do not silently alter count, authority, chronology, injury, inventory, knowledge, or claim priority;
- every added cultural beat changes relationship, choice, understanding, or future action rather than supplying encyclopedia texture;
- line revision does not restore explanatory gloss removed during structural work;
- blind readers can name memorable characters without relying only on jobs.

These checks detect named risks. They do not certify literary quality.
