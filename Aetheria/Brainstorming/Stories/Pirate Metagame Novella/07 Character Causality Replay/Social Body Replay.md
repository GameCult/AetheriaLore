### Pal's Private Sanctuary Exchange

Ship Mutiny gives Pal an air-gapped extraction token, a neutral cooling berth, independent legal custody, and a deadline of 2677.09.15. The token can be activated only by Pal's continuity shell; Luce, Sima, and the ship network cannot inspect its traffic or cancel it.

Pal answers privately: **not yet**. They state that treatment coordination, injured-crew obligations, and the urge to settle Luce's consequences all feel materially real, but they cannot distinguish chosen solidarity from learned anticipation of the family's needs. They instruct the circle not to reserve the berth beyond the deadline and not to interpret silence as a decision to stay. This is a postponement with acknowledged conditioning, not a free choice in disguise.

Ship Mutiny accepts the answer and withholds claimant support until Pal speaks again without Luce. The expiring option prevents the final operation from treating Pal's presence as settled.

## Claimant Coalition At Pass End

The coalition is actionable but not harmonious:

- injured target workers seek treatment;
- captured crews seek back wages and cleared stigma;
- Undertow seeks labor notes and blacklisting compensation;
- dependants demand cash rather than heroic equity;
- Kavi seeks care-bond release and testimony ownership;
- Olan seeks maintenance and derivative compensation;
- Ship Mutiny seeks Pal's removal from fixture law;
- Orsino pension holders fear indiscriminate claim voiding;
- Wake seeks precedent without becoming adjudicator;
- some Halcyon workers possess evidence while depending on employment;
- Corvid nests seek juvenile freedom and return of authored records.

They can cooperate around enforceable distribution while disagreeing about Luce, piracy, and one another. That disagreement is a condition of the final victory, not an obstacle the climax must dissolve.
