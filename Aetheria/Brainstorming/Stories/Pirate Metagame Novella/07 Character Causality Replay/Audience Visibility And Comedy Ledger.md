# Audience Visibility And Comedy Ledger

Sideband continuity is not a page-order. This ledger limits what the reader must consciously process. Passes 8-9 may promote latent material only by demoting something else of comparable weight.

## Visibility Classes

- **Dramatized:** the reader witnesses the choice in scene.
- **Reported:** consequence arrives through testimony, invoice, gossip, repair, treatment, or argument.
- **Latent:** changes later conduct without explanation at the moment it occurs.
- **Cuttable:** preserves causal continuity but may never enter prose.

Each campaign receives one primary dispute, one secondary complication, and no more than three externally visible decision owners. Other people still act; the manuscript need not salute each action as it passes.

## Campaign VI Presentation Rule

The visible technical question is:

> The gun knows where the target is. Does it know what that point means?

Rruk asks for disconnection. Sable refuses. Jo confirms the shot is physically real. Samira says the agreed deadline has passed. Luce orders withdrawal. The energy consequence receives one image: their precision weapon stored its violence inside their own ship before sending any of it away. Confidence provenance, architecture attribution, rescue seniority, and fixture collision arrive later through symptoms and custody terms.
