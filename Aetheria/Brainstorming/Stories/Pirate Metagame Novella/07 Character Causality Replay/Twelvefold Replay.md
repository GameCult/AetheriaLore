# Twelvefold Replay

This is authoritative sideband state for all twelve members of the Twelvefold Borrowed Nest. Human-facing scenes use only `Huginn` and `Muninn`. Pal and Edda's protected systems hold twelve opaque rescue IDs, current medical requirements, and location beacons. They do **not** expose true syrinx-names, autobiographical poems, parliament roles, human-facing appearance history, or a mapping a Pal or Edda POV can decode. Exact identities remain available to members, the parliament, and the eventual `POV: Huginn` interiority. Collective opacity protects persons; it does not dissolve them.

Each campaign entry records location, knowledge, goal, available action or refusal, body/cache state, consent, relationship residue, and reason for continued participation.
