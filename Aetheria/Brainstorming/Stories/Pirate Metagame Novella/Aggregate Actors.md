## Social-State Variables

Every campaign updates each affected aggregate across:

`information distribution`, `economic exposure`, `injury or displacement`, `rumor`, `institutional response`, `capacity to act`, `attitude toward crew`, and `claims on future decisions`.

No aggregate is treated as unanimous. Named representatives carry authority only where the ledger explicitly grants it.
