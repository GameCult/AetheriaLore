# Movement II Skeptical Review Report

Four independent reviewers examined Movement II from audience, continuity/tactics, character/social-agency, and prose/voice perspectives. The initial complete draft was 6,487 words. Review-driven expansion and correction produced a 14,027-word movement.

## Audience Reconciliation

- Sustained Hull One’s evacuation through a failed-pressure crawl, Dena’s rescue, Pal’s control sacrifice, memory triage, and overcrowded lifeboat aftermath.
- Expanded the rescue certificate through Dena’s surgery, Lorn’s departure, worker testimony, and Undertow’s collective refusal.
- Turned Hull Two move-in into lived ensemble space with loud pumps, privacy conflict, Dena’s limited mobility, Corvid cache authority, and Pal’s shell placement.
- Made Hull Two’s recoverability tempting through personal-object delays, rotating gravity, prepared volunteer salvage, and a visible missed return window.
- Expanded Pal’s mundane remote farewell and Hull Three’s construction, renewal, first night, clean capture, attack, local-control aftermath, compartment handover, and sanctuary option.
- Removed the duplicate Hull Three firing description and reduced explanatory technical gloss.

The movement remains lean relative to the 22,700 planning target. Final revision must add approximately 3,000-4,000 earned words across the whole manuscript if Movement III does not bring the combined draft above 50,000. Expansion may not be replaced by doctrine explanation.

## Prose And Voice Reconciliation

- Expanded rooms after decisions rather than adding more thesis.
- Preserved distinct sensory identities: Hull One’s sweet fire and disappearing body, Hull Two’s noise and living abandonment, Hull Three’s invasive quiet and orderly dispossession.

## Acceptance

Movement II is emotionally complete and tactically coherent. It ends with Pal holding a real private exit and the Twelvefold granting only a narrow continuation. Its remaining issue is whole-manuscript length, explicitly reserved for the unified adversarial revision rather than hidden.
