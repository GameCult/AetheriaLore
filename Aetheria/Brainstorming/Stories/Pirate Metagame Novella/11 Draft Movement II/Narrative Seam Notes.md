## Confirmed Invariants

- Detection need not resolve exact identity if an attacker can constrain all plausible future routes.
- Fire control can remain physically correct while target meaning becomes adversarially unreliable.
- Better cognition expands the surface available to architecture-specific attack.
- Rescue, possession, title, and personhood are separate transitions.
- Three hull losses should preserve distinct state transitions: destruction, abandonment, and dispossession.
