# Strategy Dossiers

Pass 5 auditions seven doctrines as narrative engines. None is selected as a complete campaign merely by appearing here. Each must seduce, work, teach the wrong lesson, and expose a different pressure on the crew.

## 1. Reversible Superiority

### Seduction

Luce's argument is unusually good. Smart weapons made hesitation survivable by allowing commitment to remain open until terminal range; recoverable PDC drones extend the same logic to the defensive response. They can fly toward unresolved traces, kill cheap eyes, force expensive reveals, and return when the contact proves harmless. A mistaken launch becomes service work instead of a corpse or a ruined premium munition.

Sable improves the task lattice, Chak designs return geometry, Samira insists on a twenty-two-percent ordinary reserve, and deck labor demonstrates excellent staggered turnaround. Luce persuades the campaign council to authorize twelve percent for one bounded eight-drone high-tempo trial with no unresolved contacts outside the screened volume; Samira records a formal objection and retains withdrawal authority. The success encourages him to seek the same exception for a full-wing sortie in a contact field that violates the trial assumptions. This is informed amateurism in its purest form: he accounts for every moving machine and forgets that all of them eventually come home to the same doors.

### Audition

Against a sparse picket screen, the doctrine is magnificent. Courtesy drones force an escort to show its hand, capture a relay, and recover with nobody dead. The deck crew services scorch marks while Luce gives an interview to nobody in particular about making violence fiscally reversible.

The validation teaches the wrong lesson. When a later opponent feeds the screen heterogeneous cheap contacts, the drones still win their local exchanges. The opponent is not trying to win those exchanges. It maps the return fan and places loiterers against recovery lanes. More cognition improves triage and makes the loss exquisitely well organized.

### Character Work

The first success makes the crew briefly enjoy his money. The failure establishes his invariant: he abandons Courtesy hulls and the capture rather than sacrifice deck crews to save the service neighborhood. Samira begins separating his bad strategic judgment from his reliable crisis ethics. Sable experiences the insult of having the correct schedule and the wrong physical plant. Pass 5 does not yet claim this engagement destroys Hull One.

### Economic Work

Recoverability changes launch behavior and produces a real favorable matchup. It also concentrates expensive labor, spares, coolant, ammunition, and maintenance. The campaign's captured value cannot cover the beautiful hole made in the drone deck.

## 2. Deep Quiet

### Seduction

After visible recovery geometry kills his first revelation, Luce decides the real error was allowing the enemy any history at all. Deep quiet makes every action an exercise in debt, discipline, and timing. A pirate who waits longer than respectable security can afford may arrive at attachment range without creating a conventional battle.

Ilya turns the slogan into operating doctrine. Samira removes corrective burns from the plan. Edda defines thermal floors below which machinery and crew become unreliable. The Twelvefold distributes passive observation across cold perches. Pal turns domestic systems into a miserly heat budget and remembers three childhood hobbies in which Luce discovered asceticism immediately after breaking something expensive.

### Audition

The favorable target is a schedule-bound escort whose legal radiator and thrust rhythm is more reliable than its sensors. A cold Remora waits where the escort must eventually put itself. The capture is quiet, profitable, and deeply unpleasant for the target.

The wrong lesson is that patience is a weapon without recoil. In truth, heat debt, stale information, crew discomfort, augmentation shutdown, deferred maintenance, and sacrificed maneuver accumulate. An opponent need not find the ship. It can vary schedules, shed cheap unclassified objects, and remain productive under uncertainty until the pirates must restore atmosphere, wake cognition, dump heat, or abandon prepositioned assets. The deep-quiet ship can remain hidden or remain capable; it cannot indefinitely do both.

### Character Work

Ilya gains authority and then has to spend it telling Luce that discomfort is not evidence of seriousness. Rin Adao's gas-analysis overlay begins translating its own oxygen debt as a safe compartment, turning a systems cost into one familiar body. Pal closes part of their hull awareness but refuses Luce's unspoken hope that they discard recent sensory memory into a detachable false continuation. Luce accepts the refusal without treating affection as permission. Edda declares the physiological limit; Samira aborts, loses the prepositioned assets, and accepts a Navigator notation that she abandoned a nearly matured capture. The Twelvefold's embodied “Cold Choir” carries observations between isolated passive stations by flight and narrow optical exchange, advancing both the mission and private cache mapping.

### Economic Work

Quiet time consumes storage media, detachable sinks, maintenance life, and opportunities. The prize looks profitable until Tess prices the discarded thermal hardware and the work lost during recovery.

## 3. Shaped Heat Ferries

### Seduction

Deep quiet loses when the enemy refuses to behave predictably. Luce's answer is to stop hiding the scene and begin authoring it. Heat ferries can spend carried thermal debt imitating plausible ships, wounded drives, legal dumps, relays, or spent sinks. The parent can perform a cousin within its own envelope. Battle becomes a kaleidoscopic hidden-object puzzle in which the pirate decides which objects deserve attention.

Ilya polices physical plausibility. Qerr composes emission history. Rruk and Sable insist on architecture isolation. The doctrine becomes less “make a fake ship” and more “present several honest-enough explanations whose resolution costs the defender time, heat, and cognition.”

### Audition

It works against an escort whose cargo-protection policy requires it to inspect apparent distress before firing through the convoy volume. Bounded false histories draw active apertures and patient weapons away from the parent's approach; ordinary correction traffic and aspect changes reveal the command relay. An architecture-tailored puzzle adds delay but does not compel the response. Luce is delighted to discover that electronic warfare can be smug.

The hard counter is an opponent that refuses the intellectual contest. Halcyon fields a deliberately coarse classifier, architecture diversity, and a human rule: protect the principal, illuminate everything, do not solve the puzzle. The ferries spend heat constructing a scene nobody agrees to read. The parent inherits the unpaid thermal debt.

### Character Work

The Twelvefold's artistry becomes operationally indispensable without becoming captain-owned equipment. Sable must accept that a less intelligent architecture can be more robust precisely because it declines the offered work. Luce confronts the difference between controlling a performance and controlling its audience.

### Economic Work

The first success creates a market for the emission family and therefore its counter. Heat ferries are cheap only if nobody counts carried heat, metamaterial wear, lost recovery, and the legal consequences of performing distress.

## 4. Patient Hand Remorae

### Seduction

After a doctrine fails because the enemy simply refuses to interpret, Luce turns from appearance to contact. Remorae promise the whole pirate economy in one chassis: reach the prize, attach, and attack exactly the subsystem that makes resistance intolerable while preserving hull, cargo, and people.

Mara specifies what surrender geometry must look like to bodies in compartments. Sable narrows autonomy. Pal draws a conduct boundary around life support. Edda builds antidote and casualty branches. The result can target sensors, safing, locks, datanets, reactor controls, thermal plant, or crew environment without pretending those effects are morally interchangeable.

### Audition

Against a ship with exposed sensor and safing dependencies, Patient Hands sever confident resistance and open surrender channels. The prize survives. Luce calls it violence with manners.

The misleading validation is property preservation. A later target has isolated networks, manual safing, sacrificial buses, hull-clearing charges, and a crew willing to damage its own ship. Several Remorae report successful control because they have entered deliberately false maintenance segments. One apparent service route shares pressure authority with a passenger refuge. Sable can reconcile the machine reports faster than Mara can physically verify them; Mara refuses entry and Luce backs the abort. Non-destructive intrusion can preserve property while terrifying, surveilling, or cognitively violating people. The hard counter is partly technical and partly a refusal to let the pirates define “harm” around resale value.

### Character Work

Mara protects an augmented captive during a boarding crisis because the person is under her authority and therefore hers to keep alive, not because her politics have improved on schedule. Sable and Mara fight over whether remote risk is mercy or class privilege. Pal refuses an efficient life-support action. Luce learns that intact is not synonymous with unharmed.

### Economic Work

Captured value is high until quarantine, evidence, specialist medicine, damaged trust, and prize decontamination are counted. Insurers learn to price subsystem topology and manual isolation.

## 5. Premium Cognition And Precision Energy

### Seduction

If subsystem capture becomes ethically and operationally messy, Luce proposes an almost Platonic answer: know more, decide faster, and place energy exactly where it ends the fight. Lasers exchange onboard reactor power and heat for pinpoint effect. Premium cognition improves sensing, classification, command, fire control, guidance, and damage control. Better decisions across the whole stack should mean fewer rounds, fewer mistakes, and fewer frightened boarders.

Jo ensures that “cycle” means cycle rounds on target, not merely make the mechanism busy. Sable allocates cognition. Ilya protects confidence labels. Edda budgets optical and reactor heat. The stack is genuinely excellent.

### Audition

Against a conventional ship with concentrated sensor and drive control, precise shots disable resistance before a ballistic exchange matures. It looks like intelligence abolishing mess.

The wrong lesson is that enough cognition turns uncertainty into a solvable queue. A countering ship generates cheap contradictory events, presents sacrificial surfaces, attacks the architecture's assumptions, and fires ballistic patterns that demand simultaneous defense. The premium system knows more than anyone has ever known about why it cannot finish every task before deadline.

### Character Work

Sable receives the victory and owns the failure. Growth does not require renouncing enhancement; it requires allowing slower judgment and distributed refusal to interrupt premium cognition. When contamination first appears, Sable delays Pal's proposed confidence fuses and Slow Vote long enough to waste one recoverable firing cycle. Pal then isolates classification from firing geometry; command takes a deliberately separated judgment from Jo, Mara, and Rruk. During the later mixed-cloud partition, Sable voluntarily delegates local coordination instead of repeating the delay. Jo discovers that a beautiful solution assigned too late is merely an accurate description of where the target used to be.

### Economic Work

Energy precision saves ammunition mass while consuming reactor, capacitor, cooling, optical maintenance, and proprietary cognition support. Tavi Ro becomes a campaign dependency. Halcyon needs only to make every decision expensive, not outthink Sable in the abstract.

## 6. Hot Ballistic Commitment

### Seduction

After being defeated by clutter designed for clever machines, Luce proposes the doctrine everyone assumes he started with: large guns, large reactor, armor, redundant systems, and radiators glowing like the sun while death is shone upon cowards trying to hide. It is funny because it is also correct.

Jo turns bravado into volume-fire doctrine. Samira defines violent translation windows. Edda makes overdrive survivable instead of theatrical. Mara approves of machinery whose danger is visible. Pal notes that Luce has finally discovered a hobby appropriate to his natural subtlety.

### Audition

Hot doctrine wins when quiet attackers need more time to identify and commit than the armored ship needs to make their likely volumes lethal. Redundant fire control keeps rounds on intended solutions after damage. Armor and repairable systems convert first-hit lethality into a brutal interruption race.

The wrong lesson is not that heat is irrelevant. It is that commitment can substitute for selection. A patient opponent attacks radiator roots, exhausts magazines with false volumes, pressures several repair neighborhoods, or simply declines the fight. A decoy threat can draw the Hot ship away while the real objective matures elsewhere. It may win every exchange it reaches and still lose the contract.

### Character Work

Jo gets an uncomplicated moment of joy. Luce earns credibility by embracing an inelegant doctrine and then ruins the moment by commissioning monogrammed recoil furniture. Samira later refuses his attractive pursuit because convoy protection, not capture, is the objective; Halcyon circulates the refusal as evidence that she lacks offensive nerve, narrowing one credible exit from Luce's crew. The crew learns he can adopt an idea culturally opposed to his self-image. The next lesson is that honesty about one's signature does not make every engagement worth taking.

### Economic Work

Ballistics export energy by spending mass. Ammunition, magazines, propellant, barrel wear, radiator repair, and transport make each glorious minute a procurement event. The moron strategy remains valid and cannot finance itself through indiscriminate use.

## 7. Mixed Drone Clouds

### Seduction

Every previous doctrine failed by becoming legible. Mixed clouds promise not one perfect role but many imperfect options: PDC drone, relay, heat ferry, spotter, beacon, illuminator, mine, Remora, terminal weapon, decoy, or recoverable scout. Components define capability; orders define present role.

Sable builds bounded retasking. The Twelvefold insists that contractual consent cannot be inherited from a hull's previous job. Mara demands a human-risk branch. Pal limits integration paths into the parent. Luce sees a tactical orchestra. Everyone else sees a scheduling dispute that can accelerate hard enough to kill them.

### Audition

A defensive PDC element drives offensively against enemy drones, forces a reveal, and locks down a battlespace while relays preserve track and Remorae exploit the opening. Leaning on a normally defensive capability wins a real matchup.

The wrong lesson is that flexibility cannot be countered. Every option consumes the resource that kept another option available. Retasking creates cognition load, authorization delay, link dependence, service conflict, and ambiguous recovery. An opponent can ignore identity, define consequence volumes, strike the parent, break links, or force contradictory tasks faster than the coalition can consent and verify. Mara withholds two rescue-capable platforms while Sable wants their robust handling systems used as relays. Preserving the reserve helps lose the capture; after fragment fire damages the launch blister, those same platforms recover two deck technicians. The allegedly unused capability turns out to have been performing its job.

### Character Work

This audition contains material for the final synthesis but cannot become the answer by itself. Sable voluntarily relinquishes central control during partition. The Twelvefold splits its two votes: the operational mandate carried by Chak favors using one shepherd as a relay, while the contract mandate carried by Tik-tik preserves both rescue craft until the false distress source is resolved. Internally, Kesh, Trraak, Khraa, Vek, Tik-tik, and Ek support the reserve; Chak, Qerr, Tlak, Ikk-rr, and Rruk support bounded relay use; Krrik records the split and refuses to manufacture unanimity. Mara must trust a remote screen while preparing bodily rescue. Luce must learn that command consists partly of declining options he can afford.

### Economic Work

Common hull grammar lowers some manufacturing and training costs while multiplying spares, software, certification, and service combinations. Flexibility is inventory held in several mutually inconvenient forms.
