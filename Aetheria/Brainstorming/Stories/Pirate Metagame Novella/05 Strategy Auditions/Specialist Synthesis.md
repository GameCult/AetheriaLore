# Specialist Synthesis

Three independent doctrine specialists supplied read-only proposals before canonical adjudication.

## Adopted Contributions

- Hot doctrine can win every exchange it reaches while losing the protected objective elsewhere.
- Deep Quiet accumulates social, physiological, and information debt, not only heat.
- A bounded council trial resolves the twelve-percent Courtesy reserve without nullifying Samira's authority.
- Shaped heat ferries perform mutually plausible histories; architecture-specific attraction may add load but cannot compel behavior.
- Remorae use distributed local authority and can be countered by false maintenance topology, physical isolation, local confirmation, and passenger coupling.
- Mixed clouds retain physical capability envelopes. Consequence-volume defense attacks shared geometry rather than solving every role.
- A protected rescue reserve is useful because it remains uncommitted; spending it as a relay may win the fight and lose the rescue.
- Premium cognition requires confidence provenance between classification and firing geometry; slower independent judgment can resist shared contamination.

## Rejected Or Narrowed Contributions

- No audition received a first-hull loss without a separate robust damage chain.
- No architecture-specific emission guarantees an enemy decision.
- No drone changes into a role unsupported by installed hardware.
- No exact performance margin became canon because it appeared in a specialist proposal.
- Not all seven doctrines are promised equal-length campaign cycles.
