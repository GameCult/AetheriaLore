# Skeptical Review Report

Pass 5 received independent audience, continuity/tactics, and character/social-agency reviews. Reviewers submitted findings only; the coordinator retained sole write and canon authority.

## Audience Review

### Findings

- Several doctrines shared physically similar counters and could become repetitive as full campaigns.
- Samira lacked costly refusals.
- Support labor remained anonymous.
- Deep Quiet risked reading as a list of debuffs.
- Mixed clouds carried too many simultaneous explanations.
- Pal observed Luce's pattern but did not exercise embodiment choice.
- Heat-ferry and premium-cognition failures needed emotional separation.

### Reconciliation

- Pass 6 will select six or seven campaigns without assuming every audition receives a complete cycle.
- Campaign forms are differentiated: evacuation crisis, domestic pressure cooker, confidence trick, boarding refusal, contaminated cognition, contractual defeat, and rescue disguised as tactical failure.
- Samira now aborts Deep Quiet at bodily cost and refuses Hot pursuit at reputational cost.
- Dena Vey, Rin Adao, and Soren Ilyev are recurring support workers before any selected hull loss.
- Pal refuses to sacrifice recent sensory memory; Luce accepts the refusal.
- Mixed clouds center the rescue-reserve choice; service complexity remains aftermath.
- Heat ferries concern performance and the audience's freedom not to believe. Premium cognition concerns contaminated confidence crossing system boundaries.

**Recheck verdict:** Accept for Failure Ladder selection. Selected campaigns must receive complete counter traces in Pass 6.

## Continuity And Tactics Review

- Recovery arithmetic was inconsistent.

- Later doctrines lacked hull/refit boundaries.
- The shaped-ferry success depended too strongly on a provisional cognition exploit.

- Deep Quiet lacked a conserved Remora delivery chain.
- Hot volume fire lacked bounded geometry.
- Patient Hands lacked a failed-capture packet.
- `Combat Model` still described database fixtures as a Pass 5 requirement.

### Reconciliation

- T-02 causes mission loss and recovery-plant damage only. Hull One destruction remains open for Pass 6 and requires a separate coarse chain.
- Every doctrine is assigned to Hull One, a declared refit, or a later hull. Hardware cannot appear through retasking.
- Cargo policy, ordinary geometry, and correction traffic decide the shaped-ferry success. Cognitive attraction only increases delay.
- Samira's twelve-percent reserve is a bounded council exception over formal objection. Jo owns all weapon and payload release.
- Deep Quiet prepositions one stocked sensor/antenna Remora eighteen hours before attachment geometry.
- Hot volume fire occurs inside a constrained extraction corridor with protected traffic and an objective deadline.
- T-05B records false topology, passenger coupling, local controls, hull-clearing charges, Mara's refusal, and target escape.
- Simulation database work is now a promotion requirement, not a novella prerequisite.

**Recheck verdict:** Accept after final arithmetic and authority corrections, which are incorporated.

- Aggregate actors reacted but rarely chose.

- Twelvefold plurality lacked an actual split.

- The Courtesy reserve needed a legitimate command mechanism.

### Reconciliation

- Every doctrine now has a target-side decision and an external decision constraining later play.
- Dena can halt recovery, Rin can reject atmosphere service, and Soren can stop unsafe ammunition feed.
- Target workers cooperate, publish countermeasures, sever systems, refuse gratitude, and control testimony.
- The mixed-cloud vote splits explicitly between Chak's operational relay use and Tik-tik's protected rescue reserve; all twelve internal positions are recorded.

- Mara protects an augmented captive before usefulness is known; the captive refuses debrief and controls publication.
- The council-authorized reserve trial preserves Samira's office and makes Luce's error an overgeneralization rather than covert mutiny.

**Recheck verdict:** Accept. The selected campaigns must continue individual Corvid, support-worker, target, and external-institution state.
