# Theme And Subtext Writer Proposals

## Governing Principle

The manuscript's thematic machinery is sound. Its recurrent weakness is checksum interpretation: an object, refusal, injury, or procedure completes the argument, after which narration or dialogue certifies the meaning. These proposals remove that second authority and return meaning to cups, doors, gauges, contracts, bodies, latches, records, votes, and the pump. They preserve thematic development by cutting only duplicate interpretation or duplicate propagation, not the distinct material transformations performed by the four ships.

Grounding throughout: [[Revision Brief]], accepted [[Coordinator Synthesis]], [[Theme And Subtext Editor Report]], the immutable baseline identified in [[Baseline And Evidence]], and the council-side authority surfaces named below. This file proposes changes only. It does not amend canon or any steward candidate.
