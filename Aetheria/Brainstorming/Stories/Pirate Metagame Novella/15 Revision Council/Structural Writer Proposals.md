## Working Principle

The plot does not need rebuilding. The four ships escalate by changing the kind of loss: concentrated recovery becomes a kill path; distributed repair produces an intact ship that must be abandoned; fused cognition becomes an attack surface; physical local authority survives one narrow contest while preserving the opponent's next move. Revision should protect those losses and compress the setup, certification, and institutional afterlife around them. The preferred whole-book landing is roughly 6-10 percent shorter, earned locally rather than imposed as quota.
