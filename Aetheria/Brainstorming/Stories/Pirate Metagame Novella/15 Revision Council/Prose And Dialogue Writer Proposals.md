# Prose And Dialogue Writer Proposals

## Remit And Method

This packet proposes changes only. It does not amend the canonical manuscript or any steward candidate. The coordinator may accept, modify, relocate, or reject each proposal independently.

The governing subject is verbal pressure: sentence rhythm, speaker-specific voice, emergency speech, comedy, chapter exits, and the removal of interpretive checksums after action has already made the point. Technical and legal precision remains when it changes authority, custody, bodily risk, livelihood, or tactical choice. The aim is not generic naturalism. It is to stop a highly articulate cast from sharing one polished editorial cadence.

Grounding throughout: the immutable baseline in [[Baseline And Evidence]], [[Revision Brief]], accepted [[Coordinator Synthesis]], [[Prose And Dialogue Editor Report]], and relevant character, authority, ship, Corvid, knowledge, and continuity sideband named in the assignment packet. Sample prose is illustrative candidate language, not canon.

## Cross-Manuscript Passes

### Pass A: Checksum removal

- Search `not`, `both`, `correct`, `distinction`, `question`, `recorded`, `nobody`, and abstract conclusions immediately after action.
- Cut only when the sentence adds no causality, authority, elapsed time, knowledge path, or unique voice.
- Concentrate on Chapters 9-18 and 25-27. Preserve enforceable language spoken by Tess, Rhyne, Pal, and Sable when exact wording is itself action.
- **Priority:** Essential.

### Pass B: Speaker-transfer test

- Read polished dialogue without tags. If a line can move unchanged, revise its machinery: Ilya qualifies and relabels; Dena names physical limits; Tess changes fields and liabilities; Jo reduces to firing conditions; Soren names feed work; Samira uses command verbs; Edda triages; Mara patrols boundaries; Sable distinguishes authority and model integrity; Pal notices conditional consequences; Luce names doctrines and wants applause from them.

- **Priority:** Essential.

### Pass C: Emergency speech

- Read Chapters 10, 17, and 27 aloud. During immediate danger, prefer command verb, object, number, location, refusal, repeated failed instruction, bodily interruption, and silence.
- Restore longer legal or philosophical syntax only when a speaker is actively creating a binding record or contesting authority.
- **Priority:** Essential.

### Pass D: Comedy thinning

- In command tables and reviews, preserve the first joke that changes status or exposes work. Cut later rejoinders that merely confirm shared wit.
- Protect failed jokes, professional embarrassment, Corvid asymmetry, name disputes, `bent`, `dakka`, bad tea, and the wheel.
- **Priority:** High-value.

### Pass E: Ending variation

- Protect object-report endings: the table, washroom door, warm latch, and pump.
- Where adjacent chapters end in aphorism or withheld interpretation, remove the explanatory penultimate line or end on an unanswered practical question, bodily need, error, or work continuing.
- Never manufacture variation by weakening a protected final line.
- **Priority:** High-value.
