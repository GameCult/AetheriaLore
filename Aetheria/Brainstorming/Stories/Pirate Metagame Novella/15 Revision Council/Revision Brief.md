# Revision Brief

## Objective

Revise *The Burden of Proof* for stronger pacing, character differentiation, subtext, reader orientation, cultural life, and prose variation while preserving the qualities that made the promoted manuscript successful.

Compression is a hypothesis, not a quota. The attached council plan suggested twelve to eighteen percent, but the council must earn every cut through demonstrated repetition, drag, duplicated interpretation, or unnecessary aftermath. A complete shorter or nearly unchanged book is acceptable.

## Audience

Readers of character-driven science fiction and serial fiction who accept technical and institutional complexity but should not need prior Aetheria lore. The book should remain playful, warm, politically serious, tactically legible, and emotionally generous without becoming conventionalized.

## Protected Identity

- Ethical and political problems remain embodied in machinery, contracts, labor, heat, injury, rescue, and tactical decisions.
- No institution, faction, or character becomes innocent through local correctness.
- Pal's arc remains about consent, care, conditioning, custody, service, and chosen presence.
- Luce improves through limits, relinquishment, and conduct without earning absolution.
- The four principal ships remain successive institutional and technical arguments.
- Distributed authority remains dramatically consequential.
- Workers, dependants, captured crews, artificial persons, and Corvid people act rather than decorate elite moral development.
- Technical specificity remains attached to bodily and institutional consequence.
- The ending preserves Pal's ability to choose temporary presence without converting that choice into permanent service.
- Dry wit, playful argument, alien cultural development, and unusually strong lines survive revision.

## Known Concerns

- The middle may repeat doctrine, validation, counter, rescue, and refit often enough to expose the machine.
- Planning language may survive as explanatory gloss, moral checksums, cast-wide epigrams, and polished conclusions after action.
- Secondary characters may remain memorable primarily by department rather than private appetite.
- The final operation may carry more institutional nouns than its emotional line needs.
- Corvid plurality may be operationally rigorous while remaining too instrumental on the page.
- Chapter and aftermath density may reduce modulation despite local clarity.
