# Systems And Continuity Writer Proposals

## Mandate And Authority

This file proposes revisions for coordinator adjudication. It does not amend the canonical manuscript, accepted story state, or any steward candidate. The manuscript remains owner of depicted events; the accepted [[Coordinator Synthesis]] governs revision; [[Systems And Continuity Editor Report]] and the authoritative sideband ground latent causal, technical, legal, bodily, chronological, resource, authority, and knowledge continuity.

The governing revision principle is narrow: preserve every state transition that later action depends on, while cutting explanations that merely certify a transition already visible. Where compression relocates information, its carrier must move with it. A fact without its witness, record, instrument, contract, body, or authenticated path is not continuity; it is authorial leakage.
