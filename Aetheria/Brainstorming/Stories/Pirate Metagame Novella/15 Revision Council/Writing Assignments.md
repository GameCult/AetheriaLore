# Writing Assignments

## Shared Contract

Each group has one chapter steward. The steward reads the complete baseline manuscript once, the accepted [[Coordinator Synthesis]], the group's source chapters, adjacent chapters, and all linked sideband. The output is candidate prose, never canon.

Every candidate file contains:

1. the complete revised assigned chapters with original Markdown heading structure;
2. a `Grounding Note` after a horizontal rule, naming sideband constraints used;
3. a cut/move/add summary;
4. proposed state deltas, normally none;
5. protected material retained;
6. unresolved contradictions or transition dependencies.

Grounding notes are stripped during manuscript assembly. Writers may not edit the promoted manuscript, ledgers, other candidates, or chapters outside their assignment.

All stewards use:

- `Room Charter.md`
- `Canon Ledger.md`
- `Story State.md`
- `Decision Log.md`
- `Cast Bible.md`
- `Character State Ledger.md`
- `Relationship Matrix.md`
- `Ship And Refit Ledger.md`
- `POV Knowledge Matrix.md`
- `Continuity And Red-Team Reports.md`

Group-specific sources supplement this list.
