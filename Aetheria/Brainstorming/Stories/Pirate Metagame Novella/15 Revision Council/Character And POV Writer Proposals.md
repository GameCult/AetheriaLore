# Character And POV Writer Proposals

## Remit And Method

This packet proposes changes only. It does not amend the canonical manuscript or any steward candidate. The coordinator may approve, modify, relocate, or reject every item independently.

My governing question is not whether each character has a biography in sideband. It is whether the manuscript gives each important person a desire that can be felt before the plot requires it, a body that changes how they know, a voice that cannot be reassigned without damage, and relationship residue that survives the scene which created it. Expansion is funded by nearby cuts to interpretation, repeated doctrine, or institutional catalogues. No proposal creates a new POV.

Knowledge boundary: latent state from [[../Cast Bible]], [[../Relationship Matrix]], [[../Character State Ledger]], [[../Character Causality Replay]], [[../POV Knowledge Matrix]], and [[../07 Character Causality Replay/Twelvefold Replay]] may shape behavior, but enters human knowledge only through witnessed conduct, authorized speech, contract record, or deliberate disclosure. Corvid public opacity remains intact.
