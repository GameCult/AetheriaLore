# Clock Machine Test Vectors

These are hand-solvable deterministic fixtures. Pass 5 will create full tactical traces.

## Vector 1: Sparse Picket Screen

### Initial State

- _The Sum of Our Parts_ begins outside resolved identification range.
- Three genuine passive pickets and one relay occupy a broad screen.
- The defender has no recovery-lane loiterers and uses cautious PDC reveal rules.
- Eight Courtesy drones launch with 22% return reserve.
- Sable, Ilya, Pal and the Twelvefold can process all current tasks below load 0.82.

### Derived Clocks

| Clock | Luce's force | Defender |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| existence track | 34 s to separate four contacts | 51 s to resolve drone parent bearing |
| firing solution | 49 s to useful PDC pressure | 66 s to confident defensive assignment |
| terminal | 71 s to first picket suppression | 93 s to first credible anti-drone intercept |
| heat | 19 min to Courtesy recovery thermal limit | 8 min to forced active/radiator reveal under pressure |
| cognition | no miss before 12 min | classification deadline miss at 58 s |

### Result

Courtesy pressure removes two pickets, forces the relay to transmit, and gives the capture cannon a useful geometry update before the defender can efficiently assign fire. Six drones recover immediately; two loiter cold and return later. Reversible Superiority is genuinely good here.

### Heuristic

Press unresolved sparse screens when contact count, cognition load, and return fan remain below service capacity. Preserve the 22% reserve.

## Vector 2: Heterogeneous Bait And Lane Loiterers

### Initial State

- Six genuine cheap contacts and ten shaped or replay-supported contacts present plausible small-hull behavior.
- Four cold loiterers hold boxes outside the apparent screen and observe return vectors.
- Sixteen Courtesy drones launch with Luce's 12% return reserve.
- The defender avoids fighting the drones except when a short burst deepens the classification problem.
- Friendly cognition begins at load 0.76 and rises with every contact split and contradictory observation.

### Derived Clocks

| Clock | Luce's force | Defender |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| existence/class track | 3 min 12 s to reduce hypotheses | 48 s to estimate parent from outbound arcs |
| terminal | 6 min 20 s to suppress enough contacts | 5 min 07 s to occupy likely recovery lanes |
| heat | 8 min 45 s to first emergency-return drone | 31 min to meaningful thermal constraint |
| cognition | first fire-control/classification miss at 4 min 38 s | no deadline miss before 14 min |
| recovery | 11 min exposed queue after first capture | loiterers retain 2 min 41 s terminal reserve at queue formation |
| economic | recoverability advantage fails after fourth abandoned drone | cheap-contact expenditure remains below one Courtesy replacement |

### Result

The Courtesy drones win several local exchanges and reveal real objects. Their return fan identifies the parent and converges hot, depleted platforms on four throats. Loiterers attack lanes and shared service geometry. Sable improves queue order; the Twelvefold identifies two false contacts; Pal protects coolant priority. None creates another throat. The doctrine loses because the enemy attacks recovery rather than drone combat.

### Heuristic

Do not surge recoverable drones when adversary contact-generation rate exceeds classification throughput and unobserved loiter volume can mature before the return queue clears. Either stagger launch, leave reserve to relocate recovery geometry, spend some drones as a screen, or decline the matchup.

## Vector 3: Hot Ballistic Interruption

### Initial State

- A heavily armored reactor-rich platform radiates openly and carries redundant fire control, magazines, and radiators.
- It accepts immediate identification and fires massed ballistic patterns at likely parent volumes.
- Quiet loiterers possess better classification but insufficient impulse to leave the beaten volume after waiting for certainty.

### Result

The Hot platform loses the signature contest and wins the interruption race. Redundant fire control keeps enough rounds on the intended volume after one sensor and one cooling loop fail. Its heat clock is short but deliberately longer than the opponent's terminal and maneuver clocks.

### Heuristic

Being seen is acceptable when armor, redundancy, ammunition and violent heat expenditure make the enemy's shortest winning clock longer than yours. Hot doctrine is a commitment strategy, not a failure to understand stealth.

## Assertions

- Replaying a vector with identical state and orders must produce identical events and outcome.
- Increasing cognition without changing power, cooling or links cannot remove a physical recovery queue.
- Increasing fire-control throughput without solution quality cycles more wasted rounds.
- Increasing solution quality without throughput preserves aim on too few threats under saturation.
- Recoverability changes the economic result only after survival and retrieval; it does not alter an outgoing drone's immediate combat statistics.
- A mask outside physical heat, aspect or thrust envelope fails regardless of cognition quality.
- A smarter terminal package may select a critical subsystem and time final thrust better, but only within remaining sensor, control, impulse and geometry.
- No shield state is admitted in these Pre-Elysium vectors.
