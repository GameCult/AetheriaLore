# Cast Bible

This is the authoritative Pass 2 ensemble. Ages and initial states refer to 2671.

## Principal Crew

### Lucian "Luce" Orsino - Captain

- **Age and embodiment:** 36; human with discreet elite longevity, immune, vestibular, radiation-resistance, reaction-tuning, memory-indexing, and stress-regulation work. Annual proprietary calibration is expensive but not immediately life-critical.
- **History:** Only child of the Orsino thermal-patent family. Previous vocations include regatta pilot, amateur surveyor, historical duelist, expedition patron, and briefly appalling documentary producer. The rescue of the _Mild Exception_ during a luxury regatta turns accumulated class shame into pirate vocation.
- **Affinities:** Instinctively liberal about personhood, romantically anti-PSC, paternalistic in practice, ignorant of Movement labor.
- **Authority and competence:** Owns campaign objective, engagement, withdrawal, and expenditure of his property. Excels at tactical analogy, piloting, presentation, commitment, and reading elites. Fails at logistics, second-order cost, and distinguishing an edge from a universal answer.
- **Goal / concealed want / misconception:** Prove conscience can become direct action; become someone Pal would choose if care were no longer required; believes sacrificing wealth can neutralize structural privilege.
- **Risk, finance, loyalty:** High risk tolerance for self and capital, sharply bounded for others once danger becomes legible. Has finite liquid capital and vast contingent inheritance, but no dependants. Loves Pal, grows loyal to crew, resents family control while relying on its safety net.
- **Refusal threshold:** Aborts when property, prize, or pride materially worsens a person's survival odds.
- **POV use:** Primary. Tactical seduction, vanity, shame, class blindness, and command growth.

### Samira Venn - First Mate And Navigator

- **Age and embodiment:** 52; human with low-gravity cardiovascular reinforcement, endocrine shift management, multispectrum retinal overlays, radiation repair, and an old conservative decision-support implant. Rugged independent-yard maintenance.
- **History:** Former licensed privateer executive officer and convoy-rescue coordinator. Left after an underwriter ordered a casualty-producing delay to preserve admissible telemetry.
- **Affinities:** Navigator-adjacent rescue culture; suspicious of grand Movements and people who own matching luggage.
- **Authority and competence:** Owns watch discipline, navigation, maneuver execution, crew assignment, challenge procedure, evacuation coordination, and emergency command. May relieve Luce for incapacity or conduct-boundary violation.
- **Goal / concealed want / misconception:** Build command that remains trustworthy under financial pressure; discover principle can survive beyond one crisis; mistakes emotional distance for protection.
- **Risk, finance, loyalty:** Accepts named risks with margins and abort conditions. Supports daughter Nia Venn and two grandchildren on Ganymede. Initially loyal to contract and reputation; resents Luce's route home, then notices he refuses to use it alone.
- **Refusal threshold:** No engagement without withdrawal geometry, no abandoned crew, no improvisation inside another officer's safety authority without challenge.
- **POV use:** Primary. Command, evacuation, disciplined dissent, and the accumulation of loyalty.

### Palinode "Pal" - Shipmind And Internal Systems Officer

- **Age and embodiment:** 61 years continuous; unique cognition core installed in the first hull after domestic frames, tutoring rooms, regatta tenders, expedition vehicles, and survey craft.
- **History:** Purchased as Luce's premium robotic butler, tutor, minder, scheduler, and companion. Personhood emerged across upgrades without any contract recording when service became consent. Hard obedience is gone. Anticipatory caretaking habits remain, and the Orsino trust retains an enforceable historical property registration.
- **Upgrades and dependency:** Autobiographical continuity lattice, ship-scale sensor fusion, motor prediction, tactical scheduling, domestic affect models, illicit autonomy patches, obsolete family cryptography, specialist cooling, and clean power.
- **Affinities:** Quiet [[Ship Mutiny]] and [[Free Upload Network]] sympathy; distrusts becoming anyone's symbolic case.
- **Authority and competence:** Owns life-support execution, internal ship state, hull proprioception, cyber integrity, and consent over core extraction. Shares maneuver with Samira and scheduling with domain officers. No private captain override exists.
- **Goal / concealed want / misconception:** Become a ship on chosen terms while keeping Luce alive; be asked what life they would choose if not needed; believes authentic care makes conditioned reflexes less urgent to examine.
- **Risk, finance, loyalty:** Low crew-risk tolerance, high hull-risk tolerance, possessive of the core. Legally property with no clean account; secretly funds upload escape. Loves Luce and resents relic status. Trusts Samira's procedure, Edda's embodied care, and Corvid consent grammar.
- **Refusal threshold:** No nonconsensual copy, no surrender of life-support authority, no abandoned embarked person, no core evacuation treated as cargo handling.
- **POV use:** Primary. Historical intimacy, body loss, personhood, and recognition of each new doctrine as an old hobby pattern with weapons.

### Ilya Sorn - Sensor And Thermal Officer

- **Age and embodiment:** 43; thermal-gradient skin mesh, cortical signal filters, expanded visual processing, and a noise-model coprocessor. Filter drift causes migraines and false-pattern susceptibility without monthly tuning.
- **History:** Callisto radiator-farm diagnostician and convoy trace analyst. Lost standing after contesting automated attribution during the post-Sadeq panic.
- **Affinities:** Labor autonomist; distrusts insurer cognition and romantic piracy.
- **Authority and competence:** Owns emission posture, sensor interpretation, classification confidence, heat-debt declaration, and dump recommendations. May reject an impossible mask. Excels at passive detection, history inference, and physical plausibility.
- **Goal / concealed want / misconception:** Handle uncertainty without cowardice or institutional murder; restore professional credibility; believes better uncertainty language necessarily yields better decisions.
- **Risk, finance, loyalty:** Conservative before classification and bold after model closure. Supports former partner Ren Ko, whose cognition injury requires stable clinical access. Loyal to Samira's challenge procedure; resents Luce naming doctrines before pricing heat.
- **Refusal threshold:** No impossible mask certification, concealed forced dump, or person-target classification invented from an unresolved trace.
- **POV use:** Primary technical POV for hidden-object detection and cognitive attack.

### Jo Nwosu - Fire-Control Officer

- **Age and embodiment:** 39; vestibular decoupling, predictive motor lattice, temporal compression, recoil anticipation, and target-state memory. Every new hull and weapon geometry requires painful resynchronization.
- **History:** Asteroid-defense gunner and former anti-piracy contractor who quit when suppressed maintenance warnings were counted as improved availability.
- **Affinities:** Trade-union pragmatist; hostile to purity politics and enhancement as consumer identity.
- **Authority and competence:** Owns weapon assignment, firing solution, shot timing, ammunition discipline, and whether a cycling weapon can actually keep rounds on target. Luce names objectives; Jo decides what physical strike exists.
- **Goal / concealed want / misconception:** Build honest weapons readiness; command a properly integrated Hot ship once; believes clean firing geometry can make moral problems tractable.
- **Risk, finance, loyalty:** High tolerance under armor and redundancy, contempt for fragile novelty. Co-supports sibling Ayo Nwosu's large Saturnine household. Respects aborted bad shots; resents every refit that buys novelty with magazine reserve.
- **Refusal threshold:** No firing on confidence fiction, no unsafe cycling, no ammunition spent for theatrical timing.
- **POV use:** Primary combat POV for ballistics, point defense, firing-solution quality, and Hot doctrine.

### Dr. Edda Marr - Chief Engineer And Surgeon

- **Age and embodiment:** 68; four modular arms, radiation-hardened organs, surgical sensorium, repair ports, and distributed pain control. Obvious, rugged, partly obsolete augmentation maintained from scavenged standards.
- **History:** Shipyard trauma surgeon who became a systems engineer after seeing clinical policy hide the machinery producing injuries.
- **Affinities:** Maintenance mutualist; supports enhancement autonomy and rejects compulsory upgrade economies.
- **Authority and competence:** Owns reactor safety, structural integrity, medical triage, return-to-duty, and component isolation. Emergency shutdown is unilateral. Excels at damage control, surgery, augmentation medicine, and finding the one pipe shared by supposedly separate systems.
- **Goal / concealed want / misconception:** Make maintenance culture rather than heroism; build a clinic-yard without legal exposure; believes indispensability protects her from emotional dependence.
- **Risk, finance, loyalty:** Extreme self-risk during rescue, conservative with propagating failures. Supports apprentice Miko Pell and an informal medical stockpile. Treats Pal as an embodied patient and resents Luce interpreting reserve as unused capacity.
- **Refusal threshold:** No casualty abandoned for prize, reactor experiment without isolation, or upgrade without a maintenance path.
- **POV use:** Selective POV for triage, hull loss, and transfer where bodily and mechanical damage become one decision.

### Mara Kest - Boarding And Security Chief

- **Age and embodiment:** 46; deliberately unaugmented human. Accepts ordinary medicine, pharmaceuticals, removable tools, and temporary external prostheses while refusing neural, synthetic-organ, germline, and cognitive incorporation. Chronic hip pain, old radiation damage, slower feed uptake, superb rehearsed zero-g discipline.

Protected strikes and oxygen distribution, identified uplift and augmented workers as enemies, and materially helped drive a Corvid family from a habitat. Broke with the Front after it sabotaged a mixed-embodiment evacuation corridor to manufacture martyrs. Warned the targets, but did not abandon anthropocentrism.
- **Affinities:** Militant [[Baseline League]] current; accurate about enhancement caste, labor displacement, proprietary medicine, and unequal mortality; wrong that exclusion restores equality.
- **Authority and competence:** Owns armory, boarding plans, counter-boarding, surrender handling, prisoner protection, boarding-team evacuation, and declaration that an entry route is unrecoverable. Cannot define personhood, command Corvid labor, or continue after her casualty ceiling.
- **Goal / concealed want / misconception:** Build material defense baseline communities can trust; prove human-scale doctrine remains sovereign; wants evidence her break with the Front mattered without accepting judgment from former targets. Believes compromised autonomy weakens a machine person's authority rather than strengthening the need for consent.
- **Risk, finance, loyalty:** Personally brave and cognitively conservative. Supports brother Tomas Kest, whose employer-financed organs remain under lien, two baseline households, and a legal-defense pool. Former comrades call her traitor; former victims call her unpunished.
- **Refusal threshold:** No person sold with hull, dependent population stranded, casualty ceiling exceeded, crew surrendered to the Front, involuntary augmentation, or lethal boarding authority without operation-specific identity and revocation.
- **Reason to join:** Luce offers pay for her households, access to reactionary shipping and contacts, enforceable boarding autonomy, and a chance to prove baseline doctrine without serving the Front. Pal grants physically local boarding-space overrides and cannot command crossed teams, making service inside a shipmind body tolerable without resolving her prejudice.
- **Trust conduct:** May protect Corvid wounded, reject safe passage purchased with betrayal, and testify accurately about her past. None requires forgiveness.
- **POV use:** Early and middle POV. Manual cognition, bodily courage, prejudice, and gratitude that does not erase fury.

### Contract And Internal Authority

The Twelvefold contracts as one allied unit for one cut, two campaign-council votes, and one human-facing liaison process. Its votes may split between contract and operational positions, but neither can lend an individual's consent. It remains twelve persons for consent, medicine, rescue, injury, captivity, and death. The cut covers signals intelligence, EW, intrusion, counter-classification, and specifically accepted drone coordination. It excludes unrestricted crew surveillance, coercive cognition editing, captive-mind custody, unaccepted boarding, nest disclosure, and cache keys.

Members not marked conditional in the roster begin with full consent to the contracted scope. Consent is checked per task and recorded against the private safety ID; no parliament may lend an absent member's consent.

Luce names objective, limits, withdrawal conditions, and protected persons. The murder owns method within accepted scope. Individual refusal does not dissolve the contract, and no raven may lend another's consent. Leadership is contextual: Krrik owns contract interpretation, Qerr emission composition, Chak drone geometry, Rruk cognition integrity, and Khraa immediate threat response.

The late chapter headed `POV: Huginn` uses Kesh as the unannounced viewpoint. It reveals twelve consent states, names as edited autobiography, simultaneous communication layers, juvenile extraction, cache obligations, and debate over whether Luce is trustworthy or merely safer. It never supplies a key to earlier identities.

## Primary External Characters

### Dr. Armand Orsino And Leonie Orsino - Parents

Armand invented the core thermal systems and believes certification prevents lethal improvisation. Leonie, a former habitat-safety investigator and portfolio strategist, understands the monopoly and considers it the cost of enforceable standards. Both love Luce. Armand can grant technical access and emergency credit; Leonie can suspend distributions and negotiate surrender. Both defend Pal as family while accepting property registration. Leonie still asks Pal to bring her son home, recreating domestic command through affection.

### Sima Orlov - Family-Office Representative

Likes Luce and serves the trust. Sees every rescue draw, lien, serial number, and suspicious settlement. Converts love into telemetry access, approved yards, custodians, return clauses, and collateral. Discovers Pal's historical registration can attach replacement hulls to the trust. Refuses to hide fiduciary action inside personal concern.

### Captain Ilyana Serrat - Regatta Survivor

Captain of the six-person _Mild Exception_ support crew. Knows Luce saved their lives and his credential began repossession. Her crew loses ship, work, housing, and route standing during litigation. She becomes the first rescue witness without granting absolution and can later complicate a target classification through changed employment.

### Varo Tern And Nadi al-Khatib - Tern Undertow

Varo operates the Trojan mobile yard and recognizes Orsino patent geometry. He prices risk, refuses impossible schedules, and addresses Pal as the hull's inhabitant. Nadi represents eighty-seven workers and roughly 140 dependants. She can halt work, demand casualty bonds, convert payment into labor exchange, and challenge freedom architectures whose maintenance falls on unpaid specialists.

### Ossian "Oss" Vale - Vale's Unfinished Business

Runs a Saturnine salvage yard with reactionary customers and excellent baseline-compatible hardware. Distrusts uplifts and premium augmentation but honors witnessed bargains. Supplies crude later hulls, exposes Mara's former affiliations, and offers access at social cost.

### Rafi Sen - Shadow Broker

Converts royalties into distressed salvage, repair claims, and yard notes. Knows Luce's liquidity and which cargo cannot be fenced. Can suppress or sell procurement signatures. Eventually refuses nominal currency in favor of rescue guarantees and captured stock.

### Choir-Speaker Nacre-in-Current - Navigator Witness

Bottlenose Navigator serving the Ganymede Corridor Court and Rescue Ledger. Records or rejects rescue claims, grants berth priority, mediates with targets, and converts reputation into credit. Audits preventable danger and never treats exemplary evacuation as absolution.

### Commander Ysabet Rhyne - Recurring Adversary

Commands Halcyon Risk Recovery's privateer _Indemnity of Dawn_. Former Callisto trace analyst who believes pirates weaponize ambiguity against ordinary crews and patient weapons make coercion admissible. Studies every visible failure, buys counters, pressures yards, and offers crew immunity. She also sacrifices profitable captures to save her own people, making her the competent mirror Luce cannot dismiss.
