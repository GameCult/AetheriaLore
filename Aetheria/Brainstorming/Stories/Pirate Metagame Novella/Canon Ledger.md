This ledger distinguishes source-grounded setting constraints from story inventions. It is authoritative for the room, not for the wider Aetheria vault.

## Combat Canon

| Fact | Story Consequence | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-Elysium combat has no science-fiction shields. | Detection, first-hit lethality, armor, point defense, redundancy, and escape geometry carry survival. | [[Worldbuilding/Politics/Combat Doctrine]] |
| Thermal concealment is heat debt, not invisibility. | Quiet strategies must eventually maneuver, dump, degrade, or withdraw. | [[Worldbuilding/Politics/Thermal Signature Warfare]] |
| Signature shaping follows the [[Cold Wake Panic]] metamaterial threshold and remains bounded by plausible thermal envelope. | A platform may imitate nearby classes, not anything desired. | [[Worldbuilding/Politics/Thermal Signature Warfare]] |
| Drones and loitering munitions are small hulls whose roles emerge from capabilities and orders. | Relay, spotter, mine, PDC, heat ferry, beacon, Remora, and munition remain fluid roles. | [[Worldbuilding/Politics/Combat Doctrine]] |
| Remorae are non-destructive subsystem-attack chassis. | Their purpose is pressure, capture, intrusion, or surrender while preserving the prize. | [[Worldbuilding/Pre-Elysium/Factions/Powers/Major/Pirate Coalition]] |
| Recoverability changes launch economics rather than immediate engagement physics. | Reusable drones remain vulnerable to thrust, heat, sensing, and counterfire limits. | [[Worldbuilding/Politics/Combat Doctrine]] |
| Cognition improves sensing, classification, targeting, fire-control throughput, firing-solution quality, command, coordination, guidance, terminal maneuver, and subsystem selection. | Cognitive quality must be modeled across the stack, not as a single accuracy bonus. | [[Worldbuilding/Politics/Combat Doctrine]] |
| Ballistics export energy by spending mass and logistics; energy weapons spend onboard power and heat for controlled energy on target. | Weapon choices alter both engagement and campaign economics. | [[Worldbuilding/Politics/Combat Doctrine]] |
| Mature Hot doctrine includes armor, redundancy, radiators, sensors, point defense, ammunition, and simple priorities. | Brute force remains a credible counter and cannot be written as stupidity. | [[Worldbuilding/Politics/Combat Doctrine]] |

## Institutional Canon

| Fact | Story Consequence | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Hard Exclusions

- shields;
- aetheric systems;
- espers;
- continuity residue;
- Parallax admissibility;
- Sol Dominion as a current power;
- collapse-level infrastructure scarcity;
- perfect invisibility or heat deletion;
- arbitrary signature transformation;
- human-speed terminal authorization;
- cheap, consequence-free hull replacement;
- smart weapons treated as intrinsically benevolent.
