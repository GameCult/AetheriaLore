## Conservation Invariants Preserved In Prose

- Signature shaping changes the history told by real heat inside a platform's envelope; it does not remove heat, mass, acceleration, or aspect evidence.
- Genuine authentication grants bounded work authority, not personhood impersonation or arbitrary access.
- Remorae are non-destructive subsystem-attack chassis here. Their terminal cognition chooses only among physically reachable, payload-compatible access regions.
- Cognition partition costs speed and integration. It prevents one contaminated conclusion from owning the stack; it does not make Sable wiser by moral declaration.
- Proper Hot doctrine wins the visible exchange through reactor output, armor, active sensing, fire-control quality, magazine mass, and human feed labor. It loses the protected objective because those resources and attention are finite.
- Recoverability changes economics and launch behavior. Carrier one is deliberately spent as cover; no immediate combat bonus appears merely because hardware could otherwise return.
- The final freeze depends on worker choice and one surviving communication path. Weapons preserve the decision space; they do not manufacture consent.
