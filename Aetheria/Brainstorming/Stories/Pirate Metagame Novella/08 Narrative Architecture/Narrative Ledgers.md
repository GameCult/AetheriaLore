## Audience Reconstruction Test

At every reversal, a nontechnical reader should be able to say:

- I: the drones came back, but the people servicing them could not keep doing it.
- II: the target kept working until the hidden pirates ran out of tolerable waiting.
- III: the captured subsystem also kept passengers alive, so the prize ceased to be acceptable.
- IV: the enemy attacked the finite door through which every reusable weapon returned.
- V: the enemy attacked every plausible route and forced the real ship to reveal itself.
- VI: the shot remained accurate after its meaning became untrustworthy.
- VII: the ship won the visible fight and lost the objective, then chose rescue over the prize.
- Final: Luce ignored the obvious vault and protected the people who could make the claims real.
