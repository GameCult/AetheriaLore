# Voice And Prose Standard

The three voice tests are temporary developmental evidence, not manuscript prose. They proved the character voices and exposed an over-compressed explanatory register. Drafting uses the rules below.

## Viewpoint Texture

- **Luce** is rhetorically precise. He builds arguments as social objects and notices when they fail to confer status.
- **Pal** is historically and domestically precise. Ship systems arrive as embodiment; care appears first as anticipation, then as a question about why the anticipation occurred.
- **Sable** is operationally precise, recursive, and self-revising. Their thought may reopen a conclusion instead of decorating it.
- **Samira** notices authority, remaining options, and the time at which an option ceases to be honest.
- **Mara** notices bodies, entrances, threat, and obligations she would prefer to classify cleanly.
- **Huginn** notices authored records, juvenile safety, earned access, and the difference between what Kesh knows and what the murder has chosen to disclose.

## Sentence Discipline

- Put strong lines inside plain functional prose. Do not make every paragraph quotable.
- Delete the explanatory gloss after an action or line of dialogue already carries the point.
- Ration parallel triads, `not X but Y` turns, aphoristic chapter endings, and polished rebuttals.
- In crisis, people interrupt, repeat themselves, answer the operational part of the wrong question, and leave sentences unfinished. Authority remains legible through action and consequence.
- Technical explanation enters through a bet, objection, repair, surrender, rescue, or refusal. Campaign VI keeps only: Rruk's contradictions, Sable's point, Jo's shot, Samira's expired deadline, Luce's withdrawal, and incoming mass striking stored-energy volumes.
- Impairment, species, augmentation, and loss of bodily control are never punchlines. Comedy may expose status, procedure, vanity, or institutional absurdity without making a body the joke.
- Corvid observations remain relationship-specific. No phrase such as “in a very Corvid way” substitutes for Kesh's actual knowledge of another person.

## Preserved Material

The following may be developed in prose if the surrounding scene earns them: the table opening and “They were. He had checked.”; Callisto etiquette; Jo's proposal to convert inventory into decisions and Dena's cooling objection; “I know what scheduler does. I asked what ship does.”; Pal's domestic clocks; “Pal noticed. Pal did not log it.”; Luce dressing for events he hoped would become memories; the three-second motor-cycle political problem; “Your point is real. I don't know what the point is.”; “Not darkness. Worse. Simplicity.”; and the embodied pleasure of guns going dakka.

The boarding-chief joke in Voice Test One is withheld until a later relationship can bear it. Mara's stated boundaries remain boundaries, not setup for a joke about her body.

## Reader Load

No scene teaches more than one primary and one secondary tactical clock. A nontechnical reader should understand a reversal from visible cause and choice before receiving terminology for it.
