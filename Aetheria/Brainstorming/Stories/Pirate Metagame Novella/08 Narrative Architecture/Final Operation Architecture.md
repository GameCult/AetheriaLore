# Final Operation Architecture: The Honest Accounting

This document accepts the final operation at architectural resolution. Exact prose, shot timing, and component branding remain available to later drafting. The operation is a genuine clean tactical victory and a historically specific economic intervention, not a solved metagame.

## Reader Question

Halcyon expects Luce to chase the ship that appears to contain the most valuable titles. The real objective is the temporary quorum of workers, witnesses, records, and local authority able to make those titles enforceable.

**Will Luce chase what looks valuable, or protect the people who can make value real?**

## Target

Halcyon's mobile continuity convoy gathers contradictory claims into one moving settlement volume because ordinary ports cannot safely reconcile them. The convergence contains:

- adjudication carrier _Common Measure_;
- conspicuous title vault;
- worker and witness habitat;
- three evidence, escrow, and service tenders;
- two escort groups, including Rhyne's _Indemnity of Dawn_;
- local Recovery, Doctrine, Liability, medical, and maintenance staff;
- physical records, escrow keys, seized tools, attached small hulls, and witnesses whose presence makes the records actionable.

It is not a treasure fleet. Destroying, stealing, or fabricating its records would reduce most of the value. The coalition needs a witnessed local continuity window in which authorized workers can freeze remote alteration and asset flight, transfer escrow into neutral custody, and make minimum distributions enforceable. Detailed adjudication continues for months afterward.

## Pal's Choice Before Commitment

Before the sanctuary deadline, Pal uses the air-gapped Ship Mutiny token. The continuity shell enters neutral cooling and physical custody beyond Luce's inspection for nineteen hours. Pal receives independent counsel and a credible alternative life: work at a neutral recovery harbor, relationships with extracted domestic minds, a small noncombat body, and no obligation to supervise another Orsino crisis.

The crew demonstrates a two-hour no-Pal fallback exercise before departure. It loses ship-scale coordination and comfort, not basic maneuver, life support, fire control, or abort authority.

Pal returns under neutral bailment and an independently witnessed, revocable one-operation berth contract. The contract denies fixture accession, preserves Ship Mutiny's extraction right, and forbids automatic transfer into any later hull. Pal chooses one operation, one berth, this crew, and Luce as its bounded captain; they do not settle their whole future. They return still unable to separate every caretaking motive from learned service and set a boundary that disappoints Luce: no private access to the continuity shell and no presumption of another embodiment after Hull Four.

Pal's choice occurs before victory. It is not a reward granted to Luce afterward.

## Operational Sequence

### 1. Selection

Luce declines more glamorous captures and chooses the continuity convergence because its objective fits Hull Four, the coalition, and the conduct rule. He names no doctrine. This is his first important plan that becomes clearer when other people own pieces of it.

### 2. Brief Quiet

_The Burden of Proof_ coasts cold outside the expected escort solution. Quiet buys approach time; nobody expects it to survive contact. Pal keeps ship control isolated. Rruk and Sable partition cognition before receiving Halcyon emissions.

### 3. Bounded Shaping

Two of the three relay hulls carry one Remora each in external service cradles; the third preserves an independent claimant path. The carriers spend real heat and impulse matching expected service returns within their envelopes. Genuine one-use worker-issued inspection tokens and scheduled transponders pass the outer administrative challenge. Shaping hides which service hull carries which capability; it does not authenticate pirate hardware by itself.

The five mixed PDC hulls remain the local screen. No uncounted carrier appears.

### 4. Worker-Opened Geometry

Mira and cooperating technicians schedule three legitimate inspection states and hold mechanical safing open through a local service cycle. Corporate closure is not impossible; it is slower than a worker standing at the mechanism. When escort classification resolves the anomaly, the relay carriers release the Remorae inside a bounded maintenance closing volume. Deception ends there; terminal evasion begins.

Each Remora carries the same bounded dual-purpose package. A successful attachment can isolate the external override path. Local workers can then hold custody and purge controls manually long enough to commit the minimum freeze. The second attachment extends the window and prevents a remote purge but is not separately indispensable. Neither chassis can command drive, life support, or inhabited refuge systems.

The open inspection state constrains _Common Measure_ to a maintenance-safe acceleration envelope. The Remora begins inside a closing volume where three access regions overlap its remaining divert envelope. At point-blank range it knows more about local motion and attachment state than the parent did at launch and may spend its final thrust choosing among those regions. It cannot match an arbitrary capital burn, cross the ship freely, or recover if workers close access. The terminal choice matters without granting free impulse.

### 5. Hot Declaration

Attachment reveals the operation. Hull Four deploys visible radiators, active sensors, and ballistic directors. Convergence procedure has constrained the escorts to three time-sensitive approach corridors around the worker habitat and _Common Measure_. Jo can deny two while retaining direct defensive fire and a protected withdrawal floor. The guns constrain routes and timing; they do not paint arbitrary space or fire through inhabited volumes.

After Campaign VII, Hull Four holds 41 percent main magazine, 63 percent PDC stock, and 46 percent translation propellant. Claimant escrow funds one bounded 620,000 SU replenishment, included in direct operational cost, bringing those states to 58, 74, and 61 percent. Jo may not spend below 25 percent main magazine, 30 percent PDC stock, or 20 percent propellant reserved for withdrawal and rescue.

Samira owns maneuver. Jo owns each solution. Edda owns survivable reactor and heat output. Luce owns continued commitment to the objective, not the physical facts.

### 6. Distributed Cognition

Sable gives each small hull a bounded local objective before hostile links mature. Ilya keeps geometry confidence separate from role confidence. The Twelvefold carries authentication, threat, and casualty state across isolated paths. Pal may quarantine ship links but cannot absorb Sable's architecture or Corvid authority.

### 7. The Title-Vault Chase

Rhyne breaks the title vault and one escort away on a route Hull Four can dominate. The vault contains the legacy accession register needed to discharge Pal's fixture claim, Hull Four's clean title, and one collateral class. Pursuit is defensible: it would make the settlement more complete and personally safer for Pal. Face value, old Luce, and ordinary piracy all say pursue.

Luce refuses. Hull Four remains with the workers and quorum, accepting that fixture/title resolution remains frozen and disputed after victory. Rhyne's choice is competent: if Luce chases, the convoy scatters and Halcyon restores suppression. Another opponent might keep the vault inside the quorum or scatter earlier.

### 8. Genuine Casualty

Escort fire destroys one relay; fragments damage a detached worker tender and separate an EVA pod from its return line. Rhyne dispatches Paz Orri's craft to recover the tender crew. Edda activates one pirate shepherd for the isolated pod; Samira deconflicts its geometry with Paz, and Sable maneuvers it. The second shepherd remains actual reserve. The surviving relay and Cold Choir paths carry claimant continuity at lower bandwidth. Rescue makes the operation harder without duplicating ownership or becoming tactical waste.

Khraa invokes the protected-person condition and may terminate Corvid participation if worker movement becomes coerced. Mara yields presumed security ownership. They negotiate a lower-exposure transfer route between the worker habitat and _Common Measure_; Mara controls boarding security, Khraa controls threat ranking and the stop condition. Neither could lawfully move the workers alone.

### 9. Local Adjudication

The Remorae do not hack money free. They create an opportunity. Mira delays invocation until two worker delegates complete a fresh vote, surprising Sable and costing another interval of route-denial ammunition. The delegates add clinical continuity for junior Halcyon staff as a condition; only then does Dev release the suppression record.

Kavi authenticates the care/testimony class while refusing coalition membership or pirate custody. The Twelvefold supplies a derived one-use claimant proof; Vek refuses live nest keys, and Kesh's contract vote terminates disclosure if the system queries juvenile identities. Nacre witnesses provenance without owning distribution.

The quorum commits an irrevocable continuity freeze: remote alteration and asset flight stop, escrow and evidence enter neutral witnessed custody, and minimum distributions become enforceable. Nela's medicine class receives less than requested to preserve Mae Corin's pension floor; the legacy fixture/title class remains unresolved because Luce did not chase the vault. Detailed adjudication continues after the battle.

### 10. Disengagement

Rhyne is appointed convergence security coordinator. She commands both escort groups and immediate convoy maneuver/deconfliction, including local rescue, but cannot bind Halcyon's corporate claims policy. She may negotiate a local ceasefire and disengagement.

Halcyon retains the physical ability to continue fighting. Continued fire now risks its workers, evidence, frozen assets, and Rhyne's own rescue craft while the committed continuity event makes corporate escalation self-defeating. Rhyne negotiates local separation rather than becoming tactically helpless. During Samira's POV section she states the future counter so its knowledge travels correctly.

The crew wins because it selected an objective Halcyon could not preserve by destroying it.

## Future Counter

Rhyne can already specify the counter:

- never reconcentrate settlement authority;
- separate evidence from execution authority;
- move override outside ordinary maintenance topology;
- precommit remote quorum rules;
- rotate service signatures;
- use several smaller convoys;

These changes make future settlement slower, costlier, and harsher to claimants. They also make this exact operation obsolete. The metagame survives.
