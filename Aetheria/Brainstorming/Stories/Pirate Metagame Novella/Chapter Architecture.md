# Chapter Architecture

The revised novella contains **28 chapters** across three movements. Every chapter or break section displays `POV: <name>`. Close third never transfers knowledge across sections.

The architecture uses [[07 Character Causality Replay/Audience Visibility And Comedy Ledger]] as a hard exposure budget. Sideband state remains richer than the manuscript.

## Architecture Invariants

- Chapters 10-11, 15, 17-18, 21, and 26-27 receive the largest tactical treatment.
- Campaign VII's Hot victory is setup, not a second climax.
- Each hull era has one ordinary-life compression scene.
- No campaign presents more than two tactical clocks to the reader.
- Break chapters reveal consequence or counterstate; they do not replay the complete event.
- The Huginn chapter reveals depth, not a roster key.
- Pal's free choice occurs before final victory.
- The final coalition appears through a few conflicts, not constituency speeches.
- Rhyne makes defensible choices and loses the selected objective, not competence.
