# Simulation Candidate Notes

These notes are noncanonical implementation prompts discovered during scene engineering. They do not block drafting.

| Candidate | Narrative use | Consumed state | Missing simulation state | Conservation and counterplay | Observable cues | Sensitivity / status / promotion trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recovery-neighborhood consequence volume | Enemy attacks the shared door and service plant rather than defeating every recoverable drone | Drone fuel, heat, ammunition; throat state; hot parking; parent geometry | Spatial coupling between recovery queue and vulnerable service volumes | Threat must infer return fan and close while doors are active; parent may scatter, abandon drones, close throat, or change recovery site | Return vectors, door state, coolant demand, parent maneuvers | Robust at coarse level; **Provisional**; promote because it recurs, changes loadouts, and must transfer observed/offscreen |
| Worker-held mechanical safing | Local worker keeps an inspection state open after remote closure command | Worker presence, mechanism state, time, coercion risk | Human/local authority as actuator distinct from network control | Worker may refuse, be removed, or close access; remote systems regain control after bounded delay | Open panels, local acknowledgements, slower closure | **Provisional**; promote if players can solicit or counter local cooperation |
| Dual-purpose Remora override isolation | One attachment isolates remote suppression while workers hold custody manually | Terminal thrust, attachment access, payload duration, worker labor | Attachment-region reachability, isolation duration, local/manual custody state | Maintenance envelope constrains target; PDC, closure, arbitrary burn, or worker refusal counter it | Service token, approach resolution, attachment alarm, lost override | **Provisional**; promote because AI/player invocation and offscreen transfer matter |
| Role migration on common small hulls | Relay carriers become terminal munition delivery and later communication assets | Cradle mass, heat, impulse, link bandwidth, carried payload | Runtime capability activation and role confidence separate from hull category | Each role consumes physical components; destruction removes all bundled capabilities | Emission, trajectory, deployed payload, link behavior | **Established** as doctrine principle, implementation incomplete; promote with small-hull component system |
| Protected reserve with distributed release authority | Rescue shepherd remains available because designation, activation, geometry, and maneuver have separate owners | Hull slot, fuel, time, cognition, opportunity cost | Multi-owner authorization and protected-resource floor | Can be reassigned only through named authority path; damage or geometry may still defeat rescue | Reserve status, activation command, assigned corridor | **Provisional**; promote because deliberate player/AI doctrine and social authority affect outcome |
| Continuity-freeze commit | Tactical objective is preserving quorum until remote alteration and asset flight become irreversibly frozen | People, records, escrow key, communication paths, procedure owner, time | Noncombat objective graph with revocable participants and irreversible commit | Participants can withdraw before commit; loss of required roles/paths collapses attempt; combat cannot adjudicate later claims | Witness confirmations, path loss, worker vote, commit receipt | **Provisional**; likely strategic-layer mechanic; promote if economic warfare and offscreen transfer use it |
| Architecture-specific adversarial cognition | Shaped emissions make a real solution semantically unreliable for a known cognition lineage | Attacker evidence, waveform/thermal budget, defender cognition load, time | Architecture provenance, targeted bias state, partition action, semantic confidence | Generic noise cannot produce precise bias; partition, independent sensors, early withdrawal, or architecture diversity counter it | Contradictory histories, confidence divergence, known provenance | **Provisional**; promote because recurring AI/player counterplay and cognition balance require numerical boundaries |
| Maintenance-safe acceleration envelope | Worker-opened state limits target maneuver enough for terminal selection among access regions | Target acceleration authority, open mechanism, Remora divert | Contextual maneuver envelope tied to maintenance state | Target may close access, sacrifice mechanism, or accelerate and abort procedure; Remora cannot match arbitrary burn | Declared maintenance state, closure attempt, acceleration limit | **Provisional**; promote with terminal maneuver and subsystem-access simulation |

## Drafting Rule

A scene may use any candidate above when conservation, era, partial knowledge, finite cognition, and materially available counterplay make the result robust. Exact thresholds remain contested and may not carry a numerical knife-edge outcome.
