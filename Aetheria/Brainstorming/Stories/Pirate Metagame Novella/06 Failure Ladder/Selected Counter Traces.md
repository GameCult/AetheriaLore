## Robustness Rule

Pass 7 may refine who knew what and when. It may not make a loss depend on:

- exact percentage superiority;
- an unseeded weapon or target weakness;
- a human approving a millisecond terminal action;
- adversarial cognition compelling behavior;
- absent hardware appearing through a role change;
- officers silently overriding established domain authority;
- an opponent continuing a fight after its rational objective has vanished merely to deliver the planned ending.
