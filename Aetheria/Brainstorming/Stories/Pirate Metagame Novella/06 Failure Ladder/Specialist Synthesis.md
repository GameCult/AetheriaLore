# Specialist Synthesis

Three read-only specialists proposed dramaturgical, tactical, and social/economic ladders.

## Coordinator Selection

- Seven pre-victory campaigns use all seven doctrine families without equal structural weight.
- Four hulls and three physical whole-hull losses preserve the user's repeated-replacement premise.
- Hull One survives the first recovery warning and later dies only after a corrected doctrine meets a robust attack on open service topology.
- Hull Two's shaped-thermal loss is an uncontrollable tumble and thermal/structural incompatibility, not another internal fire.
- Hull Three's precision-energy loss begins with a ballistic hit but becomes fatal through stored onboard energy and failed command/cooling distribution.
- Hull Four survives Hot and mixed-cloud failures and becomes the material base of final synthesis.
- Repossession remains a live threat and comic/economic pressure, but does not replace the third physical hull loss.
- The exact final target remains deferred; victory conditions and minimum realizable value are locked.

## Adopted Dramaturgy

- Deep Quiet is a domestic pressure cooker.
- Patient Hands anchors the middle through moral refusal.
- Shaped ferries are experienced partly through the intended victim.
- Premium cognition narrows through Sable's contaminated confidence.
- Hot combat is exhilarating and still loses the objective.
- Mixed-cloud failure becomes a rescue operation wearing the shape of defeat.

## Adopted Social And Economic Structure

- Face value, realizable value, and cash-now are separate story facts.
- Wages, medicine, dependants, casualty reserves, and labor notes become senior to Luce's capital recovery.
- Target-worker testimony, yard refusal, clinic allocation, insurer standards, and Wake credit decisions cause later constraints.
- Cumulative realizable captures remain below attributable campaign losses until the final operation.

## Hard Rejections

- No hull dies solely because a provisional mechanic says so.
- No adversarial cognition pattern compels behavior.
- No single exact weak-point hit is the sole causal explanation.
- No generic reactor explosion punctuates a campaign.
- Pal's portable shell never retains ship-scale cognition after extraction.
- Hot doctrine remains a valid durable base rather than another stupid build.
- A physically extant but uncontrollable, uninhabitable, and unrecoverable hull counts as lost when the causal chain is explicit.
