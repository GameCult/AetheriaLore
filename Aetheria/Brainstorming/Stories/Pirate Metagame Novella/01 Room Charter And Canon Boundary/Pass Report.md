## Contradictions And Risks

- Shipmind transfer is compatible with canon but not yet defined by it.
- The precise legal category of the continuity core must remain contested rather than conveniently settled.
- The captain's love for the shipmind must not erase the coercive ancestry of purchased domestic service.
- The captain's rescue invariant must not excuse preventable exposure.
- Family love must remain visible even when financial control tightens.
- The target policy must produce arguments and indirect harm rather than sanitizing piracy.
- Callisto-era smart weapons must retain their coercive industrial and evidentiary politics.

- Whether 2671-2677 keeps Callisto sufficiently recent without making smart-munition doctrine feel immature.
- Whether physical-core continuity gives the shipmind the right balance of survival and loss.
