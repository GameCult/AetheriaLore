# Dramaturgy Brief

## Core Contract

Every ridiculous tactical theory works once. The captain identifies real possibilities and mistakes favorable boundary conditions for universal law. The crew is expert but distributed; no single specialist owns the complete metagame.

The comedy comes from intelligent people trapped inside rational consequences.

## Emotional Progression

The crew initially stays for excellent pay. They then observe that the captain equips them properly, listens with imperfect sincerity, and never gambles with lives to rescue pride. They defend him socially before trusting him tactically. They trust him with command only after he learns to make strategy collective.

The final victory confirms growth without vindicating earlier mistakes.

## Escalation

Failures progress through:

1. embarrassment;
2. operational injury;
3. reputational distortion;
4. financial contraction;
5. shipmind embodiment loss;
6. relational fracture;
7. command crisis;
8. earned victory.

Awful luck chooses among plausible opponents. It cannot invent the counter.

## POV Use

POV rotation creates dramatic irony through unequal information and authority. The captain understands theory and presentation while missing labor. Technical officers understand failure probabilities while missing political or emotional effects. The shipmind experiences diagnostics as body. Adversaries expose the fear and expense behind apparent hard counters. Yard, insurer, worker, dependant, and civilian perspectives reveal costs the bridge cannot see.

The reader knows more than any one participant without receiving enough information to dissolve uncertainty.

## Shipmind Embodiment

A lost hull is bodily trauma, destroyed home, workplace disaster, and disputed asset. Re-embodiment may produce phantom sensor returns, missing-compartment expectations, altered movement, distrust of transfer, grief for private spaces, changed intimacy with residents of the former body, and anger when others describe survival as full recovery.

The shipmind retains vanity, humor, anger, tactical judgment, error, and desires beyond serving as solemn evidence.

As the captain's upgraded childhood robotic butler, the shipmind also holds the private archive of his earlier selves. It has survived expensive enthusiasms before and can recognize piracy as both a genuine moral break and another grand identity project. Their affection should be easy to feel and difficult to classify: the captain treasures the mind, while the mind must decide how much of lifelong attendance became freely chosen companionship.

## Final Victory

The crew wins by choosing the engagement, using several imperfect capabilities within their envelopes, distributing cognition, preserving margins, forcing a reveal, retaining an abort condition, keeping a simple Hot fallback, attacking an intact subsystem objective, refusing a secondary temptation, and honoring a precommitted withdrawal threshold.

The victory exposes a future counter. It does not end doctrine.
