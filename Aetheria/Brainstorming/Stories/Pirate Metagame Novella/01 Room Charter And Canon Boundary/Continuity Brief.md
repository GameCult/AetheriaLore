# Continuity Brief

## Recommended Frame

The room adopts 2671-2677 in the Ganymede-Callisto-Saturn corridor.

This places the story four to ten years after the [[Callisto Trace Shootdown]]. Smart-munition policy can already shape procurement, insurance, telemetry, and sales doctrine while the event remains part of living corridor memory. The period predates the [[Ceres Control War]], [[Bloom Expansion Conflicts]], [[Sol Dominion]], and [[Existential Collapse]].

## Available Material

- Cold Wake thermal storage, deferred dumps, detachable sinks, constrained signature shaping, heat ferries, and distributed sensors.
- Adversarial emissions aimed at known classifier and cognition architectures.
- Lattice-linked smart loiterers with close inspection, abort authority, retargeting, terminal thrust judgment, and subsystem selection.
- Recoverable small hulls whose role follows components and current orders.
- Non-destructive Remora payloads against cooling, reactor safing, life support, datanets, sensors, antennas, docking systems, cargo locks, and crew environment.
- Ballistic and energy weapon families with distinct mass, power, heat, ammunition, precision, and logistics costs.
- Commoditized cognition across sensing, classification, targeting, fire control, command, coordination, guidance, and terminal maneuvering.
- PSC underwriting, telemetry, provenance, damage exposure, PSCC pressure, and smart-munition incentives.

## Continuity Risks

- Signature shaping must remain plausible within heat, aspect, thrust, radiator geometry, and telemetry history.
- No Pre-Elysium shield may quietly enter through general game-design language.
- The Pirate Coalition cannot become a navy, central government, or uniform liberation movement.
- Whole hulls remain available but costly; money cannot erase yard labor, lead time, serial provenance, or insurer attention.
- The shipmind transfer mechanism is a story invention and must be treated as such.
- Smart weapons are not benevolent. Their restraint is also industrial policy, telemetry capture, insurer power, and more precise violence.
- Hot doctrine requires the whole industrial stack, not merely a large gun.
