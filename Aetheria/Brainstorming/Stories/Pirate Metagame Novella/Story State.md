## Locked Premise

- 50,000-60,000 word warm black-comedy novella.
- 2671-2677, Ganymede-Callisto-Saturn corridor.
- Rotating close third with the POV character named in every chapter heading.
- Wealthy novice captain from a loving patent-fortune family.
- Expert crew with distributed authority and no omniscient tactician.
- Persistent shipmind re-embodied through successive lost hulls.
- Repeated strategies produce real local wins before encountering grounded hard counters.
- Several hulls are lost; cumulative gains remain below cumulative costs until the final campaign.
- Final campaign produces a genuine, profitable tactical victory without solving the metagame.

## Initial Material State

### Captain

- Has liquidated approximately eight percent of expected inheritance.
- Retains a permanent family guarantee of rescue, medicine, counsel, and passage home.
- Does not retain unlimited operational funding.
- Understands adventure as a moral escape from administered life.
- Has not yet understood pirate labor, coercion, or dependency.
- Possesses creative tactical intuition without broad command competence.
- Chooses people over property immediately when the two conflict.

### Family

- Loving, living, embarrassed, and still in contact.
- Fortune comes from thermal-management coupling and control patents.
- Believes certification is a safety system; this belief is partly correct.
- Will help the captain survive but will not indefinitely fund attacks on counterparties.
- Can provide further credit only by increasing oversight, liens, traceability, and control.

### Crew

- Not yet named or cast.
- Will initially join for pay, equipment, opportunity, or specific network reasons rather than affection.
- Must retain credible exit rights, prize shares, command-challenge customs, and the ability to refuse unlawful or suicidal work.
- Expertise must cover sensing, thermal systems, fire control, drones, boarding, finance/procurement, medicine, maintenance, and social navigation without requiring one person per category.

### Shipmind

- One continuing person.
- Began as the captain's premium childhood robotic butler.
- Has accompanied his serial hobbies, failures, and reinventions through repeated upgrades and embodiments.
- Is the only crew member who knew him before the pirate identity.
- Is precious to him as family history, not merely valuable equipment.
- Carries unresolved tension between genuine mutual attachment and an origin in purchased service.
- Unique physical continuity core.
- Core evacuation is part of complete crew evacuation.
- Hull loss destroys meaningful embodiment state.
- Exact manufacturer, original service constraints, legal status, name, and transfer procedure remain deferred.

### Network

- Local Jovian-Saturnian capture-and-corridor reputation web.
- Includes yards, fences, brokers, rescue stations, couriers, capture crews, and shipmind or upload contacts.
- No central pirate government.
- Access begins commercial and can deepen into sanctuary and credit trust.

## Narrative State Invariants

1. Every doctrine earns its seduction before its defeat.
2. Every counter reveals a neglected clock, cost, dependency, or authority boundary.
3. No failure requires professional stupidity.
4. The captain risks his property before another person's life.
5. Loyalty grows through observable decisions, fair treatment, and improved command.
6. Each hull loss changes a relationship, capability, bodily habit, and financial condition.
7. Every major joke changes state.
8. Technical explanation participates in a contested decision.
9. POV controls interpretation, not physical truth.
10. Final victory demonstrates coordination, selection, and restraint rather than build supremacy.
