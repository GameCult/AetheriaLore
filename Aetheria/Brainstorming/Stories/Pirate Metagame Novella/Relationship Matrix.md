## Attraction Baseline

No romantic or sexual attraction is authoritative at Pass 2. Later attraction must be added as an explicit state change rather than inferred from trust, dependence, admiration, or ideological fixation.

## Required Relationship Arcs

- **Luce and Pal:** Luce must distinguish preserving the relic from enabling Pal to choose embodiment and departure.
- **Luce and crew:** Pay recruits them; rescue proves a boundary; accepting distributed authority makes loyalty possible.
- **Mara and Sable:** They do not become friends by debate. They learn each other's refusal thresholds are real and plan without requiring moral convenience.
- **Mara and Twelvefold:** Operational trust may grow through cryptographic accountability, protection, and truthful testimony. Forgiveness is neither promised nor required.
- **Pal and Sable:** Behavioral boundaries replace invasive source access; each relinquishes one claim to total understanding.
- **Crew and dependants:** Deferred shares turn adventure into household exposure and eventually a command crisis.
- **Crew and Undertow:** Injury shares, labor bonds, and schedule honesty convert wealth into accountable obligation.
- **Luce and Ysabet:** Each sees the other's rescue conduct. Respect sharpens rather than dissolves conflict.
