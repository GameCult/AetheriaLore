# Invention Register

These elements are accepted for the novella but are not yet wider Aetheria canon.

## Accepted Story Inventions

| ID | Invention | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I-001 | The story spans 2671-2677 in the Ganymede-Callisto-Saturn corridor. | Must not pull later wars or Sol Dominion backward. |
| I-002 | The captain's family owns a patent portfolio covering certified microchannel heat exchangers, coolant-manifold couplings, and control geometry. | The products genuinely improve safety; exploitation arises through ownership, certification, and dependency. |
| I-003 | The captain liquidates approximately eight percent of his expected inheritance as an independent hobby trust. | This is finite capital, not a permanent unlimited account. |
| I-004 | His parents permanently guarantee extraction, necessary medicine, legal counsel, and passage home. | They do not guarantee replacement warships or operational independence. |
| I-005 | His crisis of conscience begins when a family credential saves a cooling-failure crew while automatically initiating a claim against their ship. | No singular villain secretly orders the result. Ordinary workflow produces it. |
| I-006 | The crew belongs to a Jovian-Saturnian capture-and-corridor cell inside a local reputation web. | The network is not the whole Pirate Coalition and has no central command. |
| I-007 | The cell's conduct rule is: never create a dependency failure larger than the prize, and never sell a person as part of the hull. | The rule constrains conduct without making piracy harmless or morally pure. |
| I-008 | One persistent shipmind survives through a unique physical cognition core evacuated between hulls. | No interchangeable simultaneous authoritative copy exists. |
| I-009 | Hull-local sensor, motor, proprioceptive, spatial, and recent high-bandwidth state can be lost even when the core survives. | Transfer is injury and re-embodiment, not file restoration. |
| I-010 | The captain's life-over-property rule applies to every person, including the shipmind, regardless of rank or ownership. | Rescue does not excuse avoidable danger or bad command. |
| I-011 | Pirate and Navigator-adjacent rescue records eventually become shadow credit for the crew. | Benefits must arise through specific witnesses, yards, ledgers, and reciprocal acts. |
| I-012 | The shipmind began as the captain's premium childhood robotic butler and reached ship-scale personhood through decades of cumulative upgrades and embodiments. | The same continuing mind persists; upgrades cannot erase the coercive history of having been purchased to serve him. |
| I-013 | The captain treats the continuity core as a precious family relic and irreplaceable witness to his life. | His love may contain possessiveness; treasuring the core does not settle the shipmind's autonomy or consent. |

## Deferred Inventions

Pass 2 will determine:

- names, identities, embodiments, backgrounds, and relationships for the captain, parents, shipmind, officers, crew, yards, brokers, adversaries, and civilians;
- which childhood service obligations survive technically, psychologically, or contractually after the shipmind's upgrades;
- the local reputation web's proper names and internal customs;
- the captain's prior hobbies and which ones leave useful or ridiculous residues;
- the identity of the independent crew harmed by the inciting claim;
- whether any recurring adversary is linked to the family portfolio.

Pass 3 will determine:

- exact shipmind transfer hardware and procedure;
- legal ownership and custody records for the cognition core;
- embodiment-specific memory loss and recovery limits;
- first hull, component stack, crew workflow, shares, wages, operating reserve, and replacement lead times;
- the laundering and procurement chain used by named intermediaries.

## Rejected For This Story

- formal disinheritance as the source of stakes;
- secretly cruel parents funding the patent regime for pleasure;
- a weapons patent as the family fortune;
- a unified Pirate Coalition command granting membership;
- a perfectly heroic target policy with no indirect harm;
- shipmind continuity through casual backup restoration;
- a final revelation that the captain was always the best tactician aboard.
