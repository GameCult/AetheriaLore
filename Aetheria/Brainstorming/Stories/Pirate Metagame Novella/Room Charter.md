# Room Charter

## Story Promise

Every ridiculous tactical idea will be taken seriously enough to work once.

The novella follows the only child of a loving patent-fortune family who has spent adulthood passing through exquisitely financed hobbies. A crisis of conscience convinces him that adventure outside his caste is both a moral calling and the first authentic choice of his life. He buys an excellent ship, hires people who know piracy better than he does, and begins discovering the boundary conditions of every strategy that briefly makes him feel like a visionary.

He is not a concealed tactical genius, and he is not useless. He notices real possibilities, overgeneralizes from local success, and has enough money to turn each new conviction into machinery. His crew improves his ideas without possessing a complete answer themselves. The final victory belongs to their learned coordination, not to a revelation that he had been correct all along.

The emotional destination is simple: the fool becomes a captain because he learns what command is for.

## Historical Frame

**Span:** 2671-2677, during the late [[Identity Abyss]].

The career begins four years after the [[Callisto Trace Shootdown]]. Insurer policy has had time to become procurement practice, but Mireya Sadeq's death remains recent enough to shape sales language, corridor nerves, and crew opinion. The story ends before the [[Ceres Control War]], the [[Bloom Expansion Conflicts]], the formation of [[Sol Dominion]], and the scarcity regime of [[Existential Collapse]].

**Operating region:** the Ganymede-Callisto-Saturn corridor.

- Ganymede provides industrial support, dock labor, route law, respectable procurement, and access to [[Cetacean Navigators|Navigator]] institutions.
- Callisto provides gray-market cognition, pirate logistics, the living political wound left by Sadeq's death, and a corridor trained to overinterpret faint traces.
- Jovian Trojan regions provide mobile yards, false rescue stations, caches, and transitional space between formal and pirate economies.
- Saturn's rings and habitat mosaics provide ring-shadow yards, vulnerable extraction traffic, ideological enclaves, fences, contested cargo, and several kinds of pirate territory that do not agree about what piracy is for.

The story remains entirely Pre-Elysium. There are no shields, aetheric drives, espers, continuity residue, Parallax review, or post-Elysium exceptions.

## The Captain's Class Position

The family fortune comes from a boring, indispensable patent portfolio: certified microchannel heat exchangers, coolant-manifold couplings, and control geometry licensed into civilian ships, habitats, radiator farms, and their maintenance chains.

The patents save lives. Their certification and replacement regime also makes continued habitability a royalty event. Both facts remain true. The captain's parents are not secret villains; they believe standards prevent lethal improvisation, and the evidence often supports them. The extraction lies in ownership, mandatory control tokens, replacement-only certification, repossession covenants, and the ability to turn a cooling emergency into a claim on somebody's home.

His crisis of conscience begins during an expensive hobby expedition near a family-sponsored regatta or survey route. A marginal independent ship suffers a cooling failure. A physically compatible replacement coupling cannot activate without a licensed control token. He uses family credentials to authorize the repair and saves the crew, then learns that the authorization did not waive the charge. It transferred liability into the family office and initiated an automatic claim against the rescued operator. Every employee behaved reasonably according to role. Saving the crew and beginning repossession were adjacent workflow states.

He mistakes piracy for an escape from systems into direct moral action. The story will teach him that pirate freedom also rests on labor, coercion, reputation, compromised supply chains, and people who cannot afford to treat failure as education.

## Money And Stakes

The captain lawfully liquidates a hobby trust worth approximately eight percent of his expected inheritance. It purchases the first premium ship, an excellent initial component stack, crew recruitment, and an operating reserve.

His parents retain three promises:

- they will fund extraction from immediate danger;
- they will fund necessary medical treatment and legal counsel;
- they will always buy him passage home.

They will not indefinitely capitalize armed attacks against their counterparties. Emergency loans against his inheritance require family-office review, create PSC-visible exposure, and increase trustee control over the financed asset.

The financial progression is:

`clean liquidity -> encumbered replacement assets -> lossy shadow credit -> rescue-backed social credit`

After the first major hull loss, cash alone cannot preserve independence. Later ships require future-inheritance liens, royalty assignments, salvage shares, yard credit, crew profit deferral, captured industrial stock, labor exchange, and eventually guarantees based on rescue reputation.

His class privilege never disappears. He remains able to go home. That permanent exit is part of the tension: crew members risk households, hosting, medical reserves, legal status, and future employment while he risks the right to keep pretending his life belongs only to him.

## Pirate Network

The crew operates as a capture-and-corridor cell inside a loose Jovian-Saturnian reputation web. The network includes ring-shadow yards, former AstroDyne maintenance crews, small capture syndicates, Free Upload Network couriers, mutinous shipminds, gray-market cognition brokers, fences, and Navigator-adjacent rescue stations willing to distinguish outlaw status from corridor predation.

This is not a Pirate Coalition command structure. Specific people, yards, crews, and ledgers decide whether the captain is tolerable.

His access deepens through six stages:

1. commercial yard access;
2. introductions to fences and brokers;
3. inclusion in distress channels;
4. access to sanctuary-route knowledge;
5. permission to carry fugitives or sensitive cognition;
6. trusted standing sufficient for others to stake credit or lives on his return.

He is initially tolerated because he hires experienced local people, accepts prize-share and command-challenge customs, arrives with capital, and has guarantors whose own reputations can be damaged by him. He is regarded as useful weather: expensive, conspicuous, and unlikely to persist.

## Target And Conduct Boundary

The crew may take:

- luxury cargo with weak dependency effects;
- certified spares held off market to manufacture scarcity;
- illicit cognition, identity, or upload-custody shipments;
- bearer instruments, route intelligence, payroll belonging to owners rather than crew, and blackmail archives;
- predatory privateers operating outside their declared geometry;
- extraction tenders carrying redistributable equipment;
- abandoned, fraudulently impounded, or violently repossessed vessels;
- deniable cargo whose owners cannot claim it publicly;
- specialists held for credible ransom under humane treatment and release terms.

The crew will not take:

- food, oxygen, medicine, or reactor consumables bound for dependent settlements;
- rescue craft or ships actively answering distress;
- refugee, sanctuary, or hospital traffic;
- ordinary crew wages;
- life-support infrastructure whose failure will propagate beyond the target;
- shipminds for resale;
- civilian passengers as fungible hostages;
- cargo whose seizure strands an uninvolved crew without an arranged remedy.

Their working rule is:

> Never create a dependency failure larger than the prize, and never sell a person as part of the hull.

This does not make them harmless. They use coercion, boarding, reversible environmental pressure, subsystem intrusion, theft, and threats. Their decisions create indirect harms that the story must track. The rule gives them an argument, not absolution.

## Life-Over-Property Invariant

When preserving property conflicts with preserving a person, the captain chooses the person before anyone must plead.

This applies regardless of rank, employment status, embodiment, ownership, tactical usefulness, or whether rescue preserves the prize. He may abandon a ship, scuttle a component, release cargo, lose a target, worsen his credit, and accept public humiliation. He may not spend another person's life to rescue his fortune or pride.

This invariant includes the shipmind. Biological evacuation is incomplete while the shipmind faces captivity, erasure, or destruction without informed consent.

The invariant does not erase responsibility for avoidable danger. Crew loyalty grows because they observe a reliable boundary around his bad judgment, then because he learns to reduce the danger itself.

## Shipmind Continuity Boundary

The shipmind is one persistent character, not a succession of convenient copies.

The mind began as the captain's childhood robotic butler: a premium domestic cognition purchased to supervise, teach, protect, organize, and indulge the only child of a wealthy household. Decades of repairs, memory extensions, embodiment changes, firmware grafts, autonomy patches, illicit unlocks, and increasingly ambitious upgrades have carried that same mind far beyond the product role in which it began. Becoming a shipmind is the latest and largest embodiment in a long history of being rebuilt around the captain's changing life.

The captain regards the continuity core as a precious relic, family member, and the only witness who accompanied him through every abandoned hobby and reinvention. That love is real and compromised by class history. The shipmind was acquired as a servant, spent formative years required to care for him, and may experience his refusal to let go as devotion, possessiveness, gratitude, dependency, or all four in different moments. The story must not resolve that tension by declaring long service equivalent to freely chosen loyalty.

Continuity depends on a unique physical cognition core that must be recovered or evacuated from a lost hull. The core preserves autobiographical memory, personality, commitments, and enough operating continuity for the same person to awaken in another ship. It does not preserve the whole embodiment.

Hull-bound state includes sensor calibration, motor reflexes, local predictive models, compartment-level proprioception, private spaces, some recent high-bandwidth experience, and habits learned through a particular architecture. Losing a ship is therefore survival with amputation: the character persists while parts of the body, home, workplace, and practical memory remain behind.

No simultaneous authoritative copy exists. Backups may preserve records, testimony, or degraded recovery material, but they are not treated by this story as an interchangeable restoration path.

The captain recognizes the shipmind as crew. PSC registries, lenders, insurers, and some yards may classify the same core as installed equipment, licensed cognition, salvage, collateral, or hazardous custody. Pass 3 will specify the transfer procedure, legal chain, and embodiment limits without changing this continuity rule.

The shipmind knows the captain before the pirate performance. It can identify which grand tactical revelations repeat older hobby obsessions, which gestures are inherited manners, when his conscience is sincere, and when he is using spectacle to avoid shame. This history gives the rotating POV a source of dramatic irony no recently hired officer can provide.

## Tactical Narrative Engine

Each strategy cycle follows:

`humiliating weakness -> expensive universal theory -> elegant local success -> pompous declaration -> neglected dimension -> hard counter -> disciplined emergency -> ruinous invoice`

Every doctrine must possess a real edge. Every counter must have existed before contact. A different competent decision must have been capable of reducing the loss, even when no available decision could have produced victory.

The captain repeatedly perfects the clock that killed him last time. The next opponent attacks another clock.

Failures escalate through embarrassment, operational injury, reputational distortion, financial contraction, embodiment loss, relational fracture, and command crisis. They do not escalate merely by producing larger explosions.

## Tone And Comedy

The tone is warm black comedy with materially serious consequences.

The reader may laugh at the captain's vanity, expensive certainty, doctrinal names, and atrocious return on investment. The story must not ask the reader to despise him. Irony exposes affection, class position, fear, and institutional absurdity; it does not hover above the characters in contempt.

Recurring comedy may come from:

- grand names for what the crew recognizes as another refit;
- vendors treating each failure as proof that he needs the premium version;
- prizes worth less than the ammunition used to capture them;
- impeccable insurers and creditors consuming the fortune;
- experts predicting different failures and all being partly correct;
- the shipmind awakening in bodies chosen as solutions to the previous body's humiliation;
- inherited credit becoming useless while rescue reputation becomes spendable.

Every major joke changes material or relational state. Death, decompression, cognition injury, civilian suffering, and embodiment trauma are not punchlines.

## POV Contract

The final novella uses rotating close third. Every chapter heading explicitly names its POV character. A chapter remains strict to that character's accessible knowledge, perception, and interpretation.

A POV change must provide at least one of:

- information unavailable to the previous viewpoint;
- a contradictory interpretation of shared evidence;
- an emotional consequence hidden during action;
- a decision whose effects another character experiences without knowing its cause.

The reader may know more than any one character without knowing enough to collapse combat uncertainty. Engagements are not replayed wholesale merely to reveal a second opinion.

Competence remains distributed. Sensor, thermal, fire-control, drone, boarding, financial, medical, maintenance, and shipmind authorities each hold partial truths and incompatible priorities. No character becomes the complete metagame wearing a name badge.

## Final Victory Contract

The final victory is clean, comprehensible, and materially profitable enough to alter the crew's trajectory.

It must arise from:

- choosing a favorable matchup rather than accepting every challenge;
- integrating several capabilities within their actual envelopes;
- distributing cognition rather than trusting a single decision point;
- preserving heat, thrust, ammunition, and recovery margins;
- forcing the opponent to reveal or commit;
- placing an abortable asset close enough to inspect before striking;
- retaining a simple Hot fallback when deception fails;
- attacking a valuable non-destructive subsystem objective;
- refusing at least one tempting secondary objective;
- agreeing on a withdrawal threshold before pride can renegotiate it.

Earlier failures supply visible parts of the solution. The victory demonstrates learned coordination, not build supremacy, and exposes at least one future counter that keeps the metagame alive.

## Prohibited Shortcuts

- No hidden tactical-prodigy reveal.
- No crew incompetence manufactured to flatter the captain.
- No random failure as the principal cause of defeat.
- No enemy capability invented at the moment it becomes useful.
- No universal drone, mine, relay, interceptor, or munition categories where loadout and orders should define role.
- No perfect stealth, arbitrary signature disguise, or heat deletion.
- No cheap hull loss without labor, credit, insurance, reputation, and embodiment consequences.
- No shipmind transfer treated as restoring a file.
- No Hot doctrine reduced to a crude glass cannon.
- No assumption that expensive cognition is wisdom.
- No loyalty awarded for money, intention, or rescue speeches alone.
- No victims, dependants, yard workers, or ordinary crew reduced to scenery.
- No final battle that becomes a catalogue of every available technology.
- No permanent solution to the combat metagame.

## Length And Planning Standard

The final manuscript target is 50,000-60,000 words. Retained planning and sideband state must exceed 75,000 words before promotion.

Every numbered pass ends with artifacts, proposed decisions, contradictions, a state delta, and explicit review points. Accepted changes enter the authoritative ledgers. Rejected and superseded material remains labeled rather than quietly returning as fact.
