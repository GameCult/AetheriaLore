# Character Causality Replay

This ledger replays the accepted seven-campaign ladder through principal and recurring support characters. It owns who knew what, which options existed, who exercised authority, and what remained afterward. [[Failure Ladder]] continues to own physical outcomes. [[07 Character Causality Replay/Twelvefold Replay]] owns exact Corvid sideband state. [[07 Character Causality Replay/Social Body Replay]] owns external and aggregate action.

Each row tracks, in compressed form: `location; knowledge/belief; goal and available action; cognitive load/physical condition; material stake; relationship, loyalty, and decision residue`.

## Replay Rules

1. A character may act only from information available in their location and interfaces.
2. A correct objection does not grant command outside its owner's domain.
3. Equipment produces constraints and affordances. People choose what those constraints justify.
4. Agreement after impact cannot erase disagreement at commitment.
5. Rescue confidence, wage trust, peer attachment, institutional loyalty, and affection remain separate.
6. No later POV inherits private Corvid identity, Halcyon deliberation, target testimony, or another officer's diagnosis without a transmission path.
7. A character absent from a ship retains external state and receives only public, contractual, household, or deliberately shared information.

## Loyalty Audit

- **Rescue confidence:** high across most continuing crew after three complete evacuations.
- **Wage trust:** conditional on Tess's seniority rules and Wake/yard enforcement; never implied by rescue.
- **Peer attachment:** strong within cells and uneven across departments; a major reason some remain.
- **Institutional loyalty:** strongest in Samira, Tess, Dena, and several Twelvefold members because refusal becomes enforceable.
- **Affection for Luce:** Pal begins high but compromised; Sable begins warm; others range from mild fondness to none. No aggregate crew affection is authoritative.
- **Commitment to piracy:** contested even among people who stay. Rescue work, claimant access, technical autonomy, and household income can justify continued participation without romance about piracy.

## Knowledge Leak Audit

- Luce never learns exact Twelvefold identity topology or private vote sequence.
- No crew member learns Rhyne's internal conflict until later records or witnesses transmit it.
- Kavi's and Olan's withheld materials remain theirs; Halcyon receives bounded subsets.
- Meera knows public rescue and yard evidence before Campaign VII, not private command choices.
- Samira knows Pal postponed sanctuary only if Pal tells her; she does not know the private reason by default.
- Mara witnesses changed conduct but cannot access whether Khraa forgives her.
- Jo's valid Campaign VI solution and Ilya's semantic warning coexist; neither automatically knows the other's subjective confidence.
- Pal can observe ship interfaces, not unspoken motive. Their recognition of Luce's recurring obsession is inference grounded in history, not mind reading.
