# Economics Brief

The authoritative arithmetic is in [[../Ship And Refit Ledger]]. This packet records the economic design logic.

The PSCC settlement unit is deliberately relative. The story needs ratios that survive later currency naming: an elite first hull costs roughly nineteen months of routine operating burn; cash remaining after delivery supports fewer than five quiet months; a single damaged-drone sortie can consume an ordinary worker's annual income; and the first impressive prize can be profitable while still failing to amortize the doctrine.

One clean trust allocation becomes several dirtier claims. Rafi Sen takes settlement and provenance risk. Tern Undertow takes labor, raid, and blacklisting risk. The workers receive a casualty bond before urgency. Nobody is paid merely for silence; they are paid because a clean transfer into an outlaw warship is itself a map for Halcyon.

The crew's protected escrow prevents Luce from spending wages on tactical enthusiasm. Household remittances are separate line items because delayed pay is not an adventure for Nia Venn, Ren Ko, Ayo Nwosu, or the other dependants. The medical reserve is similarly fenced before ammunition replacement.

The Twelvefold arrangement is intentionally asymmetric: one economic cut, two political votes, twelve bodily liabilities, and twelve independent consent states. Collective organization saves human institutions from demanding twelve identical contracts. It does not discount rescue or injury.

The first campaign needs prizes quickly. Training, ordinary burn, and one combat sortie reduce practical runway to roughly three months. This pressure helps Luce mistake an elegant first win for validation and gives Tess a concrete reason to object when he wants to spend captured value on expanding the drone deck.
