# Material Operations Brief

## The Beautiful Bottleneck

_The Sum of Our Parts_ is not badly designed. It is brilliantly integrated around a mission profile that assumes staggered drone return. Luce's doctrine changes the sortie pattern without changing the service plant. That is the trap.

The visible drone gallery sells the joke and the tactical problem at once. Luce wanted crews and visitors to see recoverability as moral and economic refinement. The gallery puts recovery throats, manipulator rails, coolant services, and magazine handling in one legible neighborhood. Armor can protect the neighborhood from fragments. It cannot hide where sixteen hot machines must come home.

Sable can distribute approach windows. Chak can invent beautiful return geometry. Pal can shift coolant and doors. Deck crew can exceed every published turnaround. None can create an additional capture throat while loiterers occupy the lanes.

## Professional Opportunity To Object

Edda flags service concentration during construction. Sable answers that distributed scheduling prevents simultaneous return. Samira requires a 22% recovery reserve. Luce accepts the rule for trials, then adopts a 12% combat reserve after simulations show it extends the offensive screen. Nadi records that the plant meets the commissioned specification and that doctrine has changed around it.

The warning therefore exists. Nobody is stupid. The first favorable engagement staggers returns and appears to vindicate Luce. His error is treating a successful schedule as a physical guarantee.

## Pal's Embodiment

Pal experiences the cradle as expansion rather than possession: rooms become proprioception, coolant becomes fatigue, and crew footsteps become a domestic sense enlarged beyond anything the original butler product promised. The portable shell is not Pal's whole body while docked, but it is the only tissue that preserves continuous identity when the ship dies.

Luce commissioned handles, shock protection, and redundant batteries with almost devotional care. He also accepted fixture language that lets his family call Pal an heirloom. The same act protects Pal from casual disposal and makes custody legible to creditors. Love and ownership share a mounting bracket.

## Lived Ship

Zero-g belongs to labor and Corvid flight. The drum belongs to sleep, meals, medicine, argument, and the fiction of a stable home. Burn couches belong to command: everyone faces the same acceleration, every loose object becomes evidence, and no social rank can stand up.

Premium integration hides pipes behind beautiful panels. Deck and Plant cells spend more time opening the ship than Luce expected. Corvid access routes reveal several service conflicts before humans notice them. The Twelvefold cache volumes remain content-blind, but Edda knows their mass and fire rating; privacy does not become an excuse to lie to life support.
