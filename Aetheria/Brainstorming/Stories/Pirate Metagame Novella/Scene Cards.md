# Scene Cards

These cards are the authoritative drafting plan for the novella. They preserve scene causality without dictating finished prose. Every scene changes at least two dimensions among material state, knowledge, authority, relationship, belief, bodily condition, economics, reputation, and tactical geometry.

Fields:

- **Drive:** viewpoint objective and immediate conflict.
- **Boundary:** what the viewpoint knows, believes, and must not know.
- **Body:** tactical or sensory conditions and the maximum reader-facing clocks.
- **Work:** comedy mechanism and thematic labor.
- **Delta:** changed state at exit.
- **Obligation:** setup, payoff, or simulation-seam note carried forward.

## Movement I: Beautiful Answers

### Chapter 1: The Credential

#### 1.1 Regatta Courtesy

- **POV:** Luce.
- **Drive:** Win the Orsino-sponsored regatta beautifully enough that competence will feel like moral substance; Pal and the race officials keep asking him to conserve cooling margin.
- **Boundary:** Luce knows regatta systems and family etiquette. He cannot know the Serrat household or the automatic claim consequences.
- **Body:** Acceleration couches, polished sponsor traffic, rising sink load. One clock: cooling reserve.
- **Work:** Luce has commissioned emergency attire appropriate to being photographed after a rescue he does not expect to perform. Status preparation becomes practical embarrassment.
- **Delta:** He gives up his winning line to investigate _Mild Exception_; race position and self-image both change.
- **Obligation:** Seed Pal's habit of anticipating Luce's needs and Luce's conviction that preparation can make virtue reproducible.

#### 1.2 Six People

- **POV:** Luce.
- **Drive:** Keep six strangers alive while race control tells him the rescue craft is already assigned; the assigned response will arrive after their cooling margin expires.
- **Boundary:** He has direct telemetry and Pal's estimates, not the cause of the casualty or the survivors' private choices.
- **Body:** Transfer under residual spin, an overheating cabin, one improvised thermal connection. One clock: people versus cooling.
- **Work:** Callisto's post-shootdown etiquette makes waiting to classify the emergency institutionally correct and physically absurd.
- **Delta:** All six transfer alive; Luce loses the regatta and gains an authenticated rescue event.
- **Obligation:** Life-over-property invariant precedes piracy and crew loyalty.

#### 1.3 Automatic Assistance

- **POV:** Luce.
- **Drive:** Use his family credential to clear medical priority; the same credential opens an automatic recovery claim against Serrat's ship and household.
- **Boundary:** Luce sees the notice and Ilyana Serrat's reaction. He does not yet understand the full family-office machinery.
- **Body:** Quiet dock corridor, borrowed blanket, racing suit no longer comic enough to protect him.
- **Work:** A rescue form congratulates him while itemizing what the rescued people now owe.
- **Delta:** Serrat's gratitude becomes guarded hostility; Luce's pride becomes moral revulsion and a badly aimed desire to leave his caste.
- **Obligation:** Seed claims portfolio and the error that adventure can cure administered complicity.

## Drafting Restraints

- At most one polished thematic line may survive in a scene. If dialogue carries it, narration does not explain it.
- At least one scene per chapter leaves authority and theme implicit in who acts, waits, pays, cleans, or departs.
- No more than two consecutive scenes are dominated by seated or screen-mediated conversation.
- Every formal meeting has physical work, appetite, fatigue, embarrassment, distraction, or unresolved grievance in motion.
- Every chapter contains one ordinary observation that is not converted into symbolism.
- Each crisis contains interruption, repetition, a misunderstood referent, or an answer to the operational rather than emotional question.
- First drafts avoid polished “not X but Y” constructions unless later voice review earns the line.
- At least half of chapter exits land on image, gesture, interruption, bodily condition, or mundane work rather than interpretation.
- Each major institutional success leaves residue procedure cannot absorb.
- Bureaucratic irony belongs mainly to Tess and institutional encounters. Luce gets status embarrassment; Pal domestic mismatch; Samira exhausted understatement; Jo occupational enthusiasm; Mara literal friction; Sable recursive self-correction.
- Chapters 25-27 receive at most one polished procedural joke each.
- Hull One smells clean until fire makes premium insulation sweet and poisonous; Hull Two is metallic, loud, unevenly lit, and full of repaired surfaces; Hull Three is quiet, antiseptic, optically bright, and dependent on soft proprietary prompts; Hull Four smells of oil, laundry, hot metal, and shared food, with machinery audible enough to diagnose.
