# Ship And Refit Ledger

## Accounting Unit

Pass 3 uses the **PSCC settlement unit** (`SU`). It is a planning unit indexed to a standardized basket of certified habitat energy, life support, skilled labor, and freight capacity. It is not a claim that one stable coin dominates the corridor. All figures reconcile in SU and can later be rescaled without changing ratios.

### Doctrine of Reversible Superiority

The sixteen **Courtesy** PDC drones launch in two eights. Each carries passive sensors, a compact ballistic PDC, ammunition for ninety controlled bursts, 38 minutes of high-thrust maneuver, 3.5 hours of low-thrust loiter, recall authority, and a recovery transponder. They attack unresolved small objects, force defensive emissions, and return if the trace proves harmless. Capture cannon and Remorae exploit the revealed geometry.

The proposition is sound inside its envelope. Against a sparse sensor screen, the drones export risk, preserve abortability, and make the target pay to identify them.

The omitted constraint is **recovery topology**. Four external recovery throats each feed a capture lock, then a shared internal transfer corridor with twelve hot-parking cradles. A throat captures one drone every 70 seconds under ideal alignment, so sixteen simultaneous returns require four capture cycles: about 4 minutes 40 seconds from recovery start, or 3 minutes 30 seconds from the first completed capture to the last. The first four drones can enter service immediately; the remaining twelve park hot and cannot relaunch until moved through two service rails. The rails can cool, reload, inspect, and refuel four drones concurrently; a normal turnaround takes 24 minutes. Returning drones are low on thrust, hot, ammunition-poor, and geometrically committed during the external queue. Their approach vectors identify the parent. The capture locks, parking cradles, rails, coolant manifold, magazine lift, and fuel couplings share one armored service neighborhood.

Cheap mixed contacts need not defeat the drones. They can spend the drones' fuel and ammunition, observe the return fan, and place loiterers in the recovery lanes. Cognition can prioritize the queue. It cannot make four throats become sixteen.

The fifteen support crew are organized as five three-person cells. They are cross-trained, not interchangeable.

| Plant | Reactor, thermal plant, structure | Damage control and Pal extraction |
| Watch | Gunnery, sensors, logistics, galley | Medical response and communications |

Each principal officer stands one of three eight-hour watches or remains on a call rotation. Six support crew are awake in ordinary operations, twelve during contact, and all fifteen during launch/recovery or casualty. Edda is the only surgeon; one member of each boarding cell and two Watch-cell members hold trauma and augmentation-stabilization certification, while Plant can preserve Pal and damaged embodied systems for later treatment. This is response redundancy, not replacement expertise. Nobody receives a magical substitute expert: loss of a cell member reduces a named process.

### Habitation

The drum has twelve compact double cabins, three four-berth cabins, a six-place hot-bunk compartment used only at full complement, galley, clinic, washroom, exercise lane, and common table. The Twelvefold sleep where they choose among twenty-four distributed perches and four sealed emergency roosts; their six private cache volumes are structurally declared but content-blind. Human privacy is good by working-ship standards and poor by Luce's previous standards.

The zero-g spine contains plant access, magazines, cargo, drone deck, workshop, Corvid flight routes, and Pal's cradle. During burns the drum locks and everyone uses thirty-six assigned couches. The recurring domestic indignity is that an elegant rotating home must become a strapped-down industrial corridor whenever the engines tell the truth.

Food stores support 36 for 140 days. Potable water is 42 tonnes with 96.5% routine recovery. Atmosphere has three independently isolatable zones and 21 days of bottled reserve at full complement. The clinic holds four acceleration beds, two surgical cradles, twelve trauma cocoons, augmentation consumables, and species-specific Corvid respiratory and bone-care kits.

## Palinode Continuity Shell

Pal's unique cognition lattice occupies a custom **Orsino Household Continuity Shell**, built around obsolete family cryptography and repeatedly enlarged. The 118-kilogram shell is a blunt oval with cameras, microphones, lidar, speakers, tactile strips, wireless links, twelve hours of ordinary battery life, two hours of sustained emergency coordination, and 18 minutes of uncoupled tactical overclock before thermal throttling.

In the cradle, three optical control planes and a pumped thermal port unlock ship-scale sensor fusion, life support, motor prediction, and tactical scheduling. Disconnected, Pal retains identity, autobiographical memory, local senses, speech, encryption, and bounded wireless command. Pal can control intact ship relays, doors, life support, and autopilot while being carried, but loses direct high-rate weapon, drive, and whole-hull sensor control. Every destroyed relay shrinks the body Pal can still feel.

Two people can carry the shell in zero-g using opposed handles. Four are required in the habitation drum or under up to 1.5 g. Above that, it must move on its shock sled. The sled fits both lifeboats and has independent cooling for 40 minutes. Extraction takes 95 seconds with plant power, 4 minutes manually, or 11 minutes if the cradle is distorted.

Samira initiates full evacuation. Pal initiates core extraction while capable; Edda owns the physical procedure. Under Pal's witnessed incapacity directive, Samira may initiate and Edda execute. Biological urgency, not ownership rank, determines sequence, but the ship is not counted evacuated while Pal remains without informed consent. Pal may choose to remain; Luce may not make that choice for them.

Fixture registration still lists the shell as an Orsino trust antique. It provides liens, seizure claims, and arguments that inseparable hull improvements accrete to trust property. It provides no remote command path and no obedience authority.

## Procurement Chain

Rafi Sen converts clean trust distributions into distressed salvage purchases and repair claims. Those claims become Tern Undertow yard notes. Undertow purchases labor and components against the notes while reconstructing serial provenance. The initial chain loses 770,000 SU: 270,000 broker and settlement fees, 310,000 provenance haircut, 120,000 delayed-clearing premium, and 70,000 worker-exposure bond administration.

Large transfers remain signatures. Halcyon sees a regatta-derived spine, Orsino-compatible thermal geometry, Finch-adjacent service purchases, and unusual drone orders without yet resolving one pirate hull. Rafi can blur ownership, not erase material history.
