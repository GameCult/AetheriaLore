# Corvid Murder Brief

The [[../Cast Bible#The Twelvefold Borrowed Nest|Twelvefold Borrowed Nest]] is twelve persons, one contracted unit, one cut, and one campaign vote. `Huginn` and `Muninn` are rotating liaison roles, never stable identities.

Their human-facing ambiguity follows limits:

1. Any raven may use either codename, including simultaneously.
2. Encrypted internal IDs remain available to Pal and Edda for medicine, pressure doors, evacuation, pay, captivity, and casualties.
3. Identity play never lends consent, falsifies safety headcount, or converts twelve injury obligations into one.
4. True syrinx-names are autobiographical works. Humans may not record, reproduce, translate, or attach them to legal identity without permission.
5. Headcount jokes stop when anyone is missing. Counting the missing is older than the joke.

The late `POV: Huginn` chapter belongs internally to Kesh. It reveals simultaneous Corvid communication, parliamentary consent, cache and juvenile-extraction goals, and the fact that the human question "which one was Huginn?" was malformed. It does not decode prior appearances.
