# Social Body Brief

The crew's actions propagate through four linked economies:

- **Family custody:** Armand and Leonie Orsino love Luce and Pal; Sima Orlov turns rescue into liens, telemetry, and approved custodians.
- **Labor:** Varo Tern owns facilities, while Nadi al-Khatib and the Undertow Eighty-Seven own the work, schedule, injury exposure, and right to stop.
- **Reputation:** Ilyana Serrat witnesses rescue without absolution; Nacre-in-Current converts verified conduct into corridor credit; Wake-Without-Seal distributes incomplete social memory.
- **Hostile learning:** Ysabet Rhyne and Halcyon Risk Recovery correlate doctrine, serials, finance, and testimony into counters.

Dependants do not consent through crew contracts. Deferred shares, injury, delayed medicine, and exposure update their willingness to support the campaign. Target crews retain post-capture agency and determine whether the conduct rule has social force.
