# Failure Ladder

The accepted career contains seven pre-victory campaigns across 2672–2677, four hulls, and three whole-hull losses. The final synthesis is a separate eighth operation reserved for later architecture and drafting.

Every campaign obeys:

`creative thesis → small win → misleading validation → seeded counter → competent response → costly consequence → refit thesis`

The sequence does not treat every doctrine equally. Reversible Superiority returns after an initial warning because Luce believes professional corrections have repaired it. Hot ballistics and mixed clouds form two movements aboard one durable hull. The final victory will use accumulated judgment, not a fifth replacement ship or a universal build.

## Campaign I: The Refundable War

### Creative Thesis

Recoverable PDC drones turn the Callisto commitment dilemma into service work. They can investigate unresolved contacts, force defensive reveals, fight small hulls, and return if the contact proves harmless.

### Small Win

Eight Courtesies dismantle a sparse picket screen and force an escort to expose correction traffic. Jo keeps their bursts on intended solutions. Tess offers passage and back wages; a relay operator cooperates. Capture cannon and Remora pressure secure the prize without occupied-compartment penetration.

### Misleading Validation

Dena's Deck cell services the flight ahead of plan. Luce interprets exceptional labor as spare capacity. He wins a bounded council trial for a twelve-percent drone reserve under conditions Samira has formally constrained. The trial succeeds. Luce learns that its bounds were timid.

### Seeded Counter

A later target field contains heterogeneous cheap contacts and passive observers. The objects consume thrust, heat, ammunition, and cognition. The Courtesies win local exchanges and all but two come home. Recovery is not defeated in combat. It is revealed as work.

### Competent Response

Sable fragments the return queue. Chak makes false return leaders. Tik-tik refuses to route a private Collective courier through the exhausted screen even though doing so would improve the human-facing track picture. Jo converts two unrecoverable Courtesies into an intercept screen. Dena closes unsafe throats and refuses Luce's requested third sortie. Samira backs the refusal. Nobody invents another throat or refuels a drone by believing in it harder.

### Costly Consequence

Two Courtesies are destroyed, six require depot work, and the recovery neighborhood needs ten weeks of staggered repair. The favorable prize realizes roughly 740,000 SU; ammunition, overhaul, deferred ship maintenance, and labor cost roughly 1,460,000 SU. Dena records that the flight was technically recoverable only because her cell borrowed sleep, tendon health, and later maintenance from itself. Her household remittance pressure made that borrowing coercive. Nadi refuses Luce's proposed heroic rebuild schedule and imposes a funded casualty bond.

### Relationship And Refit Thesis

Samira learns that Luce obeys withdrawal authority once lives become concrete. Dena learns he will sacrifice machines for her cell but not that he understands the demand he created. Sable receives the first wound to the belief that coordination redeems physical concentration.

Luce hears the labor objection but concludes that the deeper error was producing a visible return history. The ship will stop telling the enemy it was ever present. Campaign IV, not this campaign, owns the eventual physical attack on recovery topology.

## Campaign II: Prior Absence

### Creative Thesis

Deep Quiet purchases enough uncertainty to mature surrender geometry before conventional combat begins. Preparation, passive baselines, cold deployment, and disciplined heat storage replace the need to launch visibly into ambiguity.

### Small Win

One stocked sensor/antenna Patient Hand is deployed eighteen hours before contact on a ballistic path toward a schedule-bound escort's radiator-dump volume. It attaches during the dump, blinds an aft sensor cluster, and threatens radiator authority. A debt-chartered crew accepts Tess's terms instead of dying for a hazard bonus that ends at capture.

### Misleading Validation

Luce credits silence rather than target routine, preparation, and credible surrender terms. He turns discomfort into evidence of discipline and seeks a longer quiet window.

### Seeded Counter

The next target varies schedules, sheds ambiguous objects, and earns by continuing transit rather than fighting. It never needs to find the pirates. It remains productive under uncertainty while the crew accumulates stale tracks, heat debt, atmosphere strain, augmentation faults, and dwindling maneuver choices.

### Competent Response

Rin catches her own atmosphere overlay translating oxygen debt as safety because a manual indicator disagrees. Edda declares the physiological limit. Kesh refuses to spend juvenile-route intelligence as a thermal continuation merely because it would make a convincing flight history; Vek chooses which duplicated cache can be left with the prepositioned hulls. Pal refuses to discard recent hull-local sensory memory into another false continuation. Luce accepts all three refusals. Samira aborts before either the crew or the ship crosses an irreversible threshold.

### Costly Consequence

The target completes transit. Prepositioned Remorae, detachable sinks, consumables, and augmentation life are lost. Rin's treatment and overlay replacement make the failure cost roughly 720,000 SU against 570,000 SU realized from the earlier capture. A Navigator notation damages Samira's professional record. Rin's mother refuses deferred shares; Tess places wages and household remittances ahead of refit spending.

### Relationship And Refit Thesis

Pal's refusal is accepted as embodiment authority rather than precious-property preservation. Samira's loyalty begins acquiring an external cost. The Twelvefold's Cold Choir advances private cache mapping while serving the mission, reminding the story that Luce is not everyone's principal objective.

Luce concludes that waiting cannot be allowed to become the whole engagement. Physical attachment should finish quickly once uncertainty creates an opening.

## Campaign III: The Courtesy of an Open Door

### Creative Thesis

Patient Hand Remorae manufacture surrender through selective loss of subsystem sovereignty. The target need not be destroyed if its crew can no longer trust sensors, weapons safing, drive authority, locks, or datanet continuity.

### Small Win

A pharmaceutical carrier's insurer-mandated maintenance architecture exposes standardized control surfaces. Remorae divide sensor, safing, and drive authority while Mara's team offers credible physical surrender. Target workers cooperate because Tess guarantees wages, medical continuity, and passage independently of title. The certified cargo produces roughly 1,750,000 SU, the best prize yet.

### Misleading Validation

Luce mistakes preserved property for minimized harm. Sable mistakes careful delegation for reproducible social alignment. The prize is called intact through seven incompatible certification definitions while an inspector refuses to enter it.

### Seeded Counter

A later target carries heterogeneous buses, false maintenance segments, local confirmation, analog indicators, distributed officers, hull-clearing charges, and a service route coupled to a passenger refuge. The architecture was designed after insurers studied standardized capture surfaces.

### Competent Response

Krrik authors the Twelvefold's signed operational objection without exposing private consensus. Ek authenticates evidence transmitted by target engineer Kavi Issen, but refuses Mara's proposal to forge Issen into protected custody for their own safety. Mara protects Issen and refuses entry before knowing they are useful. Jo safes compromised payloads. Luce backs the abort. The target severs sacrificial buses, destroys attached hardware, and escapes.

### Costly Consequence

Two Remorae are lost, one is contaminated, and the target publishes the intrusion family. The campaign's counter and subsequent certification changes cost roughly 1,700,000 SU, making the best prize almost break-even. Kavi Issen, an augmented life-support engineer under a passenger-care bond, refuses gratitude and independently publishes both facts: Mara prevented the entry, and the attack remained terrifying. Their employer terminates the bond for unauthorized disclosure; Wake helps them contest the debt without claiming them as a mascot. Insurers purchase the testimony and impose slower local confirmation on ordinary crews.

### Relationship And Refit Thesis

Mara protects an augmented person her politics misclassify without earning forgiveness. She repeats Corvid testimony as testimony, not machine output. Sable acts on refusal without architectural access. Wake-Without-Seal grants limited sanctuary because the crew accepted humiliating failure instead of redefining passengers as an acceptable loss.

Luce concludes that stand-off precision can remove resistance without entering another ship's social and environmental body.

## Campaign IV: The Open Mouth

### Creative Thesis

Reversible Superiority returns after real correction. The rebuilt doctrine preserves ordinary reserve, staggers flights, relocates before recall, assigns hull PDC to recovery defense, armors fuel couplings, adds sacrificial shutters, recognizes Dena's halt authority, and uses false return leaders. This is not Luce repeating the same plan unchanged. It is the crew's best professional answer to the first warning.

### Small Win

Several sorties recover safely under contested conditions. The corrected doctrine produces modest captures and appears to prove that the missing concern has been repaired.

### Misleading Validation

Luce believes the known bottleneck is now the mastered bottleneck. Rhyne's force instead attacks the distinction between recovering friendly and terminal hostile.

### Seeded Counter

The hostile field contains heterogeneous bait, passive observers, fragment dispensers, and two cold terminal loiterers. Evidence from the earlier recovery action and commercial yard ancestry gives Rhyne a broad service-topology hypothesis, not magical knowledge of exact internals.

### Robust Loss Chain

1. Courtesies clear bait and win local exchanges.
2. Samira translates before recall, invalidating the easiest return fan.
3. Fragment dispensers attack the broad recovery volume, damaging and desynchronizing friendly returns.
4. Dena closes two throats. One damaged Courtesy approaches a third with valuable sensor history aboard.
5. A loiterer uses the damaged drone and fragment field as physical occlusion, retaining terminal thrust inside safe hull-PDC geometry.
6. Trraak rejects Mara's proposed EVA interception route because the fragment volume has already made its return path nonviable. Mara accepts Corvid geometry over boarding intuition. Jo can destroy both approaching bodies before capture. Luce authorizes the shot. The correct shot turns them into a broad debris attack against machinery already exposed for recovery.
7. The opponent designed the fragment cloud to foul doors, sensors, couplings, and handling gear rather than penetrate one secret point. Debris jams the open sacrificial shutter and opens several external service runs, including a propellant transfer line.
8. Dena clears Deck, isolates the magazine lift, and dumps the affected manifold. Pal closes the transfer corridor.
9. A second already-terminal loiterer reaches the disabled open throat from another bearing and attacks the broad exposed service neighborhood. It need not thread one precise aperture.
10. Residual drone propellant and ammunition ignite in hot parking. Isolation saves habitation but cannot preserve the zero-g spine.
11. Fire and fragments sever one main coolant loop, the deck pressure boundary, and the control trunk between forward sections and drive plant.
12. The ship retains temporary life support and translation, but cannot arrest propagation toward the remaining magazine and Pal's cradle cooling before those deadlines mature.

The loss does not depend on one exact seeker percentage or one magical weak point. Once a shutter is jammed, propellant is open, and a second terminal body is already inside the defended geometry, the internal fuel and ammunition supply most of the destructive energy.

### Competent Response And Evacuation

Samira declares evacuation while drive and one life-support zone remain stable. Dena's halt saved her cell, but Dena now has to choose between joining Pal's four-carrier team and reaching a trapped deck worker. Edda gives the worker priority. Pal yields their place in the first boat and accepts a slower manual extraction that will cost more hull-local memory. Vek abandons an irreplaceable private cache when Khraa proves the same route is needed to conceal a damaged lifeboat. Sable breaks the cloud into defensive cells; the Twelvefold obscures departure without pretending those sacrifices were unanimous.

The declaration comes with at least twenty-two minutes before the remaining magazine or cradle-cooling deadline. Habitation reaches two independent boats through the aft pressure route in eleven minutes. Edda and Plant finish Pal's manual extraction in fifteen; Dena and the recovered worker board at seventeen. Both boats separate by nineteen minutes, leaving several minutes of coarse margin rather than a last-second threshold. Pal controls doors, atmosphere, and fire isolation wirelessly from the sled until deliberately severing the last relay.

Luce leaves when Pal tells him his presence no longer helps. He abandons remaining drones, cargo, the observation drum, and the table rated for combat acceleration. _The Sum of Our Parts_ is scuttled after all thirty-six biological persons and Pal clear. This loss's dramatic action is **escape**: survival depends on negotiated triage while the home is actively dying.

### Costly Consequence

The first home is lost. Campaign loss is planned at roughly 10,800,000 SU after residual salvage; only about 400,000 SU of preceding modest captures is realizable. The Twelvefold loses caches unknown to humans. Pal experiences the first death of a ship-body. Dena helps carry the continuity shell after the machine that turned her competence into spectacle burns behind her.

### Refit Thesis

The next hull will have distributed machinery, separate bays, removable Pal integration, fewer recoverable drones, passive storage, and no beautiful throat through which every useful thing must return.

## Campaign V: The Last Plausible Ship

### Creative Thesis

Bounded heat ferries present several physically possible histories while the parent performs a plausible cousin inside its own envelope. The doctrine taxes attention and commitment without requiring invisibility or compelled cognition.

### Small Win

A cargo escort's conduct policy requires it to inspect distress before firing through occupied volumes. Converted relay hulls draw active apertures and patient weapons away from the parent. Ordinary correction traffic and aspect changes reveal command geometry. Remorae exploit the opening. A target worker cooperates after Tess's guarantees. The campaign realizes roughly 900,000 SU and remains a deliberately abbreviated profitable bridge.

### Misleading Validation

Luce believes he authored the opponent's reality. Olan Tere, a sensor officer injured during the capture, retains the raw baselines and publishes the shaping family's historical inconsistencies after their employer denies compensation. Halcyon purchases Tere's testimony and distributes a coarse rule: preserve baselines, force aspect changes, assign weapons to possible parent volumes, and do not solve suspicious performances.

### Seeded Counter

Rhyne uses separated passive observers, cheap illuminators, patient ballistic patterns, and a convoy continuing toward protected space. The enemy does not need to determine which apparent ship is real.

### Robust Loss Chain

1. Ferries establish several mutually plausible histories before contact.
2. The parent coasts inside the residual volume that keeps those histories consistent.
3. Cross-aspect illumination forces a conflict between thermal claims and implied motion. Tlak recognizes that the enemy is testing history rather than identity; Qerr argues that one more coherent performance may still buy time.
4. Ilya collapses one lie early to widen escape choices. Ikk-rr refuses to imitate a distress call as the replacement history, preserving a contract boundary while narrowing the mask.
5. Samira chooses a hard burn before the convoy reaches protection.
6. The burn reveals the parent. Rhyne's weapons were already assigned to the residual volume.
7. Jo defeats the first pattern. A second crosses the limited translation corridor imposed by ferries, exclusion geometry, and detachable sinks.
8. The courier hull's commercial ancestry makes its long translation roots and detachable-sink rails externally inferable broad regions. The distributed pattern scores several coarse hits across both rather than one miraculous combined wound.
9. Local controls isolate damaged clusters, but lost authority on one side independently drives an increasing tumble; the fouled sink accelerates the thermal deadline without being necessary to create it.
10. Radiator deployment, safe main-drive firing, and navigation become mutually incompatible. Continuing thrust risks breakup; remaining contained cooks occupied compartments; radiating does not restore control and exposes the ship to follow-up.
11. Samira declares the hull lost before structural or thermal deadlines become casualty events.

### Competent Response And Evacuation

Ilya breaks the mask before full exhaustion. Samira burns out rather than remain trapped. Jo defeats the first incoming pattern. Sable spends cognition on evacuation geometry, not preserving the deception archive. Pal remains cradle-connected while lifeboats match the tumble, then disconnects into portable state and sends bounded timing requests to the independently powered Kestrel local cluster controller. Kestrel, already authorized by Samira for separation burns, owns the actual drive pulses. Pal refuses to sacrifice autobiographical memory as a heat decoy; Luce obeys. Four carriers restrain the shell through the drum.

Evacuation is declared with roughly forty minutes before the conservative occupied-volume heat limit. The tumble makes only one boat interface usable at a time; all thirty-six people transfer in three restrained waves by twenty-four minutes, Pal disconnects at twenty-six, and both boats separate by thirty. Remote stabilization remains theoretically possible, but only by sending workers back into a tumbling hull before the fouled sink cooks the access drum. Luce orders that nobody return. Rhyne marks _A Reasonable Doubt_ as an insurer-controlled hazard rather than firing on lifeboats.

The hull remains physically extant long enough for its loss to be a decision. This loss's dramatic action is **abandonment**: the crew could gamble bodies to preserve property, and refuses. Mismatched replacement panels, supplier labels nobody could agree whether to remove, and the ugly domestic modifications that helped the heat performance all rotate away together.

### Costly Consequence

Hull Two, its converted ferries, remaining small hulls, and most cargo are lost. Planning loss is roughly 5,900,000 SU after hazard/salvage claims. Pal loses a second embodiment and spends the next interval without a full cradle. Samira's early abort is publicly described as panic by people who were not inside the tumble.

### Refit Thesis

The next ship will not ask an audience to cooperate. It will sense better, decide faster, and place energy precisely enough to end resistance before a performance exhausts itself.

## Campaign VI: Exact Wrongness

### Creative Thesis

Premium cognition across sensing, classification, command, fire control, guidance, and damage control allows precision energy to disable only what resistance requires. Better cycling means more shots on intended solutions, not merely faster mechanism.

### Small Win

Against a known centralized architecture, low-energy questioning pulses and precise follow-up disable exposed sensors and drive-control roots. The target surrenders. The nearly pristine prize realizes roughly 1,100,000 SU.

### Misleading Validation

Luce concludes that sufficient intelligence converts uncertainty into ethics. Sable's political commitment to distributed autonomy collides with the exhilaration of being indispensable.

### Seeded Counter

A later target uses sacrificial apertures, false but real radiator-control structures, heterogeneous local control, distributed ballistic launchers, manual fallbacks, and willingness to lose valuable external equipment. Halcyon has attributed Sable's Finch-derived stack through clinic diagnostics sold by an upstream calibration subcontractor and the polished uncertainty grammar emitted during the first capture. That provenance permits architecture-specific adversarial emissions; generic contradiction alone would only create load. The target's technicians consciously preserve coarse capability rather than protecting every component.

### Robust Loss Chain

1. Precision fire destroys genuinely functional but nonessential structures, producing convincing evidence that the model is correct.
2. Hostile emissions create contradictory subsystem interpretations. Geometry remains good while semantic confidence becomes contaminated.
3. Rruk recognizes an architecture-specific cadence and demands confidence isolation and a Slow Vote. Pal confirms the contamination risk. Sable refuses the first partition request, believing the architecture can resolve the contradiction without surrendering coordination.
4. The resulting delay carries the ship beyond the withdrawal margin Samira named before contact; it is not important whether this lasts one cycle or several.
5. Accurate fire destroys another sacrificial structure and consumes part of the charged and thermal margin. The system explains its own uncertainty with such polished assurance that Sable initially reads legibility as control.
6. Sable recognizes contamination and accepts partition. Jo and Mara independently identify retained coarse control. Mara treats Pal's isolation order as bodily authority rather than damaged-machine behavior. Samira orders withdrawal.
7. The target's broad ballistic patterns were launched during the semantic loop at exposed energy-handling surfaces, not after a miraculous realization.
8. Procurement ancestry and prior firing traces expose several broad energy-handling volumes: capacitor transfer trunks, optical cooling roots, and dump paths. The target saturates those inferable regions rather than knowing one secret weakness. PDC defeats most of the pattern; multiple fragments and one penetrator intersect the energy neighborhood.
9. The distributed damage shorts a charged transfer bus, ruptures dielectric coolant between two banks, and disables one external dump path.
10. Automatic isolation preserves habitation. One bank dumps externally; the other cannot dump through the damaged path or into thermal storage already consumed by precision fire.
11. Pal and Edda isolate the second bank, removing power from main fusion, a translation cluster, and cradle overclock.
12. Local drive power remains, but safe command distribution and cooling do not. The energy neighborhood will arc, vent conductive debris, or cook the adjacent bus before a safe burn can complete. Any one damaged volume would be reparable; their externally targetable overlap makes the loss robust.

The opponent's deception causes costly delay, not compelled behavior. The already-launched ballistic pattern produces the first hit; the pirates' own stored energy turns it into an unrecoverable internal condition.

### Competent Response And Evacuation

Sable relinquishes control. The Slow Vote produces the correct conclusion too late for withdrawal but in time for survival. Jo's PDC saves most of the ship. Automatic isolation prevents immediate death. Pal sheds ship-scale cognition and refuses Sable's attempt to remain connected to preserve the archive. Luce backs Pal; Mara enforces Pal's isolation order against Sable without describing either cognition as property. Edda blocks salvage of premium hardware. The Twelvefold carries isolated navigation and casualty state after the central network becomes untrusted.

The damage can be stabilized only by an external rescue tug supplying cold power, physical bus separation, and custody over the untrusted network. With at least fifty minutes before the conservative vent deadline, the crew reaches both boats through two mechanically isolated routes by twenty-one minutes; Pal's powered extraction completes at eight minutes and portable state transfers at twenty-three. Separation completes by twenty-nine. Samira formally surrenders the disabled hull to the tug under rescue seniority rather than risk workers on a live charged bus. Halcyon acquires the rescue lien, the family trust asserts Pal's fixture claim, and Undertow lacks cash to redeem either. The crew survives; _The Benefit of Hindsight_ is dispossessed, stripped, and later scrapped.

Sable's partition injury leaves them unable for weeks to reconcile left-side drone proprioception with their own hand position; their speech occasionally completes a system explanation after they have consciously rejected it. Pal's autobiographical core remains continuous while more hull-local working memory is lost than in either previous embodiment. This loss's dramatic action is **surrender**: competence preserves the ship long enough for law and debt to take it.

### Costly Consequence

Hull Three, energy refit, treatment, and defaults produce a planning loss of roughly 8,100,000 SU. Sable suffers a partition injury. Tavi Ro conditions continued calibration on sharing scarce clinic access with ordinary patients. Ship Mutiny circles offer Pal independent sanctuary. Pal declines immediate separation without Luce answering for them.

### Refit Thesis

The fourth ship will make fewer dependencies elegant. It will be armored, accessible, locally controllable, difficult to deceive, and very easy to see.

## Campaign VII: The Honest Ship

### Opening Bet: Evident Intent

Hot doctrine abandons the masking contest and wins an interruption race. Inside a constrained extraction corridor, active sensing, armor, redundant directors, and cold ballistic hazard volumes force a quiet attacker to maneuver or withdraw. Jo keeps rounds on evolving solutions. Mara trusts machinery whose danger is visible. The ship wins cleanly.

The victory is the opening bet of one continuing contract, not a separate campaign cycle. Luce concludes that commitment can substitute for selection. A distributed opponent presents a decoy worthy of pursuit while another operator matures the contract's real objective elsewhere. Hull Four can dominate one engagement volume, not occupy two. Luce initially commits toward the apparent threat; Samira identifies the objective error and refuses further pursuit. He accepts her ruling, but the initial burn has made return too late. The ship wins every exchange it reaches and loses the first objective, spending ammunition and propellant the remainder of the contract cannot replace.

The penalty and lost-objective clause force the same crew into the contract's salvage phase. They cannot reset emotionally or materially between lessons.

### Climax: The Parliament of Useful Things

Bounded mixed clouds restore option value without making every hull universal. A defensive PDC element becomes an offensive screen; relays preserve track; Remorae exploit a forced reveal. The first defender wastes time classifying stable roles and yields a modest prize.

Luce and Sable briefly treat flexibility as freedom from shared infrastructure. The next opponent defines consequence volumes, partitions links, uses fragmenting fire, and injects false distress. It attacks space, bandwidth, parent geometry, and service capacity rather than solving identities.

Sable delegates local control before contamination. The Twelvefold records its split rather than manufacturing unanimity. Soren and technician Meera Tann are servicing a detached recovery tender when fragment fire cuts its return corridor and damages the parent launch blister. Mara intends to preserve both rescue shepherds while Sable wants one as a relay; Khraa challenges Mara's assumption that human security owns their deployment, and Mara negotiates a bounded rescue order rather than asserting unilateral command. Luce upholds the reserve. The capture fails. The withheld shepherds retrieve Soren and Meera. Dena halts recovery; Jo retains the parent rather than pursuing the prize.

Combined Hot and mixed-cloud campaign loss reaches roughly 3,900,000 SU; total Campaign VII realizable captures are roughly 950,000 SU. Wake denies open-ended ammunition and refit credit until wages, casualty reserves, and service labor are funded. The hull survives. The crew cannot afford another answer.

### Relationship And Final Thesis

Sable demonstrates growth by delegating before the system proves them wrong. Samira's attachment has cost a credible exit. Support workers do not become uniformly loyal: they now trust the rescue invariant, while trust in wages, Luce, piracy, and the institution remains individual. Some leave after each hull loss. Those who stay receive senior remittances, treatment, and enforceable halt authority rather than speeches. Pal sees Luce stripped of the ability to express care by buying another ship.

The final operation must use _The Burden of Proof_ as it exists. It must be selected around an actual objective, preserve rescue reserve, use quiet briefly, shape without demanding belief, attach only within collateral limits, partition cognition before contamination, threaten with Hot force, and refuse the tempting chase.

Track three values in prose and ledgers:

- **Face value:** what Luce announces.
- **Realizable value:** what survives claims, restitution, certification, and fencing.
- **Cash now:** what remains after protected wages, medicine, dependants, yard obligations, and service stock.

## Final Synthesis Requirements

The Failure Ladder does not select the exact final target, but it locks the conditions of victory:

1. _The Burden of Proof_ survives; no fifth capital hull appears.
2. The crew chooses a matchup that fits its actual build and conduct limits.
3. Luce's useful contribution is objective selection and refusal of a tempting chase, not purchasing another capability.
4. The Twelvefold's independent objective materially determines target and timing.
5. Pal receives a credible exit and later chooses the crew without Luce answering first.
6. Samira receives a credible external offer and remains by choice.
7. Sable delegates before contamination and remains proudly enhanced.
8. Mara's changed conduct earns bounded trust, never universal forgiveness.
9. Target workers cooperate for their own reasons under credible terms.
10. Distributed coalition value after the operation robustly exceeds cumulative campaign losses without making Luce personally rich or redeeming the original investment as wise.
11. Ysabet or another competent observer can identify a future counter. The metagame remains alive.
