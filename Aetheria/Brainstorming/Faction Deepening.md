# Faction Deepening

> **Status: brainstorming, Imagination pass of 2026-10-03.** Not canon. Each deepening note separates what the vault already says from what it proposes; everything under a note's **Proposal for canon** heading needs the operator's ruling.

Operator brief, 2026-10-03: "This is kinda Ghostlight territory but I'd like to do a pass on the Aetheria lore to spice up and deepen some of the factions that haven't gotten any love. Like, we can describe how Megiddo behave, how they design their stuff, because we know so much about them. Few of the cultures in the game are as legible, maybe Lucent and Zhestokost come close."

**Legible** here means: from one internal logic you can predict how a faction's people behave, how they build, what they value and fear, and how they treat strangers. [[Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Factions/Megiddo|Megiddo]] is the benchmark: one unanswered question ("if this is Israel, where is it?") predicts its two skies, its Vaults, its perimeter, its ping taboo and its exit costs.

## Deepened

Six factions, chosen because they are flags in Faction Play (or catalog records) whose quirks are already designed but whose lore cannot explain them yet:

- [[Brainstorming/Faction Deepening/Miss Terri's Sugariffic Snack Company|Miss Terri's Sugariffic Snack Company]]: the dose is the only thing that holds still at the edge of the world.
- [[Brainstorming/Faction Deepening/Adrasteia|Adrasteia]]: being found is the original harm; the vouch is the only door.
- [[Brainstorming/Faction Deepening/NiteLife Energy|NiteLife Energy]]: the night shift that became a nightlife, and the cartridge that spoils.
- [[Brainstorming/Faction Deepening/Lightsail Express|Lightsail Express]]: the motto is a lease clause, and the drivers know it.
- [[Brainstorming/Faction Deepening/Ewan Hart|Ewan Hart]]: a crop can be regrown, a farmer can't, and the seed is never dropped.
- [[Brainstorming/Faction Deepening/Alakrita|Alakrita]]: the ship, not the pilot, breaks off when scratched.

Two contrasts run through the set and are worth keeping if the notes are adopted. Lightsail never drops its load and Ewan Hart always does, except the seed. Miss Terri's feeds you to change you; Ewan Hart feeds you to keep you.
