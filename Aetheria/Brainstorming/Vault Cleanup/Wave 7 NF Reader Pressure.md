# Wave 7: Neuromorphic Firmware Reader Pressure

Status: non-canon analysis. Story evidence may identify pressure, but story-local or draft claims do not become setting-wide canon without independent support.

An isolated [[Neuromorphic Firmware]] product-list snippet left a fresh reader unable to tell what NF is, what survives reduction, what a customer receives, or why it exists beside ordinary software and [[Artificial General Intelligence]]. The Loremaster checked [[Cognitum]], [[Mind Uploading]], [[Identity Abyss]], [[Upload Continuity Hearings]], and [[Upload Vivisection Expose]].

## Supported answers

- NF is a commercial category for upload-derived cognition, not one stable kind of entity.
- Capture produces a runnable mind-state. Each instantiated branch acquires independent continuity through divergent experience, whatever an asset registry claims.
- Cognitum conditions branches and may preserve or remove capacities to produce outputs ranging from constrained subjects to narrow artifacts.
- Product language, interfaces, intermediaries, and contracts conceal provenance and deny continuity more reliably than they settle consciousness.
- Institutional buyers seek contextual human dispositions such as attention, attachment, fear, care, and spatial judgment. The vault does not establish a general performance advantage over AGI.

## Unresolved pressure

- Canon does not define whether a typical artifact is an intact mind behind a narrow interface, an excised cognitive process, a derived model, or several of these.
- Ownership from source contract through scan, branch, integration, and license remains implied rather than mapped.
- Typical deployment behavior remains unknown. Some derivatives retain memory and refusal; adaptation, self-reference, and auditability are unspecified.
- No supported benchmark explains where NF outperforms AGI or narrow software.
- No general consciousness test, provenance label, or right-to-know regime is established.

## Cut for Wave 7

The rewrite must define the category, branch lineage, output spectrum, and administrative denial without inventing a universal reduction taxonomy or comparative benchmark. Cognitum owns procurement, labor, markets, and deniability; the technology note owns transformation. Remaining operational gaps stay visible for later stories and notes to earn.
