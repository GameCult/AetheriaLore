# Vault Cleanup

This room tracks evidence-backed repairs to the Aetheria vault. It is not canon. Its tickets exist to make canonical notes shorter, better supported, and easier to navigate.

## Repair Contract

- Modeling identifies split authority, contradiction, duplication, unsupported claims, and navigation failures.
- Imagination researches stronger mechanisms and rhetorical shapes before prose is replaced.
- Hands performs bounded cuts against named files and word limits.
- Soul verifies the actual diff against canon, negative checks, and reader-visible behavior.
- A repair is incomplete until Soul passes it. Smooth prose is not evidence.

Brevity is an invariant. Every paragraph must own a necessary claim. Every claim needs canonical support, an identified inference, or an explicit uncertainty. Examples must prove mechanism or consequence. Lists do not substitute for models. Expansion is guilty until it earns its words.
