# Ewan Hart

> **Status: brainstorming, 2026-10-03.** Index: [[Brainstorming/Faction Deepening|Faction Deepening]]. Everything under **Proposal for canon** is invention awaiting the operator's ruling.

## Already Established

- [[Worldbuilding/Pre-Elysium/Factions/Powers/Minor/Ewan Hart|Ewan Hart]]: a late-Sol firm building the systems habitats need to grow food: seed architectures, closed-loop ecologies, microbial soil systems, and the slow logistics of keeping habitats fed after glamour industries leave. Clients can become dependent on proprietary ecologies. "Civilization is whatever still grows after the convoy fails."
- Midwestern US, tractor, dieselpunk, bluegrass; "Money does grow on trees"; "Space Tractor: It's a tractor. In space."; a voice that delivers a whole pitch in one unimpressed sentence ([[Brainstorming/Faction Flavor and Visual Identity#Ewan Hart Inc|Faction Flavor]]).
- Faction Play: no combat quirk, and none is invented (Cut List). Tractors and haulers flee and jettison; expressed through resources; its gear appears on AU and Aya ships.

## Proposal for Canon

### Founding Logic: A Crop Can Be Regrown, a Farmer Can't

Ewan Hart's people are the ones who stayed when the convoy failed. Every habitat they serve has, at least once, watched the glamorous suppliers leave and the shipments stop, and learned that dinner is not a product but a schedule: plant, tend, harvest, keep the seed, start again. Miss the schedule once and people starve a season later.

So the faction's logic is: **keep the schedule; everything else is replaceable, except the seed.** It explains the cut combat quirk without inventing one. An Ewan Hart hauler drops its cargo the moment it is threatened because grain can be regrown and a farmer cannot. It is the exact opposite of [[Brainstorming/Faction Deepening/Lightsail Express|Lightsail]], and both factions know it.

### Day to Day and Under Pressure

Ewan Hart habitats **invent seasons**. A tin can in vacuum has no autumn, so they make one: light cycles, temperature shifts, planting and harvest holidays, and a slow winter for soil rest. Outsiders find it uncanny to dock in a sealed habitat where everyone is wearing jackets because it is "October".

Under pressure they lower their heads and protect the schedule. They will abandon a cargo, a station module or a contract without much argument. They will not abandon a planting.

### How They Build

- **Built for gloves.** Big covers, big gauges, couplings you can work with numb fingers at the end of a fourteen-hour harvest. The tractor proportions in [[Brainstorming/Ship Design Language#Ewan Hart|Ship Design Language]] are about access, not nostalgia.
- **The implement is the ship.** A power unit plus interchangeable tools. An Ewan Hart hull is never finished; it is wearing this season's implement.
- **Fixed, not replaced.** Chipped enamel, welded patches and auction-bought parts are pride. A new machine is suspicious; it has not proved itself through a harvest.
- **The seed canister.** Every Ewan Hart vessel carries a small armored seed canister bolted to the frame, the one thing aboard designed never to be jettisoned.

### Values, Fears, Humor

They value **the harvest that came in**, and patience as a moral quality. They fear **blight**, and this is their deepest wound, because it is their own doing: proprietary ecologies mean every settlement on the same soil line shares the same weakness. One blight on one licensed line can empty a dozen habitats' tables in a season, and Ewan Hart's field reps have seen it.

Their humor is understatement. A collapsed module is "a bit of weather". A pirate raid is "visitors". The Space Tractor slogan is their whole sense of comedy in six words.

### Rituals and Customs

- **The first plate.** The first harvest of a new settlement is eaten by its people and the Ewan Hart field rep together, from the same pot. Refusing the first plate is the deepest insult the company knows. (Miss Terri's feeds you to change you; Ewan Hart feeds you to keep you.)
- **Starter culture.** Each settlement's soil microbes are a lineage, kept like sourdough and handed down. Married couples mix their families' soils.
- **Fiddle at the harvest.** Bluegrass is work music first: harvest crews play in shifts so the field is never silent.

### Outsiders and the Player

Everyone can buy dinner. Ewan Hart zones are low-security resupply stops because they have never seen the point of guarding food from hungry people. A player who drops cargo for them in a crisis, or carries seed to a failing settlement, is remembered for a generation, because Ewan Hart memory runs on seasons, not shifts.

### Internal Tension: Seed-Savers and Licensing

**Licensing**, at head office, owns the proprietary soil and seed lines. A settlement that breeds its own is in breach.

**The seed-savers** are field reps who quietly teach settlements to reproduce their ecologies, because they have seen blight. They lean toward the [[Worldbuilding/Pre-Elysium/Factions/Powers/Major/Aya Collective|Aya Collective]], and Aya's sanctuaries are full of soil that, on paper, belongs to Ewan Hart.

### Only If You'd Been There

- The farmhands' coveralls carry a pocket sewn for seed packets, and nobody leaves the habitat without one in it.
- Every Ewan Hart station has a real clock showing the habitat's invented season, and visitors' watches are politely corrected.
- Drill bits on the Good Old Boy are named after dogs.

## Tie-Back to Play

This keeps the Cut List intact: no combat quirk, and the flee-and-jettison behavior now has a reason. It proposes one resource-and-narrative lever, the **seed canister**: an Ewan Hart hauler drops its cargo but never its seed, so a player can take the grain freely, but disabling a hauler to take the seed is a different act, remembered *after reputation* by Ewan Hart and Aya. Without reputation, it is still a dock-story engine: blight on a soil line, a seed run to a starving settlement, a Licensing auditor hunting a seed-saver. Invented seasons are a cheap station-dressing idea for the content pass. For Ship Design Language, add "a small armored seed canister bolted to the frame, painted cream" to the faction block.
