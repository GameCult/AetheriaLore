## Proposal for Canon

### Founding Logic: Being Found Is the Original Harm

Adrasteia was founded by people who had been found once. This proposal leaves who they were open (a hunted research staff, a population catalogued and claimed under [[Narrative Themes#Administrative Personhood|administrative personhood]], survivors of the first negent generation), but fixes the lesson they drew: **a thing that can be located can be owned.** Borders, armies and addresses are all ways of admitting where you are. Adrasteia chose not to need them.

The name fits. Adrasteia is "the inescapable", an epithet of Nemesis. A people that cannot be found cannot be escaped either.

Following the operator's 2021 note, Adrasteia should own **heat, not all of stealth**. ECM and waveguide optics can belong to others. Adrasteia's monopoly is the thermal ledger: the right to be colder than the universe, and the bill for it.

### Day to Day and Under Pressure

Adrasteians do not make appointments; they arrive. They have no addresses, only introductions. Commerce happens through blind drops, which is why the Enigma Device ships in an unmarked box with no receipt.

The institution behind "a guy who knows a guy" is **the vouch**. Nobody enters Adrasteian space except on someone's vouch, and the voucher carries the newcomer's debts. Credentials are not ID cards but chains of vouches, which is why stealing a high-ranking ship's ID is a real heist: you borrow someone's whole chain.

Under pressure they go quiet, and stay quiet until the charges run out. Then they become loud on purpose, briefly, and leave.

### How They Build

- **Keyed, not labeled.** Parts carry no markings, so maintenance cannot rely on reading. Every facet is keyed instead: a wrong part does not fit. An Adrasteian technician repairs by feel in the dark.
- **Closed surfaces.** Nothing protrudes that would announce a function. Apertures sit under overhanging facets.
- **One facet different.** The asymmetric facet in [[Brainstorming/Ship Design Language#Adrasteia|Ship Design Language]] is the builder's signature: each yard cuts one facet its own way, so Adrasteians read a hull's origin where outsiders see only a wedge.
- **Friendliness under the lamp.** The UV smiley is the ethic in miniature: Adrasteian kindness exists, but only under the right inspection light.

### Values, Fears, Humor

They value **silence that was chosen**. They fear the cold death more than anyone, because they live nearest it: their nightmare is a crewmate who says she feels fine. Their humor is bone-dry and mostly consists of not answering. A customer complaint answered with a single "Shhh." is an in-house classic.

### Rituals and Customs

- **The warm hour.** Once per shift, crews deliberately run the ship warm and gather in one compartment, close together, with noir jazz playing loud. It is the only scheduled noise in Adrasteian life, and it mirrors the combat reheat flare: loudness is something they schedule, never something that happens to them.
- **The cheek check.** The greeting is the back of a hand laid against the other person's cheek, checking for warmth. The answer to "how do you feel?" is "warm". Anyone who answers "fine" is pulled off watch.
- **Charge calling.** Crews call their remaining charges aloud at every watch change, the way sailors once called depth.

### Outsiders and the Player

They sell to everyone and meet nobody. Outsiders are customers or vouchees, never guests. A player who enters on a vouch is safe exactly as long as the voucher is satisfied, which makes every favor for Abby's contact a real obligation. A player who enters without one is, to Adrasteia, a tracking device with a pilot.

### Internal Tension: The Hollow and the Ledger

**The Hollow** wants to sell less. Every charge sold is a trail back to the maker, and the founders hid for a reason.

**The Ledger** sells charges as widely as possible on purpose. If every navy, smuggler and yacht depends on Adrasteian charges, nobody can afford to find and destroy the maker. It is NiteLife's business model run as a survival strategy. The Ledger is winning, which is why other manufacturers are nervous.

### Only If You'd Been There

- Adrasteian interiors are kept slightly too warm. Visitors sweat; residents relax.
- Their stations have no signage. Corridors are told apart by the temperature of the handrails.
- Every Adrasteian keeps an ordinary hot drink within reach, and nobody ever finishes it.

## Tie-Back to Play

The vouch gives Faction Play's "narrative clearance rather than open borders" a mechanism and matches the 2022 clearance-and-ID-heist route, with Abby's contact as the first voucher. The cheek check and the warm hour give the reheat flare a cultural reading, so the tell ("a hole in the background, then a flare") is also how Adrasteians live. Per the operator's 2021 note, the [[Brainstorming/Corporate Roster and Item Wishlist|Corporate Roster]] should consider moving ECM and optical stealth to other brands and keeping Adrasteia on negent and charges, which sharpens its single lesson: heat is a ledger. The Enigma Device cloak would then need a ruling: keep it as the one exception, or rehome it. For Ship Design Language, the keyed-facet rule adds one line: "no labels anywhere; parts fit only one way."
