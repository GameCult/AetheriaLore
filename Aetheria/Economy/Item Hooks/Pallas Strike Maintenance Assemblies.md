# Pallas Strike Maintenance Assemblies

These proposed items come from examining the equipment and maintenance work involved in the Pallas Species Strikes at the scale of individual rooms. They are starting points for economy and item blueprints, not finished CultCache entries. Later definitions may use `SimpleCommodity`, `CompoundCommodityData`, `GearData`, or consumable types.

Each entry lists a possible use, candidate parts, and aspects of performance a future blueprint could represent.

## Cephalopod Mobile Dry-Operation Harness

**Likely class:** `CompoundCommodityData`

A harness that lets uplifted octopoid or cephalopod maintenance workers move and work in dry, cramped, low-g, zero-g, or shell-adjacent service spaces. It travels with the worker; it is not an aquarium, habitat, containment chair, or dedicated workstation.

Cheap AU variants prioritize reach, the ability to squeeze through narrow openings, low-g mobility, and keeping the worker alive. Comfort comes after those requirements.

**Candidate parts:**

- flexible body-loop harness
- humidity collar or humidity skin interface
- oxygenation loop
- padded pressure cuff set
- flexible contact-band harness
- compact tool roll or clip-on tool rail
- utility interlock socket
- limiter or diagnostic interface

**Performance to represent:**

- how far the worker can reach during maintenance
- how long the worker can operate in dry conditions
- risk of worker injury
- precision when handling tools
- which service arteries the harness allows the worker to access
- how far the worker can move independently
- how much comfort the harness provides beyond basic survival needs

## VitaForge BioDrone Standard Limiter Interface

**Likely class:** `CompoundCommodityData`

A corporate interface for diagnosing and controlling VitaForge BioDrone Standard labor lines. Its proposed functions include enforcing compliance and suppressing memory continuity—the persistence of memories over time. A blueprint would also need to represent diagnostic access, vulnerability to attempts to bypass its controls, and legal exposure.

**Candidate parts:**

- limiter bus coupler
- behavioral scaffold firmware
- audit port seal
- compliance telemetry recorder
- maintenance diagnostic slate adapter

**Performance to represent:**

- effectiveness of compliance enforcement
- vulnerability to jailbreaking
- extent of diagnostic access
- legal exposure associated with the interface
- degree of memory continuity suppression

Keep VitaForge's ownership of the product line explicit. BioElevate may supply uplift scaffolding and adaptation interfaces in the same yard, but BioDrone Standard remains a VitaForge product line unless a later source establishes otherwise.

## Bloom Manifold Seal Assembly

**Likely class:** `CompoundCommodityData`

A large habitat service assembly for a Bloom industrial spoke manifold: a junction where air, seal buffering, condensate, coolant, and pressure monitoring meet. Its blueprint would represent how well that junction maintains pressure, handles heat, detects faults, and can be isolated in an emergency.

**Candidate parts:**

- seal membrane stack
- load-bearing anchor frame
- pressure equalization baffle
- fault-sense manifold
- condensate trap
- coolant exchange cuff
- sensor nest
- service-ring access panel

**Performance to represent:**

- ability to maintain pressure
- ability to keep functioning when faults occur
- amount of maintenance required
- heat transfer between connected systems
- sensor reliability
- speed of emergency isolation

## Strike-Safe Life-Support Bypass Kit

**Likely class:** `GearData` or `ConsumableData`

A portable or semi-portable maintenance kit for keeping life support working temporarily during a labor stoppage. It provides a margin for air, pressure, condensate handling, or cooling while allowing workers to retain some of the technical leverage behind their strike.

The kit is politically volatile because it helps keep civilians alive while preserving workers' ability to refuse a dangerous order. Using it does not require surrendering all technical leverage to management.

**Candidate parts:**

- portable scrubber bridge
- manual valve logic plate
- pressure-safe flexible tube set
- tamper-evident custody recorder
- emergency seal tape
- dry-operation work-support adapter

**Performance to represent:**

- amount of temporary life-support margin provided
- ability to preserve evidence
- degree of crew coordination required
- resistance to sabotage
- reliability of the record of who held or controlled the kit
- severity of harm if the kit fails
