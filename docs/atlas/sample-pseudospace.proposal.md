# Atlas entry: Pseudospace (PROPOSALS)

**Not canon.** Elaborations and illustration briefs for the Pseudospace entry. None of this is in the vault. Source page: `Aetheria/Worldbuilding/Post-Elysium/Concepts/Pseudospace.md`, commit `14d827c` (last changed 7fd9a41, Meta Vens, 2026-09-05; created 3655aeb). Author of proposals: Weksa.

## Elaborations (each extends one canon sentence)

1. **A traveller's first sign of leaving flat space.** Extends: "Factions that explore only flat space can share a relatively consistent picture of what they will find. Beyond it, that confidence fails." Proposal: scouts agree on routes and distances inside flat space, then find their own measurements stop agreeing with each other past the edge. Changes if accepted: gives Flatlander scouting a concrete practice; adds no new physics.
2. **Camps on the islands.** Extends: "The Flatlanders build their camps on its few islands of stability." Proposal: a camp's perimeter is marked by whatever its crew checks each shift to confirm the ground is still flat (a plumb line, a string, a bubble level). **Guess:** the device and the habit are mine.
3. **Chaos factions at the edge.** Extends: "The Chaos factions inhabit the edges of stable Euclidean space." Proposal: nothing beyond that sentence; this entry asks the owner what daily life on the edge looks like (see questions).

## Illustration briefs (prompt-only, not rendered)

Style: painterly, restrained colour, dim light, no text.

- **Plate: the flat island.** A small circular settlement of camp shelters on a patch of ordinary level ground, surrounded on every side by a sky and horizon that bends and folds in ways that do not fit together: stairs of land turning over, a horizon that curves upward on one side and away on the other. A taut string stretched between two posts at the camp's edge runs straight and true. Source: "Flatlanders build their camps on its few islands of stability, surrounded by an infinite ocean of spatial and temporal chaos."
- **Spot: the checking line.** A hand-sized plumb line hanging steady beside a camp door at dusk, with a faint warping of the background behind it. Source: elaboration 2 (guess).
- **Vignette: the edge.** A ruled border of straight lines that slowly loosen into spirals and knots toward one corner. Source: "Beyond it, that confidence fails."
